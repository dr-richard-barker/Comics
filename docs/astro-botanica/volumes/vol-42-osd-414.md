## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 42

- **Title:** *The Veteran Chamber's Red Harvest*

- **OSDR / GLDS Accession:** `OSD-414` (Version 3) / `GLDS-414` | **DOI:** `10.26030/b487-6c39`

- **Chronology Note:** Follows Volume 41 (`OSD-413`, `VEG-03E`) directly (`skipped_accessions: []`), completing the `VEG-03D/E/F` multi-species `Veggie` trilogy. Grown concurrently (`3` days after `VEG-03E`) inside veteran **`Veggie SN 002`**, `OSD-414` (`VEG-03F`) profiles 'Outredgeous' red Romaine lettuce and its surrounding chamber microbiome.

- **Primary Publication:** Hummerick, M. E., Khodadad, C. L. M., Dixit, A. R., Spencer, L. E., Maldonado-Vasquez, G. J., Gooden, J. L., Spern, C. J., Fischer, J. A., Dufour, N., Wheeler, R. M., Romeyn, M. W., Smith, T. M., Massa, G. D., & Zhang, Y. (2021). *Spatial Characterization of Microbial Communities on Multi-Species Leafy Greens Grown Simultaneously in the Vegetable Production Systems on the International Space Station*. *Life*, 11(10), 1060. DOI: `10.3390/life11101060` | PMID: `34685431` | `PMC8537831`

- **Facility / Mission & Conditions:** ISS Columbus module (`Veggie SN 002`, launched `SpaceX CRS-3`), supplied via `SpaceX-12`; `VEG-03F` initiated `2/9/18` (`3` days after `VEG-03E`), `4` cut-and-come-again harvests (`3/11/18`, `3/17/18`, `3/30/18`, `4/9/18` at `59` days), returned `8/3/18`. Grouped multi-crop layout; `160 mL` water initiation, dry root mat; `16 h / 8 h` LEDs (`200–300 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹` PAR), `23°C` nominal; no `HOBO` logger was installed in `VEG-03F`.

- **Organism / Genotypes:** Simultaneous mixed crop of *Lactuca sativa* cv. 'Outredgeous' (red Romaine lettuce), *Brassica rapa* var. *japonica* (mizuna mustard), and *Lactuca sativa* cv. "Waldmann's Green" (green leaf lettuce); `OSD-414` archives `n = 10` `16S` samples from 'Outredgeous' red Romaine leaves, roots, and pillow components (`OSD-414`), paired with culture and swab assays across the chamber (`PMC8537831`).

- **Assay / Platform:** Paired-end `16S rRNA` V4 and fungal `ITS` amplicon sequencing on `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`; `RDP`/`GreenGenes`/`SILVA`/`UNITE`, `QIIME 2.0`), `TSA`/`IMA` plate counts, `Biolog`/`MicroSEQ` isolate ID, and food-safety screening (`3M Petrifilm` for *E. coli*/*S. aureus*; `BPW`/`RV` enrichment for *Salmonella*) (`OSD-414`; `PMC8537831`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **'Outredgeous' Red Romaine (`OL`) in `SN 002`:** The centerpiece of `OSD-414`. Its leaves carried `6.1 × 10⁶ CFU/g` bacteria (matching `VEG-03D` in `SN 002`, far above `VEG-03E` in `SN 001`), yet its roots yielded the **lowest** root bacterial counts of the entire trilogy (`9.3 × 10⁶` and `2.6 × 10⁷ CFU/g`) (`PMC8537831`).

- **Veteran `Veggie SN 002`:** On its second tour in this trilogy after `VEG-03D`. Its pillow surfaces recorded the **highest** bacterial (`4.0 × 10⁶ CFU/swab`) and fungal (`4.0 × 10⁶ CFU/swab`) surface loads of all three tech demos, with `7/8` sites positive for fungi (`PMC8537831`).

- **The `SN 002` Chamber Residents (*Enterobacter*, *Chitinophaga*, *Novosphingobium*, *Bradyrhizobium*):** Detected in both `VEG-03D` and `VEG-03F` (`SN 002`) yet absent from `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`), marking the persistent microbial signature of the older chamber (`PMC8537831`).

- **The `VEG-03F` Cultured Specialists (*Acidovorax*, *Brevundimonas*, *Cupriavidis*, *Fictibacillus*, *Nocardioidaceae*, *Paracoccus*):** Bacterial genera isolated on `TSA` uniquely from `VEG-03F` wicks, substrates, and swabs (`Table 3`) (`PMC8537831`).

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Three Days Behind `SN 001` (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 — Splash Page**

- **Panel 1 (Full-Page Splash):** February 9, 2018. Right beside `Veggie SN 001`, veteran **`Veggie SN 002`** powers up its magenta LEDs. Crimson-edged 'Outredgeous' red Romaine leaves unfurl in the grouped `VEG-03F` layout (`Figure 1B`, `Figure 2`). Overlaid in crisp ink: *"VOLUME 42: THE VETERAN CHAMBER'S RED HARVEST — OSD-414 (VEG-03F)."*

- **Caption:** Same crew. Same ISS air and water. Three days offset—and four years of chamber history.

**Page 2 — Grouped for Access (`2/9/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** Like `VEG-03E`, `VEG-03F` separates fast-growing mizuna from red Romaine and "Waldmann's Green" (`160 mL` water initiation, dry root mat).

- **Panel 2 (Note):** Unlike `D` and `E`, no `HOBO` environmental logger rides inside `VEG-03F`.

**Page 3 — Green Leaf's Quiet Struggle**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** Just as in `VEG-03E`, "Waldmann's Green" (`WG`) averages `62.5%` germination across `E/F`, leaving `2` of `4` `WG` pillows healthy while red Romaine (`OL`) and mizuna thrive.

**Page 4 — Overlapping Harvests (`3/11/18`–`4/9/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Timeline):** `3/11/18` (Harvest `1`) → `3/17/18` (Harvest `2`, frozen in `MELFI`) → `3/30/18` (Harvest `3`, sanitized with `Pro-San` wipes and eaten) → `4/9/18` (Final Harvest `4` at `59` days).

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** Later in the `59`-day run, red Romaine leaves show mild tip burn, with zero signs of plant disease.

**Page 5 — Eight Swabs in `SN 002`**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** Before wiping down `SN 002`, astronauts swab all `8` chamber sites—`3` pillow tops, `3` bellows zones, the internal fan screen, and the fan outlet—freezing them at `-80°C` in `MELFI`.

**Page 6 — Return to KSC (`8/3/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** `VEG-03F` leaves, swabs, and `2` intact red Romaine (`OL`) pillows arrive back on Earth (`8/3/18`), joining `VEG-03E` for parallel processing (`10/4/18`–`11/27/18`).

**Page 7 — Double-Page Spread Anchor: `4.0 × 10⁶` on the Veteran Pillows**

- **Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread):** `Table 2` comes to life across the interior of `Veggie SN 002`. On the pillow top surfaces, bacterial counts surge to **`1.3 × 10⁵`** and **`4.0 × 10⁶ CFU/swab`**, while fungal loads on Pillow 3 spike to **`4.0 × 10⁶ CFU/swab`**—the highest surface counts in the entire `VEG-03D/E/F` trilogy! Fungi are recovered from **`7/8`** sites (`2.5 × 10²`, `7.0 × 10¹`, `1.1 × 10²` on bellows; `5.1 × 10³` on fan screen) and bacteria from **`5/8`** sites (`7.0 × 10²` & `5.2 × 10³` on bellows; `8.6 × 10³` on fan screen; `MF` bellows & outlet `<DL`).

- **Caption:** *"New `SN 001` stayed quiet on its bellows. Veteran `SN 002` carried a denser film across its pillow tops."*

**Page 9 — Pathogen Screen: All Clear**

- **Panel 1 (Three-Plate Check):** Despite `10⁶` pillow-top counts, `3M Petrifilm` (*E. coli*/coliform, *S. aureus*) and *Salmonella* enrichments on `VEG-03F` leaves all read **negative**.

**Page 10 — The `SN 002` Leaf Paradox**

- **Panel 1 (Bar Chart):** Red Romaine (`OL`) leaf `APC` in `VEG-03F` averages **`6.1 × 10⁶ CFU/g`**—virtually identical to `VEG-03D` (`6.9 × 10⁶ CFU/g`, also in `SN 002`) and three orders of magnitude higher than `VEG-03E` (`5.7 × 10³ CFU/g` in `SN 001`, `p ≤ 0.05`). Meanwhile, `WG` averages `5.9 × 10⁵ CFU/g`.

### Act II: Inside the Red Romaine Pillow (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 — Quiet Roots Beneath Busy Leaves**

- **Panel 1 (Cutaway):** Paradoxically, `VEG-03F` red Romaine roots yield the **lowest** root bacterial counts in the entire trilogy: **`9.3 × 10⁶`** and **`2.6 × 10⁷ CFU/g`** (compared to `3.4 × 10⁷`–`7.1 × 10⁷` in `VEG-03D` `OL` roots and `10⁸` in mizuna).

- **Dialogue (Microbiologist):** *"Busy chamber surfaces and `10⁶` leaves—yet the cleanest roots of the series."*

**Page 12 — `77%` Sample Type (`Figure 8E`)**

- **Panel 1 (`PCoA` Plot):** `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`) profiles the `n = 10` `OSD-414` red Romaine samples. `PCoA` Axis 1 captures **`~50%`** of variance, and `sample type` explains **`77%`** (`p < 0.05`). As in `D` and `E`, the **wick** wanders farthest from leaf, root, and substrate.

**Page 13 — Thirty Shared Belowground (`Figure 9C`)**

- **Panel 1 (Venn Diagram):** `VEG-03F` red Romaine pillows share **`30` bacterial genera** across wick, arcillite substrate, and root—mirroring `VEG-03E` mizuna (`31`) and `VEG-03D` (`22–31`).

**Page 14 — Steadiness in the Red Leaf (`Figure 12`)**

- **Panel 1 (Stacked Bar Chart):** Compared to `VEG-03D` and `E`, `VEG-03F` shows **less variation** in bacterial genera across its wick and leaf samples—dominated by steady `Burkholderia`, `Ralstonia`, and `Pseudomonas`.

**Page 15 — The `SN 002` Signature Taxa**

- **Panel 1 (Comparison):** `Enterobacter`, `Chitinophaga`, `Novosphingobium`, and `Bradyrhizobium`—present in `VEG-03D` (`SN 002`) and absent in `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`)—reappear in `VEG-03F` (`SN 002`). Meanwhile, `Nevskia` and `Spirosoma` (seen in `VEG-03E`) are absent in `VEG-03F`.

**Page 16 — Six Unique `VEG-03F` Isolates (`Table 3`)**

- **Panel 1 (Petri Montage):** `TSA` cultivation recovers six genera unique to `VEG-03F`: *Acidovorax* (wick), *Brevundimonas* (wick, substrate), *Cupriavidis* (wick), *Fictibacillus* (swab), *Nocardioidaceae* (swab), and *Paracoccus* (wick)—plus *Pseudomonas*, *Curtobacterium*, *Microbacterium*, and *Paenibacillus* on `OL` leaves.

**Page 17 — *Purpureocillium* & the Fungal Core (`Table 4`)**

- **Panel 1 (Microscopic):** Alongside ubiquitous *Aspergillus* spp., *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium* spp., and *Rhodotorula* spp., *Purpureocillium lilacinum* is cultured from `VEG-03F` (and `VEG-03E`) chamber swabs.

**Page 18 — Why `Tech Demo` Beat `Plant Type`**

- **Panel 1 (Global `PCoA` Recap, `Figure 7`):** Across all leaves in `D`, `E`, and `F`, `Adonis` confirms `tech demo` (`R² = 0.397, p = 0.001`) outweighs `plant species` (`R² = 0.101`, NS). Chamber history, humidity, crew handling, and layout shape the leaf microbiome more than cultivar identity.

**Page 19 — The Built-Environment Loop**

- **Panel 1 (Diagram):** `250` genera shared between `Veggie` surfaces and ISS surfaces; `47` genera shared across ISS surfaces, `Veggie` surfaces, and leaves. `VEG-03F` confirms that `SN 002` retains a persistent chamber ecology across missions unless scrubbed clean.

**Page 20 — Pro-San & Harvest Hygiene**

- **Panel 1 (Galley Scene):** Crew members wipe `OL` leaves with `Pro-San` before eating Harvests `1` and `3`—a simple, effective barrier even when pillow-top swabs hit `10⁶ CFU/swab`.

**Page 21 — The Trilogy Synthesized**

- **Panel 1 (Three-Unit Mural):** `VEG-03D` (`OSD-412`), `VEG-03E` (`OSD-413`), and `VEG-03F` (`OSD-414`) stand side by side: three crops, two `Veggie` units, `12` harvests, and one unified spatial rule—roots and substrate hold the highest diversity, wicks are the wildest bridge, and leaves stay lowest and safe to eat.

**Page 22 — Archiving `OSD-414`**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** All `10` `VEG-03F` `16S` datasets (`GLDS-414`, `10.26030/b487-6c39`) enter NASA OSDR, completing the `VEG-03D/E/F` data triad.

### Act III: Beyond the Leafy Greens (Pages 23–30)

**Page 23 — What `VEG-03D/E/F` Taught NASA**

- **Panel 1 (Checklist):**

  - Simultaneous multi-species cropping and staggered dual-unit harvests work on ISS.

  - Crops remain free of *E. coli*, *S. aureus*, and *Salmonella*.

  - Chamber age (`SN 001` vs. `SN 002`) and humidity (`25`-day fan anomaly) leave lasting microbial fingerprints.

**Page 24 — The Next Frontier: Epigenetics**

- **Panel 1 (Transition):** Having mapped *who* lives on orbital crops, the chronicle pivots back to *how* plant genomes rewrite their own regulation in microgravity.

**Page 25 — Skipping the Animal & Cell Interlude**

**Page 26 — `APEX-04` (`SpaceX-10`) Returns**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** `10-cm²` `0.5%` Phytagel / `0.5×` MS plates wrapped in black Duvetyne cloth chill at `4°C` before launch—carrying *Arabidopsis thaliana* methylation mutants **`elp2-5`** and **`met1-7`**.

**Page 27 — Methylation in Orbit (`OSD-427`)**

- **Panel 1 (Molecular Close-up):** Cytosine methylation (`CG`, `CHG`, `CHH`) marks along plant DNA glow as `11`-day-old seedlings grow inside `Veggie` on ISS before fixation in `KFTs` with `RNAlater`.

**Page 28 — Silencing & Stress**

### Right Spread (Page 32): `OSD-414` `16S` Red Romaine Profile (`n = 10`) & Trilogy Synthesis

- **OSDR Sequencing (`OSD-414`):** `n = 10` `16S` V4 samples from `'Outredgeous'` red Romaine leaves, roots, and pillow components; `Illumina MiSeq` `250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`.

- **Community Structure (`Figure 8E`, `Figure 9C`, `Figure 12`):**

  - `PCoA1` explained `~50%` of variance; `sample type` drove **`77%`** (`p < 0.05`), with wick showing greatest variance.

  - **`30`** genera shared across wick + substrate + root (`Figure 9C`).

  - Less genus variation in wick and leaf than in `D`/`E`; *Burkholderia*, *Ralstonia*, and *Pseudomonas* present across samples.

- **`SN 002` Signature & Unique Isolates:**

  - *Enterobacter*, *Chitinophaga*, *Novosphingobium*, and *Bradyrhizobium* shared between `VEG-03F` and `VEG-03D` (`SN 002`), absent in `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`); *Nevskia*/*Spirosoma* absent in `F`.

  - Unique `VEG-03F` cultured bacteria (`Table 3`): *Acidovorax*, *Brevundimonas*, *Cupriavidis*, *Fictibacillus*, *Nocardioidaceae*, *Paracoccus*.

  - Fungi: *Aspergillus* spp., *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium* spp., *Rhodotorula* spp., plus *Purpureocillium lilacinum* on `E`/`F` swabs.
