## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Volume:** Vol. 14 — *The Three Canisters and the Zero-Waste Wall*

- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-121` (`GLDS-121` | `LSDS-56` | Experiment `OS-636`)

- **Version & Dates:** Version 10 | Submitted: `30-May-2017` | Initial Release: `08-Jun-2017` | Updated: `31-Jul-2024`

- **Study DOI:** `10.26030/23fe-sa48`

- **Payload & Mission:** `BRIC-16` (`BRIC-16-Cyt`) aboard Space Shuttle *Discovery* (`STS-131`, `04/05/2010`–`04/20/2010`, middeck locker)

- **Primary Publications:**

  - Johnson et al. (2017), *Am J Bot* 104(8):1219–1231 (PMID: `28827451` | DOI: `10.3732/ajb.1700079`) — *Comparative transcriptomics indicate changes in cell wall organization and stress response in seedlings during spaceflight*

  - Johnson et al. (2015), *J Plant Res* 128(6):1007–1016 (PMID: `26376793` | DOI: `10.1007/s10265-015-0749-0`) — *Morphometric analyses of petioles of seedlings grown in a spaceflight experiment*

- **Organism & Genotype:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* Landsberg *erecta* (`Ler-0`, J. Z. Kiss lab line; 40 seeds/plate in 1 central row, wet-sown on `1.2%` w/v agar, `1 mM` MES pH `5.5`, `1%` w/v sucrose, `1/2×` MS salts; 1 d dark stratification at `24°C`)

- **Hardware & Conditions:** `BRIC-PDFU` (Canisters `E`, `F`, and `H` PDFUs 3–5; `13 mL` `RNAlater` reservoir/PDFU) vs. Orbital Environmental Simulator (`OES`) at KSC SLSL on 24-h delay (microarray `GC` replicates `H-F2/F3/F4` sourced from Pre-flight Verification Test [PVT]); `309 h` (`~12.88 d`) continuous darkness at `24°C`; TLD middeck dosimetry (`DLOC1–3`): `3.22 mGy` mean (`0.25 mGy/day`; range `2.62–4.42 mGy`)

- **Preservation & Assays (`n = 6` shoots):** In-orbit `RNAlater` fixation at `309 h` + `53 h` post-landing soak; Canon PowerShot SX20 IS morphometry; root/shoot bisection (**shoots only**); `Ovation Pico WTA` + `Affymetrix ATH1 121501` microarray (`RMA`, `MAS5`, `PLIER`, GeneLab `NF_MAAffymetrix 1.0.5`); **Zero-Waste Glycomics** (`4 M KOH` extract from shredder-column residue screened by ELISA against **155** CCRC/JIM/MAC/BG1/LAMP mAbs); `qRT-PCR` (`UBC10` reference)

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Ler-0 Shoot ("Lans"):** Etiolated *Arabidopsis* `Ler-0` hypocotyl and cotyledon team from Canister `F` (`PDFU 2–4`). Unlike its `Col-0` neighbors in `BRIC-16-Reg` and `BRIC-16-DNA`, Lans is dissected away from its roots before RNA extraction—speaking purely for the shoot.

- **AGP31 (`AT1G28290`):** An arabinogalactan scaffolding protein surging `+22.97`-fold (`RMA`) to `+23.44`-fold (`MAS5`) in flight shoots, frantically reinforcing a rounding, loosening cell wall.

- **The Xyloglucan Cutters (`XTH32`, `XTH9`, `GH9C2`):** Wall-remodeling enzymes (`+3.19` to `+12.92`-fold across normalizations) cleaving hemicellulose cross-links while **Xylan-3–7** and **HG/RG-I Pectic Backbones** thin out in the 155-antibody ELISA screen.

- **The Four Formins (`FH2` family: `AT3G05470`, `AT2G43800`, `AT3G07540`, `AT3G32400`) & `NET1A`:** Cytoskeletal actin-nucleators (`+5.45` to `+18.66`-fold) trying to steer cell elongation without a gravity vector.

- **XERO1 (`AT3G50980`):** The water-stress dehydrin—downregulated a staggering **`-268.27`-fold** in `BRIC-16-Cyt` flight shoots (and `-6.07`-fold in `BRIC-16-DNA`), proving that in dark `BRIC` canisters, microgravity shoots feel *less* drought stress than Earth-bound controls.

- **HSFA2 (`AT2G26150`) & The Adventitious Roots:** The hypoxia/heat-shock factor downregulated in flight shoots while hypocotyl-sprouting adventitious roots act like emergency snorkels.

- **The Shredder-Column Ghost (CCRC 155-mAb Panel):** The microscopic cell-wall residue left behind after RNA extraction—rescued from the trash by Michael Hahn and Sivakumar Pattathil's zero-waste `4 M KOH` ELISA.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Launch & Environmental Shift (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 — Splash: Three Teams, Eight Canisters**

- *Panel 1 (Full-page splash):* Inside the half-sized middeck locker of Space Shuttle *Discovery* (`STS-131`, April 2010), eight anodized aluminum `BRIC` canisters sit locked in the dark. A translucent cutaway reveals `BRIC A, B, G` (`BRIC-16-DNA`, grid-sown `Col-0`), `BRIC C, D` (`BRIC-16-Reg`, multi-row `Col-0`), and `BRIC E, F, H` (`BRIC-16-Cyt`, single central row of `40` wet-sown `Ler-0` seeds).

- *Caption:* "Same shuttle. Same dark aluminum. Three teams—and zero agreement on what the genes will say."

**Page 2 — The Stereomicroscope Audit**

- *Panel 1:* Flashback to Kennedy Space Center's SLSL laminar flow hood (`36 h` before launch). Tweezers drop `Ler-0` seeds through `TX-100` in `70%` ethanol, `95%` ethanol rinses, and sterile water.

- *Panel 2:* Under a stereomicroscope, a cracked seed coat is flicked aside. Forty pristine seeds line up in a single central file on `1.2%` agar (`pH 5.5`, `1%` sucrose).

- *Panel 3:* Parafilm seals the `60 mm` dish; `13 mL` of `RNAlater` fills each `PDFU` fluid chamber above it.

- *Lans (narration):* "One day at `24°C` in the dark, then straight into Discovery's middeck."

**Page 3 — Liftoff & Orbital Drift**

- *Panel 1:* Sound-effect rumble (`VROOOOM`) shakes the `PDFU` rack as `Discovery` clears the pad; in the KSC Orbital Environmental Simulator (`OES`), the ground control locker is tilted manually by human hands `24 h` behind schedule.

- *Panel 2:* Microgravity arrives. Inside `BRIC F`, `89%` of flight seeds crack open (`90%` on Earth). Without `1 g` to pull downward, roots and shoots begin to skew unidirectionally across the agar.

**Page 4 — Dark Hour 150: The Snorkels Sprout**

- *Panel 1:* Continuous darkness (`309 h` total). No red photons, no blue photons—just `24°C` air and stalled convection.

- *Panel 2:* Along Lans's etiolated hypocotyl, unexpected buds push outward: **adventitious roots** flaring into the humid headspace.

- *Lans:* "No gravity to drain the moisture, no convection to stir the air... so we grow our own air-pipes."

**Page 5 — The Dosimeter Whisper**

- *Panel 1:* Outside the `BRIC` shells, passive TLD badges `DLOC1`, `DLOC2`, and `DLOC3` quietly tally cosmic hits (`0.25 mGy/day`, `3.22 mGy` total over `12.88` days).

- *Panel 2:* Inside the `Ler-0` shoot, `DRT100` (DNA repair) barely flinches compared to the ground control's baseline—this isn't an ionizing-radiation crisis. It's mechanical.

**Page 6 — The Endodermis Rounds Out**

- *Panel 1:* Cross-section of a `Ler-0` petiole endodermal cell in `1 g` (tall, brick-like, disciplined) vs. microgravity (smaller, swollen, rounded like a water balloon).

- *Panel 2:* The large shoot vacuole presses outward against a wall that no longer carries `1 g` weight.

- *AGP31:* "Turgor's spiking! The bricks are turning into bubbles!"

**Page 7 — Hour 309: The Actuator Gun**

- *Panel 1:* An astronaut's gloved hand grips the `BRIC` actuator gun in orbit. *CLICK-HISS.*

- *Panel 2:* `13 mL` of `RNAlater` floods each `PDFU` chamber, freezing `309 h` of dark growth mid-sentence.

- *Caption:* "No `-80°C` freezer on *STS-131*. Every transcript must ride home to Earth in `RNAlater` at ambient cabin temperature."

**Page 8 — Fifty-Three Hours in Limbo**

- *Panel 1:* *Discovery* touches down. For `53 h` post-landing, the seedlings steep in `RNAlater` before deintegration.

- *Panel 2:* Jin Nakashima's `Canon PowerShot SX20 IS` flashes over the opened plates (`F_F2-2`, `G_F2-2`), capturing the skewed roots and adventitious sprouts before fresh `RNAlater` vials head to Gainesville (`-80°C`).

**Page 9 — The Bioanalyzer Warning**

- *Panel 1:* At UF's ICBR, the `RNA 6000 Pico LabChip` trace flickers—degraded peaks from ambient `RNAlater` return, and the official `OES` ground control RNA fails quality check.

- *Panel 2:* Researchers pivot to the Pre-flight Verification Test (`PVT`) ground plates (`H-F2`, `H-F3`, `H-F4`) to match flight plates (`F-F2`, `F-F3`, `F-F4`). *n = 3 vs. 3.*

**Page 10 — The Bisection**

- *Panel 1:* On a chilled stage, forceps and scalpel sever hypocotyl from root.

- *Panel 2:* While `BRIC-16-Reg` and `BRIC-16-DNA` grind whole seedlings, `BRIC-16-Cyt` sends **shoots only** into the `RNeasy` shredder column—leaving a microscopic speck of crushed cell wall trapped in the plastic filter.

- *Caption:* "Act I ends at the blade. Inside the shoot—and inside the discarded wall—two separate wars are about to be read."

### Act II: The Molecular Battle (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 — The Scaffolding Surge (`AGP31`)**

- *Panel 1:* Inside the `Ler-0` shoot plasma membrane, `AGP31` (`AT1G28290`) explodes onto the scene (`+22.97`-fold `RMA`, `+23.44` `MAS5`, `+22.72` `PLIER`), weaving glycoprotein cables across the apoplast.

- *AGP31:* "If gravity won't tell us which way is up, I'll lace this wall together myself!"

**Page 12 — The Xyloglucan Cutters**

- *Panel 1:* `XTH32` (`+7.98` `MAS5`), `XTH9` (`+12.92` `PLIER`), `GH9C2` (`+5.33` `RMA`), and `EXPA4` (`+7.78`) swarm the cellulose microfibrils, snipping and loosening xyloglucan tethers.

- *Panel 2:* `FLA8` (`AGP8`) and `EXPA10` (later confirmed by `qRT-PCR`) cluster right beside them in the heatmap.

**Page 13 — Formins on Patrol**

- *Panel 1:* Beneath the membrane, four `FH2` formin brothers (`AT3G05470`, `AT2G43800`, `AT3G07540` at `+18.66`, `AT3G32400`) plus `WLIM2A`, `NET1A` (`+14.38`), and `SPC97` (`+6.59`) grab actin filaments and microtubule anchors.

- *FH2 (`AT3G07540`):* "Cellulose tracks are drifting! Lock the cytoskeleton!"

**Page 14 — The Silence of `XERO1`**

- *Panel 1:* Over in the stress-response bunker, `XERO1` (`AT3G50980`), `LTI78`, `DREB2A`, and `MYB2` sit with their alarms unplugged. `XERO1` plunges **`-268.27`-fold**!

- *Panel 2:* On Earth (`1 g` control), water drains downward through the agar, stressing the shoots (`12%` stress GO terms vs. `5%` in `Reg`/`DNA` controls). In microgravity, capillary film hugs the shoot effortlessly.

- *XERO1:* "Drought? What drought? We're floating in a wet cloud."

**Page 15 — The Hypoxia Paradox (`HSFA2`)**

- *Panel 1:* `HSFA2` (`AT2G26150`) and chaperones `HSP101` and `HSP90.1` power down (`-5`-fold or more), even as `HSP40` (`AT2G20550`) flares up.

- *Panel 2:* An adventitious root outside the shoot pumps dissolved oxygen straight up the hypocotyl, sparing the shoot from the suffocation `BRIC-16-Reg` roots endure.

**Page 16 — Three Algorithms, One Truth?**

- *Panel 1:* Three data-processing avatars—**RMA** (`71` up / `55` down), **MAS5** (`187` up / `121` down), and **PLIER** (`384` up / `66` down)—argue over the `ATH1` GeneChip (`±5`-fold, `P < 0.01`).

- *Panel 2:* Only **43** transcripts (`37` up, `6` down) survive all three algorithms unscathed—with `AGP31`, `GH9C2`, and `XTH32` standing at the center.

**Page 17 — The Venn Diagram of Zero**

- *Panel 1:* The three `BRIC-16` teams lay their `±5`-fold Venn diagrams on the table.

- *Panel 2:* At the dead-center intersection of `BRIC-16-Cyt`, `BRIC-16-Reg`, and `BRIC-16-DNA`: a stark, empty **`0`**.

- *Caption:* "Zero shared transcripts across all three. Only `XERO1` bridges `Cyt` and `DNA`."

**Page 18 — The Mirror-Image Six**

- *Panel 1:* Six genes bridge `BRIC-16-Cyt` and `BRIC-16-Reg` (`GDSL`, `AT1G75280`, `AT2G22510`, `AT2G38390`, `ATEXPA4`, `AT4G24780`)—but their arrows point in **opposite** directions!

- *Panel 2:* Up `+5.41` to `+12.98`-fold in `Cyt` shoots; down `-5.22` to `-8.04`-fold in `Reg` whole seedlings.

- *Lans:* "You pooled the roots with the shoots! We're two different organs!"

**Page 19 — Rescue from the Shredder Column**

- *Panel 1:* Back in the lab, a gloved hand pulls the spent `RNeasy` shredder column from the biohazard bin.

- *Panel 2:* "Zero waste." At the University of Georgia's CCRC, `4 M KOH` with `1%` sodium borohydride strips noncellulosic glycans from the trace shoot-wall residue.

**Page 20 — The 155-Antibody Gauntlet**

- *Panel 1:* A `96`-well ELISA grid lights up in a yellow-to-black heatmap as **155** monoclonal antibodies (`CCRC`, `JIM`, `MAC`, `BG1`, `LAMP`) probe `0.3 µg` glucose-equivalent wall extracts.

- *Panel 2:* Nonfucosylated and fucosylated xyloglucans blaze bright yellow in both flight and ground—confirming the core scaffold survived.

**Page 21 — The Missing Xylans & Pectins**

- *Panel 1:* Closer inspection of the heatmap: `xylan-3` through `xylan-7` bands dim in spaceflight shoots.

- *Panel 2:* Beside them, `HG backbone-1`, `HG backbone-2`, `RG-I backbone`, and `RG-Ic` pectic epitopes fade marginally too. The `4 M KOH` screen proves the transcriptome's prophecy: microgravity thinned the pectin–xylan network.

**Page 22 — The qRT-PCR Cross-Exam**

- *Panel 1:* At Miami University, `UBC10` (`AT5G53300`) holds the baseline steady.

- *Panel 2:* `MYB2` confirms down; `EXPA10` confirms up; `CCX2` and `XYL1` stay flat under small-`n` noise—closing the molecular case.

**Page 30 — Closing Splash: What Remains in the Filter**

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

**Page 31 (Left Spread): `BRIC-16` Three-Team Matrix & Microarray Convergence**

- **Header Banner:** `STS-131` (`BRIC-PDFU`, `309 h` Dark, `24°C`, `RNAlater` in-orbit + `53 h` post-landing, `3.22 mGy` mean TLD dose)

- **3-Column Comparison Table (`Johnson et al., 2017`, Table 4):**

  - `BRIC-16-Cyt` (Kiss): Canisters `E, F, H` | `Ler-0` | **Shoots only** | `40` seeds/1 row | `1.2%` agar + MS + `1%` sucrose | `RMA`

  - `BRIC-16-Reg` (Blancaflor): Canisters `C, D, H` | `Col-0` | Whole seedling | `150–200` seeds/rows | `1.2%` agar + MS + `1%` sucrose | `RMA`

  - `BRIC-16-DNA` (Paul/Ferl): Canisters `A, B, G` | `Col-0` | Whole seedling | `75` seeds/grid | `0.5%` Phytagel + MS | `MAS5`

- **DEG Normalization Funnel (`±5`-fold, `P < 0.01`):**

  - `PLIER`: `384` Up / `66` Down (`450` total)

  - `MAS5`: `187` Up / `121` Down (`308` total)

  - `RMA`: `71` Up / `55` Down (`126` total)

  - **Consensus Core:** `43` transcripts (`37` Up, `6` Down) across all 3 methods

- **3-Team Venn Callout:** `0` transcripts shared by all three; `1` (`XERO1`, `-268.27` `Cyt` / `-6.07` `DNA`) shared by `Cyt` & `DNA`; `6` (`GDSL`, `AT1G75280`, `AT2G22510`, `AT2G38390`, `ATEXPA4`, `AT4G24780`) shared—and **oppositely regulated**—between `Cyt` shoots (`+5.41` to `+12.98`) and `Reg` whole seedlings (`-5.22` to `-8.04`).

**Page 32 (Right Spread): Zero-Waste Glycomics & Cell-Wall Architecture**

- **Zero-Waste Pipeline Box:** `RNeasy` shredder-column shoot residue → `4 M KOH` + `1%` NaBH₄ extraction → `0.3 µg` glucose-eq/well ELISA → **155** mAbs (`CCRC`, `JIM`, `MAC`, `BG1`, `LAMP`).

- **Glycome Shift Callout:**

  - *Dominant:* Nonfucosylated & fucosylated xyloglucans (high in both `FLT` & `GC`).

  - *Reduced in Spaceflight:* **Xylan** (`xylan-3` to `-7` mAbs) and **Pectic Backbones** (`HG backbone-1/2`, `RG-I backbone`, `RG-Ic`).

- **Key Molecular Movers (`BRIC-16-Cyt`):**

  - *Wall Remodeling (Up):* `AGP31` (`+22.97` `RMA`), `GH9C2` (`+5.33`), `XTH32` (`+3.19` `RMA` / `+7.98` `MAS5`), `XTH9` (`+12.92` `PLIER`), `EXPA10` (`qRT-PCR` confirmed).

  - *Cytoskeleton (Up):* `FH2` family (`AT3G07540` `+18.66`, `AT3G05470` `+10.32`, `AT2G43800` `+8.74`, `AT3G32400` `+5.45`), `NET1A` (`+14.38`), `SPC97` (`+6.59`), `WLIM2A` (`+4.39`).

  - *Water/Hypoxia/Heat Stress (Down):* `XERO1` (`-268.27`), `LTI78`, `DREB2A`, `MYB2` (`qRT-PCR` confirmed), `HSFA2`, `HSP101`, `HSP90.1` (`HSP40` Up).

- **Phenotypic Anchor (`Johnson et al., 2015`; `Millar et al., 2011`):** Unidirectional root/shoot skewing, abundant adventitious roots, and smaller, rounder petiole endodermal cells.
