## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Invisible Gas (`C₂H₄`, *Ethylene*):** The simplest gaseous phytohormone in existence. Synthesized in two enzymatic steps from methionine; acts as a volatile airborne messenger that diffuses across plant tissues to trigger systemic defense.
- **The Central Signaling Conduit (`EIN2`, *ETHYLENE INSENSITIVE 2*):** The master transmembrane coordinator located in the endoplasmic reticulum. Cleaved upon ethylene perception to send its nuclear C-terminal domain to activate transcription.
- **The Master Transcription Factors (`EIN3`, `EIL1`):** Nuclear regulators binding directly to Ethylene Response Factors (*ERF1*, *EDF1*) to direct stress tolerance.
- **The Epigenetic Bridge (`MET1`, `CMT3`):** DNA methyltransferases whose chromatin-remodeling activities are directly governed by the EIN2-EIN3 signaling axis.
- **Squad Mutant Warriors:**
  - **The Blind Sentry (*ein2-1*):** Unable to sense ethylene gas; unable to remodel its epigenome under chronic radiation, suffering severe oxidative lesions and cellular senescence.
  - **The Hyper-Shielded (*ctr1-1*):** Locked in perpetual ethylene signaling; constitutively hyper-methylated, displaying extreme natural immunity to cosmic radiation!
- **Dr. Nele Horemans & Dr. May Van Hees:** Radiobiologists and systems geneticists proving that a humble volatile gas holds the key to safeguarding the planetary genome for interstellar travel.

### Act II: The Epigenetic Coupling (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- Whole-genome bisulfite sequencing (WGBS) under chronic low-dose radiation:
  - In wild-type Col-0, chronic ethylene signaling orchestrates targeted cytosine hyper-methylation at transposable elements while maintaining open hypomethylated chromatin at DNA repair promoters (*RAD51*, *BRCA1*).
  - In *ein2-1*, this coordinated epigenetic patterning collapses: transposable elements become aberrantly hypomethylated and begin transposing, causing insertional mutations across the genome!

**Page 16–20**
- Multi-omics cross-correlation:
  - Direct interaction between *EIN3* and *MET1* / *CMT3* DNA methyltransferases.
  - Ethylene acts as the bridge connecting physical stress perception with the stable epigenetic memory of the chromosome!
