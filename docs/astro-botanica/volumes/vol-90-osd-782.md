## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*
- **Volume:** 90 (The Grand Series Finale — Complete 90-Volume NASA OSDR Plant Biology Archive)
- **Chapter Title:** *The Planetary Hormone: Ethylene and Epigenetic Resilience*
- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-782` (*Multi-omics profiling reveals ethylene signalling as a key pathway underlying both genetic and epigenetic responses to low-dose ionizing radiation in Arabidopsis*; Study DOI: `10.26030/qpqe-8956`)
- **Primary Publication:** Newman JRB, Zhou M, Pires JC, Bacher RL, Kladde M, Concannon P. (2023). *Multi-omics profiling reveals ethylene signalling as a key pathway underlying both genetic and epigenetic responses to low-dose ionizing radiation in Arabidopsis*. `DOI: 10.1101/2023.10.14.562363`.
- **Setting & Hardware:** University of Florida Ground Analog Ionizing Radiation Facility — controlled acute low-dose ionizing radiation exposure chambers calibrated to deep-space transit dose regimes across multiple absorbed doses and post-irradiation recovery time points (`n = 36` verified biological samples in NASA OSDR `OSD-782`). Paired with **MAPit** (*Methyltransferase Accessibility Protocol for individual templates*) chromatin accessibility assays and Illumina stranded RNA-Seq.
- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* seedlings profiled across three verified NASA OSDR experimental factors: **Ionizing Radiation**, **Absorbed Radiation Dose**, and **Time of Sample Collection After Treatment** (`36` samples). Integrated multi-omics combining whole-genome RNA-Seq with **MAPit** single-molecule endogenous cytosine methylation (`5mC`) and nucleosome chromatin accessibility profiling.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Invisible Gas (`C₂H₄`, *Ethylene*):** The simplest gaseous phytohormone on Earth—revealed by `OSD-782` multi-omics as the master signaling hub coordinating *both* transcriptional and epigenetic chromatin responses to low-dose ionizing radiation.
- **The Master Transcription Factors (`EIN3`, `EIL1` & `ERF` Brigade):** Ethylene-responsive nuclear regulators binding directly to newly opened chromatin promoters to trigger stress defense and recovery programs.
- **The Central Signaling Conduit (`EIN2`, *ETHYLENE INSENSITIVE 2*):** Transmembrane messenger linking ethylene perception at the endoplasmic reticulum to nuclear gene regulation and chromatin state remodeling.
- **The Chromatin Accessibility Probe (`MAPit` — `M.CviPI` Methyltransferase):** Experimental molecular sentinel that footprints open, accessible DNA (`GC` sites) simultaneously alongside endogenous plant `CG` methylation (`MET1`) on the very same DNA molecule!
- **The DNA Methylation Guardian (`MET1` / Endogenous `5mC`):** Native cytosine methylation network whose promoter remodeling aligns with radiation-induced chromatin opening.
- **Dr. Jeremy Newman, Dr. Mingqi Zhou, Dr. Michael Kladde & Dr. Patrick Concannon:** Systems geneticists and epigenomic pioneers applying single-molecule MAPit + RNA-Seq to decode how plants sense and survive low-dose space-relevant ionizing radiation.
