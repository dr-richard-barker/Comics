## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 40

- **Title:** *Three Greens Under One Fan*

- **OSDR / GLDS Accession:** `OSD-412` (Version 1) / `GLDS-412` | **DOI:** `10.26030/0jpd-te04`

- **Chronology Note:** Follows Volume 39 (`OSD-406`, suborbital *Arabidopsis* `VP-03` & `NS-12`) after skipping `OSD-407` through `OSD-411` (non-plant studies, including `OSD-410` *Homo sapiens* blood radiation). `OSD-412` returns to the International Space Station for `VEG-03D`, the first simultaneous **multi-species** leafy-green grow-out inside a single `Veggie` unit (`SN 002`), paired with `OSD-413` (`VEG-03E`) and `OSD-414` (`VEG-03F`).

- **Primary Publication:** Hummerick, M. E., Khodadad, C. L. M., Dixit, A. R., Spencer, L. E., Maldonado-Vasquez, G. J., Gooden, J. L., Spern, C. J., Fischer, J. A., Dufour, N., Wheeler, R. M., Romeyn, M. W., Smith, T. M., Massa, G. D., & Zhang, Y. (2021). *Spatial Characterization of Microbial Communities on Multi-Species Leafy Greens Grown Simultaneously in the Vegetable Production Systems on the International Space Station*. *Life*, 11(10), 1060. DOI: `10.3390/life11101060` | PMID: `34685431` | `PMC8537831`

- **Facility / Mission & Conditions:** ISS Columbus module (`Veggie SN 002`, launched on `SpaceX CRS-3`), supplied via `SpaceX-12` (`08/14/2017`–`09/17/2017`); `VEG-03D` initiated `9/26/17` (`160 mL` water initiation, root mat installed dry) with `4` cut-and-come-again harvests (`10/27/17`, `11/3/17`, `11/13/17`, `11/23/17` at `59` days), returned `5/5/18`. LEDs at `200–300 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹` PAR (`16 h / 8 h` light/dark), `23°C` nominal; **fan anomaly** raised internal `Veggie` humidity to **`100%`** for `~25 days`, causing bellows condensation. No ground control (`ISSES` ground controls noted only for select leaf comparisons in OSDR metadata).

- **Organism / Genotypes:** Simultaneous mixed crop (`2` pillows each) of *Brassica rapa* var. *japonica* (mizuna mustard), *Lactuca sativa* cv. 'Outredgeous' (red Romaine lettuce), and *Lactuca sativa* cv. "Waldmann's Green" (green leaf lettuce), all from Johnny's Select Seeds (Fairfield, ME, USA), plus their associated **microbiota** across leaves, roots, wicks, arcillite media, and `8` `Veggie` chamber surfaces (`n = 43` samples on `OSD-412`).

- **Assay / Platform:** Paired-end `16S rRNA` V4 (`F: 5'-GTGCCAGCMGCCGCGGTAA-3'`, `R: 5'-GGACTACHVGGGTWTCTAAT-3'`) and fungal `ITS` amplicon sequencing on `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` paired-end, `10%` `PhiX`; `RDP`/`GreenGenes`/`SILVA`/`UNITE`; `QIIME 2.0`), paired with `TSA` (`30°C`, `48 h`) and `IMA` (`30°C`, `72–120 h`) plate counts, `Biolog` / `MicroSEQ`, and food-safety pathogen screening (`3M Petrifilm` for *E. coli*/*S. aureus*; `BPW`/`RV` enrichment for *Salmonella*) (`OSD-412`; `PMC8537831`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Mixed-Crop Trio (`VEG-03D`):** **Mizuna mustard** (`91.6%` overall emergence; fast-growing canopy that shaded neighbors and drank slightly more water), **'Outredgeous' red Romaine lettuce** (`83.3%` overall emergence; late tip burn after Day `30–31`), and **"Waldmann's Green" leaf lettuce** (`75%` germination in `VEG-03D`), sharing `~1 L` of ISS potable water per plant per `30` days (`PMC8537831`).

- **`Veggie SN 002` & the `25`-Day Fan Anomaly:** The veteran `ORBITEC` growth chamber (`16 h / 8 h` LEDs, Kevlar/Nomex pillows with `250 mL` autoclaved arcillite + `7.5 g/L` `18-6-8` `100`-day Nutricote). A fan malfunction pushed internal humidity to `100%` for `~25 days`, fogging the transparent bellows (`OSD-412`; `PMC8537831`).

- **ISS Crew Cultivators (Joseph M. Acaba, Mark T. Vande Hei, Scott D. Tingle):** Tended the mixed pillows, wiped visible pillow-thread mold and harvested leaves with **`Pro-San`** organic-acid wipes, ate Harvests `1` and `3`, and froze Harvests `2` and `4` plus `8` chamber swabs at `-80°C` in `MELFI` (`OSD-412`; `PMC8537831`).

- **The Water- & Wick-Borne Colonists (*Burkholderia*, *Ralstonia*, *Pseudomonas*):** Dominant `16S` genera in `VEG-03D` wicks, arcillite substrate, and roots—seeded from ISS potable water, endophytes, or cabin air rather than soil (`PMC8537831`).

- **The Persistent Four (*Aspergillus* spp., *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium* spp., *Rhodotorula* spp.):** Culturable fungi recovered across swabs, wicks, substrates, leaves, and roots of `VEG-03D`, `E`, and `F` (`PMC8537831`).

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: The Mixed Canopy (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 — Splash Page**

- **Panel 1 (Full-Page Splash):** Inside the Columbus module, `Veggie SN 002` glows magenta-pink. Through its condensation-beaded bellows, six Kevlar plant pillows hold three distinct silhouettes: serrated **mizuna mustard**, crimson-tinged **'Outredgeous' red Romaine**, and ruffled **"Waldmann's Green"** leaf lettuce. Overlaid in crisp ink: *"VOLUME 40: THREE GREENS UNDER ONE FAN — OSD-412 (VEG-03D)."*

- **Caption:** Six pillows. Three species. One closed loop of air, water, and microbes.

**Page 2 — Packing the Sterile Pillows**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** At Kennedy Space Center, `75–100` seeds per crop sit in an open Petri dish inside a jar with `0.5 mL` `HCl` and `30 mL` `5%` bleach for `1 h` chlorine-gas fuming, followed by overnight off-gassing and `TSA`/`IMA` sterility checks.

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** Guar gum glues `2` sanitized seeds onto each germination wick above `250 mL` of autoclaved porous arcillite mixed with `7.5 g/L` `18-6-8` type `100`-day Nutricote.

**Page 3 — Initiation (`9/26/17`)**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** An astronaut injects `160 mL` of ISS potable water into each pillow—leaving the underlying root mat **dry** this time to avoid the overwatering seen in earlier `Veggie` runs.

- **Panel 2 (Inset):** Days later, the seedlings emerge (`91.6%` mizuna, `83.3%` red Romaine, `75%` green leaf in `VEG-03D`), then are thinned to `1` plant per pillow.

**Page 4 — The Canopy Race**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** By Week `3`, mizuna mustard surges upward and outward, its broad serrated leaves overshadowing the neighboring red Romaine pillows and complicating watering access.

- **Panel 2 (Note):** A mission planner jots on a clipboard: *"For `VEG-03E` and `F`, separate mizuna from the lettuces."*

**Page 5 — The Fan Anomaly (`100%` RH)**

- **Panel 1 (Close-up):** The `Veggie SN 002` fan stalls. Inside the chamber, the `HOBO` logger watches relative humidity climb to **`100%`**—and stay there for roughly **`25 days`**.

- **Panel 2 (Wide):** Droplets of condensation bead along the transparent bellows. Near the pillow seams, faint fuzzy threads of mold appear; crew hands wipe them carefully with **`Pro-San`** organic-acid wipes.

**Page 6 — Cut-and-Come-Again**

- **Panel 1 (Four-Beat Calendar Strip):** Harvest `1` (`10/27/17`, Day `31`) → Harvest `2` (`11/3/17`, frozen in `MELFI` at `-80°C`) → Harvest `3` (`11/13/17`, sanitized with `Pro-San` and eaten by crew) → Final Harvest `4` (`11/23/17`, Day `59`, split between `MELFI` and the galley table).

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** After Day `30–31`, older mizuna leaves yellow at the dry edges and red Romaine shows mild tip burn—signs of nutrient drawdown, not disease.

**Page 7 — Double-Page Spread Anchor: Eight Swabs & Four Pillow Layers**

- **Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread):** An exploded architectural diagram of `Veggie SN 002` at harvest (`59` days). On the left, astronauts collect **`8` sterile swabs**: upper, middle, and lower interior bellows (`1.0 × 10²` to `1.5 × 10³ CFU/swab`), `3` pillow tops (`3.5 × 10²` to `6.0 × 10⁴ CFU/swab`), internal fan screen (`3.5 × 10¹ CFU/swab`), and fan outlet (`1.4 × 10² CFU/swab`). On the right, a thawed pillow opens into **four distinct microbial zones**: **Leaf** (`~10⁶–10⁷ CFU/g`), **Wick** (`1.9 × 10⁸–1.8 × 10⁹ CFU/g`), **Arcillite Substrate** (`2.6 × 10⁶–2.2 × 10⁷ CFU/g`), and **Root** (`3.4 × 10⁷–2.6 × 10⁸ CFU/g`).

- **Caption:** *"From the fan screen to the root tip, every surface in `Veggie` writes its own census."*

**Page 9 — Return to Earth (`5/5/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** `MELFI` tubes and four intact `VEG-03D` pillows (`2` red Romaine, `1` mizuna, `1` green leaf lettuce) arrive back at KSC, thawed overnight at `4°C` and shaken in `PBS` with `3–4 mm` glass beads on an `Omni BeadRuptor`.

**Page 10 — The Food-Safety Gate**

- **Panel 1 (Three-Plate Check):** `3M Petrifilm` (*E. coli*/coliform, *S. aureus*) and `BPW`/`RV` *Salmonella* broths all read **negative**.

- **Dialogue (Microbiologist):** *"Zero foodborne pathogens. The crew's orbital salad passed every safety screen."*

### Act II: The Spatial Microbiome (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 — Bacterial Loads Across Leaves**

- **Panel 1 (Bar Chart Panel):** `TSA` Aerobic Plate Counts on `VEG-03D` leaves: red Romaine averages `6.9 × 10⁶ CFU/g`, green leaf lettuce averages `7.9 × 10⁶ CFU/g`, and mizuna spans `1.4 × 10³` to `2.6 × 10⁷ CFU/g` across `D`/`E`—right in line with terrestrial farm and market produce.

**Page 12 — The Humidity Fingerprint (Yeast & Mold)**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** `IMA` fungal counts on `VEG-03D` leaves: mizuna lowest (`2.4 × 10⁴ CFU/g`), red Romaine (`5.1 × 10⁶ CFU/g`), green leaf lettuce highest (`9.8 × 10⁹ CFU/g`).

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** Second-harvest leaves carry higher fungal counts across all three crops—the biological echo of `25` days at `100%` humidity.

**Page 13 — Inside the Pillow (`10⁸` on the Wick)**

- **Panel 1 (Cutaway):** `VEG-03D` wicks hold the densest bacterial biofilms (`1.9 × 10⁸` to `1.8 × 10⁹ CFU/g`) and `4.7 × 10⁶` to `2.2 × 10⁸ CFU/g` fungi on the red Romaine wicks.

- **Panel 2 (Root Comparison):** Below them, red Romaine roots carry `3.4 × 10⁷` and `7.1 × 10⁷ CFU/g`, green leaf `1.3 × 10⁸ CFU/g`, and mizuna `2.6 × 10⁸ CFU/g`.

**Page 14 — Twenty Cultured Pioneers**

- **Panel 1 (Petri Montage):** Out of `30` bacterial genera isolated across `VEG-03D`, `E`, and `F`, **`20`** are recovered from `VEG-03D` alone—and bacteria grow from **all `8/8` surface swabs** (fungi from `5/8`), reflecting `SN 002`'s longer history on ISS compared to the brand-new `SN 001`.

**Page 15 — The `16S` Diversity Gradient**

- **Panel 1 (Violin/Box Plot Metaphor):** `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`) maps the `Shannon Index` (`0.1` to `2.0`): **arcillite substrate and roots** sit at the top, **wicks** in the middle, **swabs** (`0.8–1.4`), and **leaves** at the bottom (`Pielou` evenness `0.3` in substrate down to `0.1` on leaves).

**Page 16 — `99%` Sample Type (`PCoA`)**

- **Panel 1 (Three `PCoA` Plots):** Within `VEG-03D`, `Bray-Curtis` Axis 1 alone explains `67%` (mizuna), `76%` (red Romaine), and `79%` (green leaf) of variance. `Adonis` confirms `sample type` drives **`97%`** (mizuna), **`84%`** (red Romaine), and **`99%`** (green leaf) of community structure (`p < 0.05`).

- **Caption:** Root and substrate cluster together; the leaf stands apart; the wick bridges both worlds.

**Page 17 — Two Highways of Transfer**

- **Panel 1 (Venn Diagram Spread):** `22–31` genera are shared across **wick, substrate, and root** (`26` mizuna, `22` & `31` red Romaine, `27` green leaf). Only `1–9` genera span all four compartments (highest `9` in green leaf lettuce).

- **Panel 2 (Arrow Diagram):** Leaves share **zero** `16S` species with roots—yet share `6` cultured isolates with the **wick**, revealing **vertical transfer** from the above-ground wick and **horizontal transfer** between substrate and root.

**Page 18 — `Burkholderia` & `Ralstonia` from the Tap**

- **Panel 1 (Water Droplet Inset):** *Burkholderia* and *Ralstonia*—two genera routinely monitored in ISS potable water—dominate `VEG-03D` roots, wicks, and arcillite substrate while remaining rare or absent on leaves.

**Page 19 — The Leaf Specialists**

- **Panel 1 (Leaf Surface):** *Pseudomonas* (growth-promoting epi-endophyte) appears across `VEG-03D` leaves alongside *Enterobacter*, *Erwinia*, *Chitinophaga*, *Novosphingobium*, and *Bradyrhizobium*.

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** *Methylobacterium* settles on red Romaine and mizuna (consuming plant methanol), while antimicrobial *Janthinobacterium* appears on red Romaine.

**Page 20 — The Cabin Connection (`47` Shared Genera)**

- **Panel 1 (Station Map):** Cross-referencing `Avila-Herrera et al. (2020)` reveals **`250` bacterial genera** shared between `Veggie` surfaces and ISS habitable surfaces—and **`47` genera** shared across ISS surfaces, `Veggie` surfaces, and plant leaves (`Staphylococcus`, `Bacillus`, `Pantoea`, `Pseudomonas`).

- **Dialogue (Microbiologist):** *"The plants aren't isolated from the station. Crew hands, cabin air, and potable water all write onto the leaf."*

**Page 21 — The Fungal Four**

- **Panel 1 (Microscopic):** *Aspergillus* spp., *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium* spp., and *Rhodotorula* spp. appear across swabs, wicks, substrates, leaves, and roots—showing that once established in `Veggie SN 002`, fungi persist across grow-outs unless eliminated by cleaning.

**Page 22 — Tight Cluster of `VEG-03D`**

- **Panel 1 (Global Leaf `PCoA`):** Across all leaves from `D`, `E`, and `F`, `tech demo` accounts for **`~40%`** (`R² = 0.397, p = 0.001`) of variance while `plant type` accounts for only `10%` (`R² = 0.101`, non-significant). Every `VEG-03D` leaf (save one green leaf sample) clusters tightly together—stamped by the shared `SN 002` chamber and `25`-day humidity event.

### Act III: Continuous Harvest Ahead (Pages 23–30)

**Page 23 — Lessons from `VEG-03D`**

- **Panel 1 (Checklist):** Mixed cropping works in `Veggie`, crops are microbiologically safe (`E. coli`, *S. aureus*, *Salmonella* negative), and spatial compartments—not plant species alone—govern who lives where.

**Page 24 — Redesigning the Tray**

- **Panel 1 (Layout Comparison):** `VEG-03D`'s interleaved pillows (`Mizuna` shading `Outredgeous`) give way to a grouped layout in `VEG-03E` and `VEG-03F` for easier crew watering and canopy access.

**Page 25 — Archiving `OSD-412`**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** All `43` `MiSeq` `16S` amplicon datasets (`GLDS-412`, `10.26030/0jpd-te04`) lock into NASA OSDR, preserving the first multi-species ISS `Veggie` spatial microbiome.

**Page 26 — Two Units Side by Side**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** February 2018. In Columbus, a brand-new `Veggie` unit—**`SN 001`**—mounts beside veteran `SN 002` (`Figure 1B`).

**Page 27 — Three Days Apart (`VEG-03E` & `F`)**

- **Panel 1 (Timeline):** `VEG-03E` initiates `2/6/18` in `SN 001`; `VEG-03F` initiates `2/9/18` in `SN 002`—testing continuous, staggered orbital salad production.

**Page 28 — New Hardware vs. Veteran Chamber**

- **Panel 1 (Split):** Clean, unused `SN 001` (`VEG-03E`) vs. seasoned `SN 002` (`VEG-03F`)—setting up a natural experiment in how chamber age shapes surface and crop microbiomes.

**Page 29 — The `SN 001` Contrast**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** Early swabs from `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`) show far fewer colonized sites (`3/8` vs. `8/8` in `VEG-03D`), hinting at how a fresh chamber starts clean.

**Page 30 — Next Launch Window**

- **Panel 1 (Final Splash):** `Veggie SN 001` glows beside `SN 002` as `VEG-03E` (`OSD-413`) begins its `59`-day run.

- **Caption:** Next: `OSD-413` (*VEG-03E*). A brand-new `Veggie` unit, staggered harvests, and what changes when the chamber starts sterile.

### Right Spread (Page 32): Spatial `16S`/`ITS` Community Structure & `PCoA` Drivers

- **Sequencing (`OSD-412`, `n = 43`):** `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`), `16S` V4 & `ITS`, `RDP`/`GreenGenes`/`SILVA`/`UNITE`, `QIIME 2.0`.

- **Alpha & Beta Diversity:**

  - `Shannon Index`: `0.1–2.0` (substrate & roots > wick > swabs `0.8–1.4` > leaves); `Pielou` evenness `0.3` (substrate) to `0.1` (leaves).

  - Across all `VEG-03D/E/F` leaves: `tech demo` `R² = 0.397` (`p = 0.001`) vs. `plant type` `R² = 0.101` (NS).

  - Within `VEG-03D` pillows: `sample type` drove **`97%`** (mizuna, PCoA1 `67%`), **`84%`** (red Romaine, PCoA1 `76%`), and **`99%`** (green leaf, PCoA1 `79%`) of variance (`p < 0.05`).

- **Spatial Sharing & Key Taxa:**

  - `22–31` bacterial genera shared across wick + substrate + root (`26` mizuna, `22` & `31` red Romaine, `27` green leaf); `1–9` shared across all `4` compartments (`9` in green leaf); `0` `16S` species shared between leaf and root, vs. `6` cultured isolates shared between leaf and wick.

  - `250` genera shared between `Veggie` surfaces and ISS surfaces; `47` shared across ISS surfaces, `Veggie` surfaces, and leaves.

  - Key bacteria: *Burkholderia*, *Ralstonia*, *Pseudomonas*, *Enterobacter*, *Chitinophaga*, *Novosphingobium*, *Bradyrhizobium*, *Methylobacterium*, *Janthinobacterium*, *Staphylococcus*. Universal fungi: *Aspergillus* spp., *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium* spp., *Rhodotorula* spp.
