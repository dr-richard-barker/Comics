## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Leg-Pouch Trio (`F1`, `F2`, `F3` `KFTs`):** Three sealed `RNAlater` fixation tubes strapped to astronaut Sirisha Bandla's calf aboard `VSS Unity`. Each holds `6` seven-day-old `Col-0` seedlings on stainless-steel gel coupons, waiting for a gloved thumb to snap the plunger at climb (`F1`), rocket burnout (`F2`), and the end of weightlessness (`F3`).

- **`ROS` Core III (The Rapid-Stress Vanguard):** The high-light and peroxisomal oxidative-stress cluster from the `ROS Wheel` that fires across *all six* tissue-and-phase comparisons—proving plants register the transition into space within minutes, long before orbital steady state sets in.

- **`HSP20` vs. `HSP70`/`HSP90` (The Split Chaperones):** In long-term orbital flights (`APEX03-2`, `APEX04`), heat-shock families march in unison. During `Unity 22`, small **`HSP20`s** surge across roots and leaves, while **`HSP70`s**, **`HSP90`s**, and heat-shock factors (`HSFA2`, `HSFA7`, `HSFB2`) crash at `F3`.

- **The `F3` Root Wave (`714` DEGs):** The massive transcriptional surge captured at the end of `~3 minutes` of microgravity—activating light-harvesting **`LHC`** genes and `GUN`-linked `ROS Core I` retrograde signals while silencing **`GST`** scavengers and cytochrome `P450`s.

- **The Transient Repressors (`bHLH` & Histones):** Suborbital-exclusive responders absent from `APEX03`/`04`—`bHLH` transcription factors (split up in leaves, down in roots) and S-phase **histone** genes (`H4` in roots; `H2A`, `H2B`, `H3` in leaves) transiently silenced at `F3` as the cell cycle pauses at the edge of space.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: The Calf-Pouch Countdown (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1:**

- **Panel 1:** Spaceport America, New Mexico, `July 11, 2021`. Under a desert sunrise, mothership `VMS Eve` cradles the rocket-powered spaceplane `VSS Unity` on the runway.

- **Panel 2:** Flashback to `04:00 AM` inside the Virgin Galactic hangar: `7-day`-old `Col-0` seedlings—flown in by commercial air (`8 h` dark) and nurtured under `80 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹` LEDs—are lifted onto stainless-steel coupons coated with `1%` Phytagel, `0.5× MS`, and `5 g/L` sucrose.

- **Caption:** *Orbital experiments tell us how plants live in space after weeks. To catch the very first spark of adaptation, someone has to flip the fixative switch mid-flight.*

**Pages 2–3:**

- **Panel 1 (Page 2):** At `07:00 AM`, coupons seal inside three flight `KFTs` (`6` seedlings each); at `07:20 AM`, they are handed to the flight crew.

- **Panel 2 (Page 2):** Inside the cabin, Virgin Galactic astronaut **Sirisha Bandla** straps a custom three-slot pouch onto her calf, tethering `F1`, `F2`, and `F3` for one-handed access under high acceleration.

- **Panel 3 (Page 3):** Outside the hangar, an investigator straps on an identical analog pouch holding ground controls `G1`, `G2`, and `G3`, waiting on radio cues.

**Pages 4–6:**

- **Page 4:** `10:40 AM EST`. `VMS Eve` lifts off. During the climb at `~1 g` (`~4 minutes` before spaceship release), Bandla reaches down, pulls **`F1`**, and actuates the `RNAlater` plunger—locking in the baseline climb transcriptome.

- **Page 5:** At `~45,000 feet`, `VSS Unity` drops free. Its rocket motor ignites for `~60 seconds`, slamming cabin and calf pouch with sustained `3 g` (peaking near `4 g`) as the craft pitches vertical.

- **Page 6:** Engine cutoff! Silence—and weightlessness. Immediately after the `hyper-g` burn ends, Bandla actuates **`F2`**, freezing the exact molecular boundary between rocket thrust and microgravity.

**Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread Anchor):**

- **Visual:** A sweeping arc diagram of the **`Unity 22` flight profile** (`14 min 17 s` from release to runway) overlaid on `VSS Unity`'s cabin. At `~86 km` apogee, Sirisha Bandla floats beside the window, actuating **`F3`** at the end of `~3 minutes` of microgravity just before re-entry's `hyper-`/`hypo-g` spikes begin. Below each flight phase (`F1`, `F2`, `F3`), the root DEG bar chart explodes upward: **`151` DEGs (`F1`) → `355` DEGs (`F2`) → `714` DEGs (`F3`)**, dwarfing the **`20` DEGs** of the old autonomous `VP-03` flight!

- **Caption:** *Wait until landing (`VP-03`) and only `20` genes remain audible. Fix in real time on the astronaut's calf, and `714` root genes roar at apogee.*

**Pages 9–10:**

- **Page 9:** Back in Gainesville at UF ICBR, `KFTs` are opened and seedlings dissected into roots and leaves (`hypocotyls` set aside). `36` poly(A)-enriched directional libraries hit the `Illumina NovaSeq 6000` (`S4` lane, `2 × 150/151 bp`).

- **Page 10:** Because radio lag prevented exact second-by-second sync on the ground, `G1`, `G2`, and `G3` (`n = 9` per organ) are pooled as a robust baseline—revealing **`1,401` total DEGs** (`|log2FC| > 1`, `padj < 0.05`) across the six flight comparisons.

### Act II: The `ROS` Wheel & The Broken Chaperones (Pages 11–22)

**Pages 11–13:**

- **Page 11:** Overlap Venn diagrams split roots and leaves apart: `<24%` of root DEGs and `<23%` of leaf DEGs overlap across `F1`, `F2`, and `F3`, and only `~2%` of the `1,401` DEGs fire across all six conditions.

- **Page 12:** Mapping the `1,401` DEGs onto the **`ROS Wheel`** (`8` oxidative-stress clusters) lights up Groups `I`, `III`, `IV`, and `VII`. **`ROS Core III`**—the rapid high-light, peroxisomal `H2O2` alarm—blazes across *every* phase, while **`ROS Core I`** (`GUN` plastid-to-nucleus retrograde signaling) surges in `F3` roots.

- **Page 13:** Meanwhile, `flowering` and `housekeeping` genes stay underrepresented, proving this `3-minute` burst is no random noise—it is targeted stress triage.

**Pages 14–16:**

- **Page 14:** `t-SNE` and `DBSCAN` sort the `1,401` DEGs into **`27` clusters**, with **`11` clusters** significantly enriched (`padj < 0.01`) for specific gene families—peaking sharply at `F3`.

- **Page 15:** A surprise splits the **Heat Shock Protein (`HSP`)** family in half. In orbital `APEX03-2` and `APEX04`, `HSP`s rise together; here, small **`HSP20`s** ignite across roots and leaves from `F1` to `F3`, while **`HSP70`s**, **`HSP90`s**, and heat-shock factors (**`HSFA2`, `HSFA7`, `HSFB2`**) are **repressed** in leaves and `F3` roots.

- **Page 16:** **Dialogue (Transcriptomics Lead):** *"The plant doesn't launch its full orbital chaperone suite in three minutes. It deploys fast `HSP20` first-responders while throttling down `HSP70`/`90`."*

**Pages 17–19:**

- **Page 17:** At `F3`, root signaling undergoes a sharp metabolic pivot: **`LHC`** (chlorophyll a/b-binding) transcripts surge upward in roots, while **`GST`** (glutathione S-transferases) and cytochrome `P450`s drop—`5` `LHC` and `7` `GST` genes matching long-duration `APEX03`/`04` signatures.

- **Page 18:** Pathway bars diverge by organ: **circadian** genes stir only in roots, while **jasmonic acid** and **red/far-red light** pathways wake only in leaves; **ER protein processing** rises in both.

- **Page 19:** Then come the **suborbital exclusives**—genes never flagged in `APEX03`/`04` orbital steady state: **`bHLH` transcription factors** (`1` up, `3` down in roots; `5` up in leaves) and **histone genes** (`H4` in roots; `H2A`, `H2B`, `H3` in leaves), heavily **repressed at `F3`**.

**Pages 20–22:**

- **Page 20:** *Chromatin Pause:* As `VSS Unity` crests `86 km`, repressing core histones (`H2A`, `H2B`, `H3`, `H4`) hints at an instant, transient brake on S-phase DNA replication and cell-cycle progression.

- **Page 21:** Across `Unity 22`, the `1.61 TB` `GLDS-611` dataset proves that terrestrial plants sense the loss of gravity and initiate orbital-like `ROS` and `LHC` programs in **less than three minutes**—via a distinct, transient transcriptional bridge.

- **Page 22:** `OSD-624` closes the suborbital arc—leaving one burning question hanging over the epigenome.

### Act III: From Transient Histones to Single-Molecule Methylation (Pages 23–30)

**Pages 23–25:**

- **Page 23:** If three minutes of microgravity can silence histone transcripts, what happens when *Arabidopsis* roots spend **days** in spaceflight? Do those transient chromatin signals harden into **DNA methylation**?

- **Page 24:** Flashback to **`APEX-04-EpEx`** on the ISS (`Col-0` wild-type vs. **`elp2-5`**, a mutant lacking **Elongator Complex Subunit 2**).

- **Page 25:** Earlier whole-genome bisulfite sequencing (`WGBS`) showed broad methylation shifts—but short-read bisulfite fragments shattered long-range patterns across spaceflight genes.

**Pages 26–28:**

- **Page 26:** Inside the UF ICBR sequencing core, a new long-read capture tool powers up: **Flap-Enabled Next-Generation Capture (`FENGC`)** paired with `EM-seq` conversion on a **PacBio `Sequel IIe` SMRT** platform.

- **Page 27:** Instead of averaged population percentages, single-molecule long reads trace cytosine methylation molecule-by-molecule across entire target gene regions in `Col-0` and `elp2-5` spaceflight roots.

- **Page 28:** Regional methylation blocks snap into focus—showing exactly how `ELP2` sculpts the spaceflight methylome.

**Pages 29–30 (Cliffhanger):**

- **Page 29:** On the OSDR chronology ledger, `OSD-624` locks into place and the very next accession—with **zero skipped studies**—lights up: **`OSD-625`**.

- **Page 30:** Close-up on a single-molecule PacBio read glowing with `5mC` methyl tags along an `elp2-5` root promoter.

- **Caption:** *Next in Roots in Orbit: Volume 53 (`OSD-625`) — Single-Molecule Methylation & Elongator Subunit 2 (`APEX-04-EpEx`).*
