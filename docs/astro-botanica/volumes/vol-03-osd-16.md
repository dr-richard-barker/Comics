## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Volume:** `Vol. 3 — THE DENATURED PROTEOME`

- **Target Accession:** `OSD-16` (`GLDS-16` / `PXD001179` / DOI: `10.25966/scd9-gb35`)

- **Primary Publication:** Ferl RJ, Koh J, Denison F, Paul AL. "Spaceflight induces specific alterations in the proteomes of Arabidopsis." *Astrobiology*. 2015;15(1):32–56. DOI: `10.1089/ast.2014.1210` (PMID: `25517942`)

- **Organism & Genotypes:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* — GFP reporter lines (`Adh::GFP`, `DR5r::GFP`, `35s::GFP`) harvested from **Run 1B** (`12-day-old` seedlings; Roots `n = 3` flight vs. `n = 3` ground, Leaves `n = 1` pooled flight vs. `n = 1` pooled ground).

- **Flight Hardware & Conditions:** **Advanced Biological Research System (ABRS)** with **GFP Imaging System (GIS)** on the ISS (`STS-129` launch Nov 16, 2009; Run 1B active growth `12/3/2009–12/15/2009`; returned on `STS-130` Feb 8, 2010), `10 cm²` solid media plates, VOC-scrubbed air, `light cycle: Not Available` in OSDR metadata. Ground control in identical ABRS inside the **Orbital Environmental Simulator (OES)** at KSC. Preserved on orbit in **RNAlater-filled KFTs** and stored at `-80°C`. Analyzed via **iTRAQ 8-plex** (`113–115` GC roots, `116–118` FLT roots, `119` GC leaves, `121` FLT leaves) on an **LTQ Orbitrap XL MS/MS** with **Eksigent nano-LC AS2**.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Run 1B Veterans (`Adh::GFP`, `DR5r::GFP`, `35s::GFP`):** Returning directly from the **ABRS/GIS** plates of Vol. 1 (`OSD-7`)—except this time, we follow the **proteins** extracted from their `RNAlater`-denatured tissues.

- **The RNAlater Salt Armor (Preservation Hazard):** A strong protein denaturant meant strictly for RNA. Surviving extraction in liquid-nitrogen-chilled buffer (`16 µg` per sample) strips away subcellular compartments, leaving only soluble protein survivors.

- **The iTRAQ 8-Plex Squad (`Tags 113–121`):** Eight mass-tag couriers (`113–115` Ground Roots, `116–118` Flight Roots, `119` Ground Leaves, `121` Flight Leaves) racing through a `14-fraction` SCX-HPLC and nano-LC Orbitrap gauntlet.

- **Root Oxidative & Energy Corps (`358` Differential Proteins: `237` Up, `121` Down):** Root proteome units surging in **cofactor metabolism**, **oxidative stress responses**, **cellular lipid metabolism**, and **ATP / purine nucleoside binding**—while **cell structure** proteins drop into scarcity.

- **Leaf Translation & Photosynthesis Corps (`256` Differential Proteins: `200` Up, `56` Down):** Leaf proteome units ramping up **ribosomal structural constituents**, **translation**, **photosynthesis**, **stress/defense**, **isomerase activity**, and **cell structure**—while **precursor metabolite/energy generation, localization, and transport** power down.

- **The 21 Polarity Flippers:** `21` shared proteins that reverse polarity between organs (up in leaves, down in roots—or vice versa), echoing `PGP18` from Vol. 1.

- **The 34 Concordant Messengers (`r = 0.49`):** The rare `34` genes that show matching differential shifts at *both* the mRNA (`OSD-7`) and protein (`OSD-16`) levels.

- **Metadata Anomaly ("Single-Pool Canopy & Missing Clock"):** While roots have `3` biological replicates (`80 mg` each), leaves are represented by only `1` pooled sample (`7` plants) per condition, and `light cycle` remains `Not Available`.

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

### Page 31 — Proteomic Divergence & Multi-Omic Concordance

- **Core Biological Takeaway:** Spaceflight drives **organ-specific proteomic remodeling** in 12-day-old *Arabidopsis* (`ABRS/GIS`, Run 1B). Out of `1,570` proteins identified (`5%` FDR; `1,167` leaf and `1,150` root proteins quantified), **`358` root proteins** (`237` up, `121` down) and **`256` leaf proteins** (`200` up, `56` down) changed significantly (`p < 0.05`, FC `> 1.2` or `< 0.8`).

- **Organ Polarity:** Only `47` proteins shifted in the same direction in both organs, while `21` shifted in **opposite directions**.

  - **Leaves:** Upregulated **translation/ribosomes, photosynthesis, stress/defense, isomerase activity, and cell structure**; downregulated **transport, localization, and precursor metabolite/energy generation**.

  - **Roots:** Upregulated **oxidative stress response, cofactor metabolism, cellular lipid metabolism, and ATP/purine nucleoside binding**; downregulated **cell structure** abundance.

- **mRNA vs. Protein (`OSD-7` vs. `OSD-16`):** Only `34` genes were differentially regulated in both datasets, but those `34` showed a strong positive correlation (`r = 0.49`, `p = 0.0003`; `rs = 0.51`, `p = 0.00016`).

### Page 32 — BLSS Implications & Study Citation

- **Bioregenerative Life Support (BLSS) Impact:**

  - **Multi-Omics from Single KFTs:** Proved that **RNAlater-fixed** orbital samples can yield both high-quality RNA (`OSD-7`) and quantitative iTRAQ proteomics (`OSD-16`), maximizing science return per gram of crew-harvested crop tissue.

  - **Root Energetic & Oxidative Load:** Spaceflight roots burn more ATP/cofactors and mount heavier oxidative-stress defenses than leaves—key targets for engineering resilient hydroponic/aeroponic BLSS crops.

  - **Post-Transcriptional Gap:** Because mRNA and protein profiles diverge (`34` shared DEGs/DEPs), crop-health biosensors must validate protein-level outcomes rather than relying on transcriptomes alone.
