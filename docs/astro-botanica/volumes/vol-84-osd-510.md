## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Computational Oracle (The DREM Algorithm):** A systems biology algorithm modeling the time-series flow of gene expression as a branching tree of hidden Markov model states, identifying the precise seconds when gene paths bifurcate.
- **Node 1 Master Governor (`SOG1`):** The root split point at `T = 0.5 hours`. SOG1 controls Split Event 1, dividing all 28,000 genes into two overarching branches: Activated Defense Paths vs. Repressed Growth Paths.
- **Node 2 Mitotic Brake (`MYB3R3` / `MYB3R5`):** Bifurcation Node 2 at `T = 1.0 hour`. Shuts down the G2/M mitotic engine across 410 genes via MSA promoter element binding.
- **The Secondary Wave Regulators (`ANAC085`, `ANAC044`, `ERF115`):** Downstream transcription factors activated at Node 3 (`T = 2.0 hours`), taking over long-term chromatin stabilization and stem cell regeneration.
- **The E2F-DP Coordinate Axis (`E2FB`, `E2FC`, `RBR1`):** Regulating the G1/S transition branch in parallel with the MYB3R G2/M branch.
- **Dr. Lieven De Veylder & Dr. Arp Schnittger:** Systems architects transforming raw omics data into the definitive circuit diagram of plant genome defense.
