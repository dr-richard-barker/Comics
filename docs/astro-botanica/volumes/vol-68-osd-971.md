## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 68

- **Chapter Title:** *The Polyamine Shield: Unlocking the Orbital Genome*

- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-971` (Genetic alterations of the putrescine metabolic pathway attenuate plant responses to spaceflight)

- **Primary Publication:** Su, S.-H., & Masson, P. H. (2026). *Genetic alterations of the putrescine metabolic pathway attenuate plant responses to spaceflight*. *npj Microgravity*, `12:45` (DOI: `10.1038/s41526-026-00663-4`).

- **Chronology Note:** The Grand Series Climax of ***ASTRO-BOTANICA: ZERO-G ROOTS***. Continues directly after Volume 67 (`OSD-895`, EDEN ISS polar citadel). Accessions `OSD-896` through `OSD-970` (non-plant multi-omics datasets) are recorded in `skipped_accessions`. Synthesizes sixty-eight volumes of plant spaceflight discovery into the ultimate genetic adaptation: targeted engineering of the polyamine (putrescine/spermidine) metabolic pathway.

- **Setting & Hardware:** International Space Station (ISS) US National Laboratory — **Advanced Plant Habitat (APH)** and **Veggie** flight facilities (SpaceX-25 mission / Increment 67). Growth rigs: sterile Phytagel growth plates (`10 cm × 10 cm`) inside automated growth cassettes under controlled environmental parameters (`22°C`, `65%` RH, `16:8` light/dark photoperiod under broad-spectrum LED arrays at `100 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹`). Ground controls conducted in parallel inside the KSC Orbital Environmental Simulator (OES) with real-time telemetry synchronization. Harvest: Kennedy Space Center Fixation Tubes (KFTs) with RNAlater fixative followed by MELFI deep-freezing (`<-80°C`).

- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* (Columbia-0 wild-type) compared against genetically engineered polyamine metabolic pathway mutant and overexpressor lines:
  - Wild-Type (`Col-0`) baseline controls.
  - *spds1 spds2* (Spermidine Synthase double mutants with elevated putrescine pools).
  - *adc1 adc2* (Arginine Decarboxylase mutants with depleted putrescine).
  - *SAMDC* overexpressors (S-adenosylmethionine decarboxylase metabolic flux lines).
  Assays: High-throughput whole-genome stranded RNA-Seq (`Illumina NovaSeq 6000`), targeted polyamine metabolite quantification (HPLC-MS/MS), reactive oxygen species (ROS) fluorescent live imaging, root skewing and waving morphometrics, and comprehensive multi-omic differential pathway network analysis.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Squad Col-0 (Wild-Type Benchmark):** The veteran spaceflight warrior. Experienced in dozens of orbital missions, but still prone to chronic transcriptomic stress, oxidative burst, cell wall softening, and premature senescence in microgravity.

- **Squad Polyamine (The Putrescine Mutants):**
  - **The Putrescine Sentinel (*spds1 spds2*):** Genetically engineered line blocking downstream spermidine conversion, accumulating abundant intracellular putrescine. Radiates a calm blue metabolic aura that deflects oxidative damage.
  - **The Depleted Scout (*adc1 adc2*):** Lacking arginine decarboxylase; hyper-sensitized to orbital stress, confirming the protective role of polyamines.

- **The Molecular Commanders (The Polyamine Regulatory Axis):**
  - **Commander `ADC2` (Arginine Decarboxylase 2):** Primary tactical officer initiating the synthesis of putrescine from basic amino acid stores.
  - **Officer `SPDS1` & `SPDS2` (Spermidine Synthases):** The downstream gatekeepers controlling the conversion of putrescine into higher polyamines.
  - **The ROS Scavenger Vanguard (`CAT1`, `APX1`, `SOD`):** Peroxidase and dismutase enzymes working hand-in-hand with polyamine pools to extinguish free radicals.
  - **The Transcriptomic Dampener (`WRKY`, `HSF`, `DREB` Axis):** Stress transcription factor brigades whose emergency alarms are naturally quieted when putrescine levels are fortified.

- **The Space Biologist Architects (Dr. Patrick Masson & Dr. Shih-Heng Su):** Leading space geneticists deciphering how altering a single ancient metabolic node can silence the cacophony of spaceflight stress genes.

- **The CoSE Ensemble (Doc B, Sprout, Myco, Statolith, Pixel, and Commander Astra):** Gathered in the ISS Cupola to witness the final synthesis of sixty-eight volumes of discovery.
