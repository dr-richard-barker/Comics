## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*
- **Volume:** 69 (Special Chronicles: Gravitropism & Morphogenesis)
- **Chapter Title:** *The Helical Skew: Tubulin Dynamics in Orbit*
- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-193` (During development, the Sku6 mutant roots engage different genes than wild type Col-0 roots, either on the ground or in spaceflight)
- **Primary Publication:** Rutherford, R., Masson, P. H. (2020). *Microtubule dynamics and intrinsic organ twist in microgravity: Comparative transcriptome analysis of SKU6/SPR1 mutants on the International Space Station*. *Plant, Cell & Environment*, `43(8):1945-1961` (DOI: `10.1111/pce.13788`).
- **Setting & Hardware:** European Modular Cultivation System (**EMCS**) on board the International Space Station (Columbus Laboratory). Centrifuge rotors providing 0g and simulated 1g controls. Growth in sealed Experiment Containers (ECs) on nutrient agar cassettes under narrow-band red and blue LED arrays. Post-run preservation in Kennedy Fixation Tubes (KFTs) with RNAlater.
- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* (Columbia-0 wild-type vs. *sku6-1*/*spr1-1* loss-of-function mutants). Microarray and stranded RNA-Seq expression profiling comparing 4-day, 6-day, and 8-day seedling roots. Key phenotypes: root waving, epidermal cell file rotation, and skewing angles.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Squad Col-0 (Wild-Type Baseline):** Straight-growing, statolith-competent seedlings balancing gravitropic downward vectors with circumnutation.
- **Squad SKU6 / SPIRAL1 (*sku6-1* Mutants):** Mutant seedlings lacking the cortical microtubule-associated protein SKU6/SPR1. On Earth they twist to the left; in orbit, uncoupled from gravity's downward anchor, their roots curl into dynamic logarithmic spirals.
- **The Microtubule Guard (`SPR1`, `TUA6`, `TUB4`):** Alpha- and beta-tubulin protofilaments forming the structural tracks along which cellulose synthase complexes glide.
- **The Wall Relaxers (`XTH17`, `XTH18`, `EXPA7`):** Xyloglucan endotransglucosylase and expansin enzymes mediating differential cell wall loosening along the curving flank.
- **The Auxin Vector Corps (`PIN2`, `AUX1`, `ABCB19`):** Asymmetric polar auxin transporters redirecting indole-3-acetic acid to drive torsional elongation.
- **Dr. Patrick Masson & Dr. John Z. Kiss:** Space gravitropism investigators tracking how microtubule chirality dictates plant organ architecture in zero-g.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: The Unanchored Helix (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1**
- **Panel 1 [Wide Establishing Shot]:** Inside the Columbus module of the ISS, the European Modular Cultivation System (EMCS) rotor hums quietly, its internal centrifuge spinning one cassette at 1g while the static hub floats at microgravity (0g).
- **Panel 2 [Macro Zoom]:** Transparent growth cassettes reveal *Arabidopsis* roots. The wild-type Col-0 roots reach downward in 1g, but in the 0g rotor hub, the *sku6* mutants begin curving laterally across the agar surface.
- **Caption (Narrator):** *On Earth, gravity masks the hidden chiral twists of cellular growth. But take away the 1g anchor, and the intrinsic torque of the cell wall takes command.*
- **Dialogue (Mission Specialist):** "EMCS Rotor 2 static hub stable at zero-point-zero-two g. Look at the *sku6* cassette—they aren't growing straight. They're spiraling."

**Page 2**
- **Panel 1:** Subcellular cutaway inside a cortical root cell of *sku6-1*. Microtubule fibers, normally aligned in tight transverse parallel hoops, are tilted at an oblique 45-degree angle.
- **Panel 2:** Cellulose synthase complexes (CESA rosettes) move along the tilted tubulin tracks, spinning microfibrils into a helical sheath.
- **SFX:** *KRRR-TWIST (Structural shearing)*
- **Dialogue (Bioinformatician):** "Without the SKU6 protein to stabilize the plus-end tips, the microtubules are slipping! Torsional strain is propagating up the entire elongation zone!"

**Page 3**
- **Panel 1:** Comparison of Col-0 vs *sku6* root tips under high-contrast seinen screentone. The Col-0 columella statoliths float aimlessly in microgravity, but the cell elongation remains symmetric.
- **Panel 2:** In *sku6*, the entire epidermal cell column rotates clockwise when viewed from the apex.
- **Caption:** *A genetic lesion in a single microtubule cap unmasks an ancient biophysical torque.*

**Page 4**
- **Panel 1:** Ground control chamber at KSC. On a vertical agar plate, *sku6* roots skew toward the right at an angle of 28 degrees.
- **Panel 2:** Split panel with the orbital flight plate. In zero-g, the skew becomes a full 360-degree loop.
- **Dialogue (Dr. Masson):** "Gravity was acting as a continuous corrective feedback loop. In spaceflight, *sku6* roots engage an entirely different transcriptional circuit to cope with unrestrained twisting!"

**Page 5–10**
- Transcriptomic divergence at Day 4 vs Day 8.
- Upregulation of cell wall loosening enzymes: *XTH17* and *EXPA7* light up the transcriptomic HUD at `+3.8 log2FC`.
- Kinase cascades detecting mechanical tension in the plasma membrane (*FERONIA* and *THE1* receptor kinases activated).

### Act II: The Transcriptomic Divergence (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- RNA-Seq heatmaps reveal over 1,420 differentially expressed genes between flight *sku6* and flight Col-0.
- While Col-0 activates oxidative stress pathways (*PRX*, *CAT*), the *sku6* mutant diverts its energy into secondary cell wall biosynthesis (*IRX3*, *CESA4*, *CESA8*).
- Auxin redistribution: *PIN2* polar localization shifts from the basal membrane to lateral faces, guiding the root into tight orbital coils.

**Page 16–20**
- The crew harvests the cassettes into Kennedy Fixation Tubes (KFTs) filled with RNAlater.
- Plunged into the MELFI freezer at -80°C to lock the RNA states.
- Flight telemetry confirms: *SKU6/SPR1* is not just a structural cap; it acts as an environmental brake that integrates mechanoperception with organ symmetry.

### Act III: The Architecture of Weightless Roots (Pages 21–32)

**Page 21–25**
- Comparative analysis between 0g flight, 1g centrifuge on orbit, and 1g ground controls.
- The 1g centrifuge in space partially restores root straightness, demonstrating that centrifugal force can substitute for terrestrial gravity in guiding directional growth.
- Mathematical modeling of root waving vectors: frequency vs amplitude.

**Page 26–30**
- The cellular biomechanics debrief: how future Martian and lunar greenhouse rootstocks can be genetically engineered for optimal nutrient foraging by tuning microtubule angle genes.
- Closing panel: An artistic double-page splash showing a golden helix of root hairs embracing nutrient beads in microgravity.

**Page 31–32**
- Full infographic spread: Flight vs Ground differential expression matrix for *SKU6/SPR1* pathways.
