## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Col-0 Squad (`SW` / `GW`):** Wild-type etiolated seedlings (`14 days` in `BRIC-16`). Their primary roots maintain relatively straight trajectories in `0g`—until their root-hair tip-growth genes go dark.

- **act2-3 Squad (The Uncoiled Actin Mutant, `SA` / `GA`):** Vegetative `ACTIN2` knockout mutants. Indistinguishable from `Col-0` on Earth (`1g`), in microgravity their roots violently **coil and skew left-handed** (high FFT entropy) with severely disrupted nascent cell walls.

- **PRX44 & PRX57 (The Tip-Growth Peroxidases):** Uncharacterized root-hair-enriched **class III peroxidases** **repressed** in microgravity across *both* `Col-0` and `act2-3` (actin-independent), mirroring hypoxia repression. When knocked out on Earth, their root-hair tips **rupture** from thin, weakened cell walls.

- **Phosphoinositide Signaling Unit:** Membrane lipid-signaling relays modulating root-hair polarity in an **actin-dependent** branch—the sole cytoskeletal fork where `act2-3` diverges from `Col-0`.

- **Plastid & Mitochondrial Organelle Choir:** Dark-grown organelle transcripts (`GLDS-44` enrichment noted in PMC7830483) humming abnormally in total darkness alongside `BRIC-16` sibling `OSD-17` (Vol. 4).

### Page 31 (Mission Debrief — Biological & Molecular Summary)

- **Header:** `OSD-44 MISSION DEBRIEF: ROOT HAIRS & ACTIN IN BRIC-16 (STS-131)`

- **Panel A — Root Hair Peroxidase Repression:** `14`-day dark-grown *Arabidopsis* seedlings (`Col-0` and `act2-3`) repressed **oxidative stress and cell-wall remodeling** genes enriched in **root hairs**, including class III peroxidases **`PRX44`** and **`PRX57`** (Kwon et al., 2015).

- **Panel B — Earth Mutant Proof:** `prx44` and `prx57` knockouts on Earth exhibit **ruptured root hairs** due to mechanically weak tip cell walls, directly linking microgravity gene repression to spaceflight root-hair defects.

- **Panel C — Actin-Independent vs. Actin-Dependent:** Class III peroxidase repression in `0g` is **actin-independent** (seen in both `Col-0` and `act2-3`), whereas **phosphoinositide-mediated** root-hair modulation is **actin-dependent**.

- **Panel D — Left-Handed Coiling & Cell Wall Disruption:** `act2-3` spaceflight roots coiled/skewed left-handedly with higher FFT entropy and severely disrupted cell walls (`Nakashima et al., 2014`), proving the actin cytoskeleton suppresses endogenous skewing in `0g`.

### Page 32 (Mission Debrief — BLSS Implications & Citation)

- **BLSS Engineering Takeaway:**

  - **Protecting Nutrient Capture:** Because root hairs drive water and mineral uptake, `0g`-induced hypoxia and `PRX44`/`PRX57` repression threaten nutrient-use efficiency in space crops.

  - **Cytoskeletal Architecture:** Preserving `ACTIN2` integrity prevents excessive root coiling and wall delamination when gravity's stabilizing vector is absent.

- **Primary Source:** Kwon T et al. (2015), *Am J Bot* 102(1): 21–35 (PubMed) | `OSD-44`.
