## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Squad `MM2d` (The Statolithless Callus):** Undifferentiated *Arabidopsis* suspension-derived cells flattened into a `1–2 mm` biofilm on agar. Unlike Vol. 1's seedlings, they have **no roots, no leaves, and no amyloplast statoliths**—meaning any gravity sensing here is purely cellular.

- **The Bitter Bore (16.5 T Magnet):** A towering vertical superconducting/water-cooled solenoid (`22°C` bath) where diamagnetic water levitates at `BB' = -1360 T²/m`. Divided into five vertical battlegrounds: `μg*`, `0.1 g*`, `1 g*`, `1.9 g*`, and `2 g*`.

- **The Twin Mechanical Arenas (`μg-RPM` & `2 g-LDC`):** A 3D-tumbling gyroscope (`60 rpm`) and a roaring centrifugal arm at ESA-ESTEC (plagued by a `+1°C` thermal leak in the central `1 g` gondola).

- **Thylakoid Alignment Corps (Upregulated in `1 g*`, `16.5 T`):** Plastid membrane units physically torqued into parallel formation by magnetic fields `≥ 1 T`, firing biosynthetic signals even in total darkness.

- **Transferases & Peroxidases (Common Magnet Stressors):** Frontline enzyme engineers reacting across magnet positions to the `> 10 T` magnetic field.

- **Cell Wall & Cytoskeleton Corps (Altered in `μg*`):** Structural remodelers and encapsulating-structure proteins responding to diamagnetic levitation.

- **Abiotic Stress Repressors (Downregulated in `μg-RPM`):** `77` stress-and-stimulus genes powered down under 3D clinorotation.

- **The GEDI Mosaic (`20 × 16` Centroid Grid):** A glowing `320-tile` bioluminescent HUD (`~54` probesets/tile) that bypasses single-gene `FDR` blindness to reveal the inverted hypogravity vs. hypergravity genome-wide wave.

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

### Page 31 — The Synergy & Statistical Blindness Takeaway

- **Core Biological Takeaway:** Undifferentiated *Arabidopsis* `MM2d` callus cultures—lacking statoliths—still sense and respond to altered effective gravity (`μg*`, `0.1 g*`, `1.9 g*`, `2 g*`), but the response is a **subtle, genome-wide fine-tuning** (`17,419` probesets in GEDI) rather than a massive single-gene spike.

- **Magnetic vs. Gravitational Effects:**

  - **Pure High Magnetic Field (`1 g*`, `16.5 T`):** Altered `96` genes (`limma p < 0.01`; `14` at `RankProd FDR < 0.05`), notably upregulating **thylakoid-related genes** (physical alignment at `≥ 1 T`) alongside **transferases** and **peroxidases**.

  - **Synergistic Stress (`0.1 g*` & `1.9 g*` at `14.7 T`):** Combining high `B` with altered `g*` tripled altered probesets (`283–318`) and triggered the broadest GO enrichment collapse.

  - **GEDI Inversion:** Subtracting `1 g*` from `xg*` revealed opposite global transcriptional states between **hypogravity** (`μg*`/`0.1 g*`) and **hypergravity** (`1.9 g*`/`2 g*`), mirrored between `μg-RPM` (`77` abiotic-stress genes repressed) and `1.9 g* − 1 g*`.

### Page 32 — BLSS Implications & Study Citation

- **Bioregenerative Life Support (BLSS) Impact:**

  - **Whole-Genome Diagnostics Over Single-Gene Thresholds:** Relying solely on strict `FDR` or `2.5-fold` cutoffs misses subtle, coordinated transcriptional shifts in partial-gravity (`0.17 g` Moon / `0.38 g` Mars) environments.

  - **Simulator Caveats:** Ground simulators introduce non-gravitational confounders—high magnetic fields (`10–16.5 T`) align plastid membranes, while centrifuges (`2 g-LDC`) add inertial shear and thermal drift (`+1°C`).

  - **Statolith-Independent Sensing:** Even undifferentiated plant cell cultures (`MM2d`) detect gravity shifts, proving cellular tensegrity/membrane mechanosensing operates independently of root statoliths.
