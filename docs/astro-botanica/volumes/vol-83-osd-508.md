## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Chronometer of Repair (The Temporal Cascade):**
  - **The Immediate Alarm (0.5h–1h):** SOG1-driven emergency activation (*WEE1*, *SMR5*, *PARP2*).
  - **The Repair Engine (2h–4h):** Full-scale deployment of homologous recombination machines (*RAD51*, *BRCA1*, *GMI1*).
  - **The Resolution Vanguard (8h–12h):** DNA ligases, chromatin repackaging chaperones (*CAF1*), and dephosphorylation of γ-H2AX.
  - **The Renaissance (24h):** Reactivation of mitotic cyclins (*CYCB1;1*, *CDKB2;1*) as the meristem returns to normal growth.
- **The Structural Cohesin Complex (`GMI1`, *SMC5/6*):** Chromosome scaffolding proteins that physically clamp severed chromatid ends together during homologous recombination.
- **The Recovery Phosphatases (`PP2A`, `PPM1D`):** Enzymes removing phosphate tags from SOG1 and γ-H2AX once repair is validated, releasing the cell cycle brake.
- **Dr. Camilla Sjogren & Dr. Lieven De Veylder:** Chrono-biologists mapping the temporal symphony of plant genome preservation.

### Act I: The Stopwatch Begins (Pages 1–10)

- **Panel 2:** The molecular landscape at `T = 30 minutes`. Over three hundred double-strand breaks have been detected. Phosphorylated SOG1 has already occupied the promoters of its primary targets.
- **Caption (Narrator):** *A genome under radiation is not a static picture. It is a four-dimensional kinetic performance where seconds decide survival and hours restore order.*
- **Dialogue (Robotic Operator):** "Timepoint Thirty Minutes secured. Transcriptional burst detected in *WEE1* and *SMR5*. Mitotic arrest is instantaneous."

**Page 2**
- **Panel 1:** At `T = 1 hour`. *RAD51* and *BRCA1* transcript levels climb exponentially, reaching a towering peak of `+5.8 log2FC`.
- **Panel 2:** Nuclei are packed with RAD51 nucleoprotein filaments searching for homologous DNA donor sequences across sister chromatids.
- **SFX:** *SHHH-ZIP (Homology searching along chromatid strands)*
- **Dialogue (Bioinformatician):** "Two hours post-irradiation! Homologous recombination is running at maximum velocity! The break repair curve is steepening!"

- **Caption:** *The crisis is passing. The broken threads are sewn back together.*

**Page 4–10**
- Kinetic cluster analysis reveals four distinct temporal transcriptional waves:
  - Wave 1 (0.5–1h): Immediate-Early Sensor / Arrest (*SOG1*, *WEE1*, *SMR5*).
  - Wave 2 (1–4h): Catalytic Repair Engine (*RAD51*, *BRCA1*, *PARP1*, *GMI1*).
  - Wave 3 (4–12h): Chromatin Restoration & Phosphatase Activation (*CAF1*, *PPM1D*).
  - Wave 4 (12–24h): Mitotic Re-Entry & Growth Recovery (*CYCB1;1*, *CDKB2;1*).

### Act II: The Resolution Switch (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- What turns the alarm off?
- Phosphatase *PP2A* dephosphorylates SOG1, causing it to detach from the palindromic promoter boxes.
- Simultaneously, the *MYB3R1/3/5* repressor complex is disassembled or cleared from MSA elements, releasing the transcriptional blockade on *CYCB1;1*.

**Page 16–20**
- The cost of recovery:
  - Seedlings that resume growth at 24 hours exhibit normal cellular elongation.
  - Root growth velocity recovers to 92% of the un-irradiated control by Day 3.
  - Proving that the plant's repair kinetics are fully homeostatic and non-permanent.

### Act III: The Mars Transit Chronometer (Pages 21–32)

**Page 21–25**
- Modeling continuous low-dose radiation during an 8-month transit to Mars:
  - In a continuous low-dose environment (`0.5 mGy/day`), the system does not cycle through extreme waves.
  - Instead, it establishes an elevated steady-state plateau: baseline repair enzymes (*RAD51*) remain upregulated by `+1.8 log2FC`, while the cell cycle proceeds at 88% velocity with zero developmental arrest.

**Page 26–30**
- The technological blueprint: Using the kinetic time-series data to calibrate radiation biosensors in autonomous space plant habitats.
- Final panel: An automated Mars greenhouse habitat under the red sky, its robotic sensors tracking the steady, unbroken kinetic pulse of healthy plant chromosomes.
