## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 67

- **Chapter Title:** *The Polar Citadel: 268 Days at Neumayer III*

- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-895` (Biomass Production of the EDEN ISS Space Greenhouse in Antarctica During the 2018 Experiment Phase)

- **Primary Publication:** Zabel, P., Zeidler, C., Vrakking, V., Dorn, M., & Schubert, D. (2020). *Biomass Production of the EDEN ISS Space Greenhouse in Antarctica During the 2018 Experiment Phase*. *Frontiers in Plant Science*, `11:656`. (PMID: `32528506` | DOI: `10.3389/fpls.2020.00656`)

- **Chronology Note:** Continues directly after Volume 66 (`OSD-883`, Apollo 14 Fra Mauro contact trials). Accessions `OSD-884` through `OSD-894` (non-plant, microbial, and spaceflight radiation benchmarks) are recorded in `skipped_accessions`. Advances to the iconic EDEN ISS closed-loop Antarctic polar analog campaign.

- **Setting & Hardware:** German Neumayer Station III, Ekström Ice Shelf, Atka Bay, Antarctica (`70°40'S, 08°16'W`). The **EDEN ISS Mobile Test Facility (MTF)**: a 20-foot shipping container complex consisting of the Cold Porch, Service Section, and the **Future Exploration Greenhouse (FEG)** (`12.5 m²` footprint, `3.68 m²` active multilevel plant growth area). Closed-loop aeroponic/hydroponic cultivation systems, high-efficiency water-cooled LED light fixtures with dynamic spectral adjustment (deep red `660 nm`, far-red `730 nm`, blue `440 nm`, white `400–700 nm`), autonomous nutrient delivery loops with automated EC and pH dosing, HEPA-filtered air circulation with `CO₂` enrichment (`800–1200 ppm`), and an extreme sub-zero exterior environment (`-45°C` winds, polar night).

- **Organism & Assay Scope:** Comprehensive multi-crop CEA portfolio across `12` space crop species/cultivars grown over a `9-month` continuous overwintering mission (February–November 2018, `268 days` operation):
  - *Lactuca sativa* (Lettuce, cv. 'Outredgeous', 'Waldmann's Green', 'Flandria')
  - *Brassica rapa* subsp. *nipposinica* (Mizuna mustard)
  - *Solanum lycopersicum* (Dwarf tomato, cv. 'Red Robin')
  - *Cucumis sativus* (Cucumber, cv. 'Picowell')
  - *Raphanus sativus* (Radish, cv. 'Raxe')
  - *Eruca vesicaria* subsp. *sativa* (Arugula / Rocket)
  - Culinary & medicinal herbs: *Ocimum basilicum* (Basil), *Petroselinum crispum* (Parsley), *Allium schoenoprasum* (Chives)
  Assays: Continuous biomass production tracking (fresh vs. dry edible weight, harvest index), electrical energy consumption per kilogram of edible biomass, crop water uptake and transpiration recycling efficiency, light use efficiency (LUE), and crew psychological/hedonic acceptance surveys during Antarctic isolation.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Overwintering Garden Vanguard (The EDEN ISS Squad):**
  - **Cucumber 'Picowell' (The Biomass Giant):** High-yielding, vertical vining powerhouse producing crisp fruit every 3 days; demands intense water flux and heavy potassium feeding.
  - **Dwarf Tomato 'Red Robin' (The Ruby Jewel):** Compact determinate bush loaded with sweet, antioxidant-packed cherry tomatoes, thriving under far-red photon supplementation.
  - **Mizuna & Arugula (The Rapid Cut-and-Come-Again Squad):** High-velocity green leaf biomass engines providing weekly fresh salad harvests for the isolated 10-person overwintering crew.
  - **Lettuce 'Waldmann's Green' & 'Outredgeous':** Reliable leafy anchors of the controlled-environment multilevel racks.

- **The Polar Operator (Paul Zabel / DLR Overwinterer):** The sole human technician embedded inside the Antarctic ice shelf, managing pollination, daily crop audits, nutrient adjustments, and harvesting while blizzards rage outside.

- **The Mobile Test Facility (FEG Citadel):** A double-hull, hermetically insulated container facility standing on steel stilts above the drifting Antarctic snow. Its internal air-circulation and LED engines maintain an oasis of `21°C` while the polar outside plummets to `-45°C`.

- **Spectral Light Vanguard (Water-Cooled Heliospectra LED Arrays):** Water-jacketed lighting fixtures radiating precisely tuned photon ratios (`Red:Blue:Far-Red = 6:2:1`) while heat-recovery exchangers capture waste thermal energy to warm the habitat.

- **Panel 1:** Day 20: The first green explosion. The greenhouse canopy closes as leafy greens fill the illuminated racks.

  - **Data Overlay:** Real-time energy and mass balance vectors: Water transpiration loop recycling `98%` of evaporated moisture back into the clean condensate tank; electrical meters tracking `41 kWh/day` lighting draw.

- **Caption:** *Outside, life is impossible. Inside, high-efficiency LEDs turn water and electricity into crisp, life-saving nutrition. This is the prototype of a Martian greenhouse.*

**Page 9**

- **Panel 1:** March 2018: The Cucumbers Awaken! The vining shoots of *Cucumis sativus* cv. 'Picowell' reach the ceiling gantries.

- **Panel 2:** Manual pollination: Paul moves between the giant yellow blossoms with a fine horsehair brush, transferring pollen from male flowers to female ovaries.

**Page 10**

- **Panel 1:** Day 60: The cucumber surge. Long, dark-green mini-cucumbers swell rapidly, accumulating water at over `100 grams` per fruit every 48 hours!

- **Panel 2:** The Neumayer overwintering crew gathers in the galley. Fresh cucumber slices and tomato wedges served on steel dinner trays. Smiling faces—the first fresh crunch in two months of frozen rations.

### Act II: The Overwintering Crucible (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11**

- **Panel 1:** Mid-winter blizzard: May 2018. Wind speeds surpass `120 km/h`. The bridge between the main station and EDEN ISS is sealed by whiteout conditions.

- **Panel 2:** Remote operation mode activated! Mission control at the German Aerospace Center (DLR) in Bremen takes direct telemetry control of the FEG across satellite links, adjusting light schedules and watering cycles autonomously.

**Page 12**

- **Panel 1:** Inside the roots: Aeroponic and deep-flow root compartments. Nutrient mist nozzles pulsing every `15 minutes`.

- **Panel 2:** Root health monitoring: Snow-white root mats of cucumber and tomato fill the troughs without root rot or anaerobic stagnation.

**Page 13**

- **Panel 1:** Day 120: Light Recipe Tuning. Testing Far-Red photon supplementation (`730 nm`) on *Red Robin* dwarf tomatoes.

- **Panel 2:** Phytochrome photo-equilibrium (`PSS`) shifts, triggering shade-avoidance allocation that directs more assimilates into fruit swelling rather than excessive stem lengthening.

**Page 14**

- **Panel 1:** The Pick-and-Eat Routine: Weekly harvest records.
  - Lettuce: Continuous single-head replacement.
  - Mizuna & Swiss Chard: Cut-and-come-again harvesting every 7 days from the same root base, tripling the cumulative biomass yield per square meter.

**Page 15**

- **Panel 1:** Day 150: The Humidity Challenge. Transpiration from `3.68 m²` of dense canopy generates up to `25 liters` of water vapor daily!

- **Panel 2:** Cold-plate dehumidifiers condense the vapor into ultra-pure liquid, cycling it through UV sterilizers directly back into the nutrient preparation tanks.

**Page 16**

- **Panel 1:** Pathogen surveillance: Routine microbiological swabs across leaves, air ducts, and nutrient lines.

- **Panel 2:** Petri dishes reveal zero *Salmonella*, zero *E. coli*, and zero phytopathogenic fungi. The biocontainment barrier of the Antarctica Citadel remains completely unbreached.

**Page 17**

- **Panel 1:** The Strawberry setback: Testing *Fragaria* plants in Rack 4. High humidity and low airflow stall flowering.

- **Panel 2:** Paul logs the lessons: Some crops require higher vapor-pressure deficit (`VPD`) and active shaking to achieve full fruit set. The greenhouse adapts.

**Page 18**

- **Panel 1:** August 2018: Peak polar night. Crew psychological evaluation surveys.

- **Panel 2:** Psychological scores peak on days following time spent inside the greenhouse. The green light, humidity, and plant scents counteract the oppressive monotony of the sensory-deprived white continent.

- **Dialogue (Crew Doctor):** "It is not just calories. Entering the greenhouse restores our circadian rhythm, lifts our mood, and reminds us of living Earth."

**Page 19**

- **Panel 1:** Cumulative harvest milestone: The digital scale ticks past `150 kilograms` of fresh produce!

- **Panel 2:** High-yield star: *Picowell* cucumber breaks all records, producing over `67 kg` of edible fruit from just four root clusters.

**Page 20**

- **Panel 1:** Nutrient solution recovery audit. Over `90%` of dissolved minerals are retained within the plant tissue or recirculating fluid.

- **Panel 2:** Closed-loop efficiency equations validated: Proving that life support systems can minimize resupply mass to near-zero for three-year lunar or Martian stays.

**Page 21**

- **Panel 1:** Day 220: Spring approaches. The first sliver of sunlight breaks over the Antarctic horizon after months of darkness.

- **Panel 2:** Inside EDEN ISS, rows of ripe red tomatoes, purple radish roots, and vibrant green basil leaves gleam under the LED racks.

**Page 22**

- **Panel 1:** Final harvest tally: Over `268 kilograms` of fresh vegetables harvested across 268 days—averaging over `1 kilogram` of fresh food every single day for the overwintering crew.
