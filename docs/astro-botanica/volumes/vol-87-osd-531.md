## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Endodermal Amyloplast Fleet:** Starch-dense statoliths inside the endodermal sheath cells of the flowering stem. When the stem is tilted horizontally, they tumble downward across the vacuolar membrane.
- **The Immediate-Early Indicator Brigade (`SAUR19`, `SAUR20`, `SAUR24`):** Small Auxin Up-Regulated RNA genes that ignite within 15 minutes exclusively on the lower stem flank, activating plasma membrane H⁺-ATPases to drive acid growth.
- **The Proton Pumps (`AHA1`, `AHA2`):** Hydrogen ion motors pumping protons into the apoplast, dropping the cell wall pH from 6.0 to 4.8.
- **The Acid Wall Looseners (`EXPA1`, `EXPA8`, `XTH4`):** Expansin proteins that disrupt hydrogen bonds between cellulose microfibrils and xyloglucan chains under acidic conditions, allowing turgor pressure to expand the lower flank cells.
- **The Repressor Feedback Loop (`IAA19`, `IAA29`):** Auxin/IAA transcriptional repressors that surge at 60 minutes to brake the bending motion and prevent overshooting the vertical target.
- **Dr. Patrick H. Masson & Dr. Chun-Teh Tan:** Space biologists decoding the fastest genetic response to gravity known in the plant kingdom.

### Act II: The Immediate-Early Gene Ledger (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- RNA-Seq analysis reveals 312 "Immediate-Early Indicator Genes."
- Top upregulated genes on the lower flank: *SAUR19* (`+6.2 log2FC`), *SAUR20* (`+5.8 log2FC`), *IAA19* (`+4.9 log2FC`), *TCH4* (`+4.1 log2FC`).
- In contrast, the upper flank shows downregulation or static expression of growth genes, halting upper tissue elongation.

**Page 16–20**
- Comparison with spaceflight clinostat samples.
- On a 2D clinostat, the endodermal statoliths roll continuously in circles; the *SAUR* gene burst is completely scrambled, firing symmetrically across the entire circumference.
- The stem grows in strange, irregular corkscrew shapes because it cannot resolve a clear directional vector!

### Act III: The Planetary Angle (Pages 21–32)

**Page 21–25**

**Page 26–30**
