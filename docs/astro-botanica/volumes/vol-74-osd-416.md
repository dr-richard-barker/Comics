## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*
- **Volume:** 74 (Special Chronicles: Illustrated Chromatin Edition)
- **Chapter Title:** *The Epigenetic Imprint: Bisulfite Methylomes in Orbit*
- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-416` (*Characterizing Epigenetic Changes in Methylation Mutants (elp2-5 and met1-7) in Response to Spaceflight. [Bisulfite-Seq]*)
- **Primary Publication:** Paul AL, Haveman N, Califar B, Ferl RJ. (2021). *Epigenomic Regulators Elongator Complex Subunit 2 and Methyltransferase 1 Differentially Condition the Spaceflight Response in Arabidopsis.* *Frontiers in Plant Science*, `12:691790` (DOI: `10.3389/fpls.2021.691790`, PMID: `34589093`).
- **Setting & Hardware:** International Space Station (ISS) — **APEX04-EPEX** (*Advanced Plant Experiment 04 — Epigenetic Expression*) flown aboard **Veggie** flight growth hardware (`10 cm × 10 cm` vertical sterile Phytagel plates, continuous LED lighting, `n = 48` verified biological samples across Flight vs. Ground Control in the KSC Orbital Environmental Simulator [OES]). Fixed on orbit into KSC Fixation Tubes (KFTs) with `RNAlater` and deep-frozen in MELFI (`-80°C`).
- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* — Wild-Type **Columbia-0 (`Col-0`)** vs. **`elp2-5`** (Elongator Complex Subunit 2 histone acetyltransferase/epigenomic mutant) vs. **`met1-7`** (DNA Methyltransferase 1 CG-methylation mutant), dissected into **Roots** and **Leaves** (`48` verified samples). Whole-Genome Bisulfite Sequencing (WGBS / Illumina) mapping single-base 5-methylcytosine (`5mCyt`) across **CG**, **CHG**, and **CHH** sequence contexts.

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **The Master Methylator (`MET1`, *METHYLTRANSFERASE 1*):** Primary maintenance DNA methyltransferase responsible for preserving symmetrical CG cytosine methylation across gene bodies and transposons. When lost in `met1-7`, spaceflight triggers distinct stress-response rewiring.
- **The Chromatin Remodeler (`ELP2`, *ELONGATOR COMPLEX SUBUNIT 2*):** Epigenomic histone-acetyltransferase regulator essential for terrestrial pathogen-defense signaling. In `elp2-5`, seedlings fail to mount normal spaceflight root growth and transcriptional conditioning.
- **Squad `Col-0` (Wild-Type Benchmark):** Reference *Arabidopsis* seedlings demonstrating organ-specific DNA methylome remodeling—diverging sharply between orbital leaves and roots.
- **Squad `elp2-5` (The Epigenetically Locked):** Mutant seedlings showing stunted, severely compromised development in orbit compared to terrestrial controls, proving `ELP2` is indispensable for spaceflight physiological acclimation.
- **Squad `met1-7` (The Demethylated Pioneer):** Mutant seedlings with globally depleted CG methylation that nevertheless mount surprisingly robust orbital survival through alternative non-CG (CHG/CHH) pathways.
- **The 5-Methylcytosine Marks (`5mCyt` — CG, CHG, CHH):** Chemical methyl flags decorating cytosine bases; acting as a dynamic second layer of instruction above the DNA sequence.
- **Dr. Anna-Lisa Paul, Dr. Robert J. Ferl, Natasha Haveman & Brandon Califar:** University of Florida spaceflight epigenomics pioneers executing `APEX04-EPEX` aboard the ISS.

- **Dialogue (Brandon Califar):** "Bisulfite conversion efficiency above ninety-nine percent. Single-base methylomes for all forty-eight samples are streaming in!"
