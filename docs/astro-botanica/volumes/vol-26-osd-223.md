## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles* — **Volume 26**

- **Title:** *The Fractional Gradient*

- **OSDR / GeneLab Accession:** `OSD-223` / `GLDS-223` (NASA OSDR | Data.gov) | DOI: `10.26030/wdtz-v612`

- **Chronology Note:** `OSD-221` (unlisted/non-plant) and `OSD-222` (`STS-135` mouse cerebellum transcriptomics) are skipped; Volume 26 advances from `OSD-220` (Vol. 25, `SJ-10` methylome) to `OSD-223` (*Plant Signaling in Microgravity*).

- **Primary Publications:** Sheppard et al. (2021), *Life* 11(10):1010, DOI `10.3390/life11101010` (PMC8539686); Land et al. (2024), *Front. Plant Sci.* 14:1308713, DOI `10.3389/fpls.2023.1308713` (PMC10800490)

- **Mission & Vehicles:** Launched aboard **STS-135** (Space Shuttle *Atlantis*, **July 2011**); operated on the **International Space Station (`ISS`)**; returned frozen aboard **SpaceX Dragon** (**March 2013**)

- **Hardware:** **European Modular Cultivation System (`EMCS`)** with twin centrifuge rotors (`0g` stationary vs. fractional-`g` spinning) and `TROPI`-like seed cassettes (`27` seeds/membrane); ground reference in **ERM-2** at NASA Ames Research Center (`ARC`) and `OVT` in **ERM-1** at `N-USOC` (Trondheim, Norway)

- **Organism & Genotypes:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* (`Col-0` wild type and transgenic **`InsP 5-ptase`** lines with compromised `InsP₃` signaling)

- **Assay & Sequencing:** `RNA-Seq` (`n = 40` samples in `OSD-223`), `RNAqueous Micro kit` (`RIN > 9.5`), `NEBNext Poly(A)` ultra-directional libraries on **3 lanes of Illumina HiSeq 2500 (`125 bp` single-end)**

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Dr. Imara Y. Perera (Principal Investigator):** Research Professor at North Carolina State University (with Co-Is Christopher Brown & Heike Winter-Sederoff), investigating how lipid-derived **`InsP₃`** second-messenger signaling and altered gravity reshape *Arabidopsis* transcription (NASA NLSP).

- **James Sheppard & Eric S. Land (Lead Analysts):** NCSU researchers who mapped the root fractional-gravity continuum (`0g` to `0.88g`) in PMC8539686 and paired `Plant Signaling` (`PS`, `OSD-223`) with `Plant RNA Regulation` (`PRR`, `OSD-437`) shoots in PMC10800490.

- **Dr. Colleen J. Doherty & Tiffany A. Toennisson (Co-Investigators):** NCSU molecular biochemists who co-led motif/network analysis and hypergravity (`6.5× g`) validation of heat-shock targets.

- **The `EMCS` Twin Rotors:** Hardware inside the ISS Columbus module that spun `4` Experimental Containers (`ECs`, `5` seed cassettes each) at a radial gradient (`0.53g`, `0.65g`, `0.76g`, `0.88g`, `1.0g`) while holding the second rotor stationary at `μg`.

- **`InsP 5-ptase` Transgenics:** *Arabidopsis* plants constitutively expressing mammalian type I inositol polyphosphate 5-phosphatase, hydrolyzing `InsP₃` to probe gravity-to-`Ca²⁺` signal transduction.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: The Last Shuttle and the Twin Rotors (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 (Splash Page):** Full-bleed dawn liftoff of **STS-135** (*Atlantis*)—the final Space Shuttle launch in **July 2011**—carrying `80` dry-mounted `TROPI`-like seed cassettes toward the ISS (PMC8539686). **Caption:** *"Volume 25 caught 60 hours of weightlessness inside a free-flying satellite. Now, aboard the final Shuttle, 2,160 dormant seeds ride into orbit to test a sharper instrument: a centrifuge built to dial gravity by tenths."*

**Page 2 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* `35 days` pre-launch at NASA Ames Research Center: Caroline Smith and the NCSU team screen `Col-0` and `InsP 5-ptase` seeds (`≥99%` germination) and mount `27` seeds per black-gridded `PES` membrane (`PALL 65561`) with guar gum, micropyles pointing away from the LEDs.

- *Panel 2:* Close-up of the blotter sandwich: `Whatman #3` / `17 CHR` paper pre-soaked in `0.5× MS` (`pH 5.7`, no sucrose), sealed with `3M` aluminum foil tape.

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Perera):** *"Hydrolyzing `InsP₃` leaves the plants looking normal on Earth—until you tilt them or deny them water. In orbit, we'll see what `InsP₃` actually buys a seedling."*

- *Panel 4:* Engineering Reference Module (`ERM-1`) in Trondheim, Norway (`N-USOC`) and `ERM-2` at NASA ARC: preflight Schedule and Operations Verification Tests (`OVT`/`EVT`).

**Page 3 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* Inside the ISS Columbus module, an astronaut slides `4` Experimental Containers (`20` cassettes per rotor) into the **European Modular Cultivation System (`EMCS`)**.

- *Panel 2:* Remote hydration triggers from the ground. Both rotors spin at `1g` for the first **`48 hours`**, giving every seedling an identical initial gravitational cue.

- *Panel 3:* At `48 h`, Rotor A halts (`μg`), while Rotor B holds its spin—creating a **five-step fractional-gravity ladder** across each EC's five cassette slots: **`0.53g` → `0.65g` → `0.76g` → `0.88g` → `1.0g`** (PMC8539686).

**Page 4 (5-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Telemetry readout inside `EMCS`: continuous white LED light, **`24 °C`**, `~20–21% O₂`, ethylene scrubbing, and `CO₂` rising from `~370 ppm` post-purge to `~760 ppm`.

- *Panel 2:* `6-hour` camera downlinks show roots oriented uniformly down the cassette grid thanks to the initial `48 h` `1g` cue (PMC10800490).

- *Panel 3:* Day `5` harvest: the spinning rotor is pulled first, cassettes are transferred straight into the ISS **`MELFI` (`-80 °C`)** freezer, and the `μg` rotor follows.

- *Panel 4:* **March 2013** — **SpaceX Dragon** splashes down, returning the frozen cassettes at cryogenic temperature.

- *Panel 5:* Cold-platform dissection in Raleigh: `RNAlater®` is flooded over each thawed cassette as roots and shoots are separated (`RNAqueous Micro kit`, **`RIN > 9.5`**).

**Page 5 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Quality-control reality check: Position 5 (`1.0g` outer edge) cassettes dried out in several ECs and are excluded from the root gradient model.

- *Panel 2:* The remaining root archive locks in cleanly: **`16` `μg` replicates** vs. **`4` replicates each** at **`0.53g`**, **`0.65g`**, and **`0.88g`** (with `0.76g` reserved for paired `1g`-control analyses).

- *Panel 3:* NCSU Genomic Sciences Laboratory loads **`3` lanes of Illumina HiSeq 2500 (`125 bp` single-end)** across `40` `OSD-223` samples.

- *Panel 4:* `HiSat2` alignment hits a **`97%` mapping rate** against `TAIR10`/`Araport11`, followed by `svaseq` batch correction and `DESeq2`.

**Page 6 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* James Sheppard runs `DESeq2` treating **Gravity as a continuous variable** (`padj < 0.001`), flagging `382` candidates and curating **`101` fractional-`g` responsive genes** (PMC8539686).

- *Panel 2:* Six distinct dose-response curves emerge across `μg`, `0.53g`, `0.65g`, and `0.88g`: **linear up**, **linear down**, **peak up**, **peak down**, **threshold up**, and **threshold down**.

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Sheppard):** *"Genes don't just flip on or off at zero gravity. Some climb linearly toward 1g, some spike at 0.65g, and some hit a plateau the moment 0.53g arrives."*

**Page 7 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Transcription factor curves (`Fig. 3`): **`NFYA1`**, **`SPL6`**, and **`TCP7`** climb steadily with increasing gravity.

- *Panel 2:* **`HM2`** drops linearly as gravity rises, while **`GATA9`** and **`ANAC009` (`FEZ`)** plunge at `0.53g` and plateau through `0.88g`—a **threshold-down** switch.

- *Panel 3:* `20` TFs and coactivators lead the `101`-gene list (`13` rising with `g`, `7` falling with `g`), including root-cap regulator `ANAC009` and lateral-root gravistimulation responder `MYB53`.

- *Panel 4:* **Dialogue (Doherty):** *"Even half a g—nearly Martian to lunar-plus—is enough to trip the `GATA9` and `ANAC009` threshold."*

**Page 8 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* **Heat Shock Proteins (`9` of `101` genes):** `HSP70`, `HSP90.1`, `HSP101`, `HSFA3`, and `DNAJ` (`ATJ10`) all **increase** with increasing `g`, while small chaperone `HSP15.4` peaks in `μg`.

- *Panel 2:* Ground validation (`6.5× g` hypergravity for `4 h` in the dark): qRT-PCR bars shoot past **`> 9-fold`** for `HSP70`, `HSP90.1`, `HSP101`, and `DNAJ` (PMC8539686).

- *Panel 3:* **Caption:** *"Touch didn't trigger `HSP70`, `HSP90.1`, or `HSP101` in prior studies—only gravity did. From microgravity to 6.5g, chaperones track mechanical load."*

**Page 9 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Cell Wall & Defense (`17` and `14` genes):** Pectin lyases (`At3g59850`, `At3g61490`) and pectin acetylesterases (`PAE7`, `PAE12`) peak in **`μg`** and drop as `g` rises—loosening primary walls when weight vanishes.

- *Panel 2:* Meanwhile, **`10` of `12` defense genes**—including `Ca²⁺` channels `CNGC19` and `OSCA1.3`, `CRK11`, `SGT1A`, and `ICS2`—are **lowest in `μg`** and climb with `g` (`CML8` moves opposite).

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Perera):** *"In microgravity, roots soften their pectin walls and quiet their immune sensors. That leaves space crops vulnerable if opportunistic pathogens turn virulent in orbit."*

- *Panel 4:* Auxin & transport markers (`PILS5`, `NDL2`, `DAO2`) rise with `g`, bridging root sensing to hormone flow.

**Page 10 (2-Panel Act Break):**

- *Panel 1:* Split-frame: root cassettes on the left, **shoot tissues** from `PS` (`OSD-223`) and its successor **`PRR` (`OSD-437`)** on the right.

- *Panel 2:* Title card overlay: **ACT II: THE `1g` ALIBI IN THE SHOOTS**.

### Act II: The `1g` Alibi in the Shoots (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Eric Land compares wild-type shoot `RNA-Seq` from **`PS` (`OSD-223`, `0.76g` onboard control)** and **`PRR` (`OSD-437`, `1.0g` onboard control)** (PMC10800490).

- *Panel 2:* Individual `DESeq2` (`padj < 0.05`) yields **`1,819` DEGs** in `PS` (`510` up, `1,309` down in `μg`) and **`1,792` DEGs** in `PRR` (`599` up, `1,193` down).

- *Panel 3:* Hypergeometric overlap test: **`178` shared upregulated** (`p = 9.97 × 10⁻¹⁸⁵`) and **`399` shared downregulated** (`p = 1.63 × 10⁻²⁷²`) genes.

- *Panel 4:* Combined `PS + PRR` re-analysis (`padj < 0.01`) locks in **`1,112` upregulated** and **`1,429` downregulated** microgravity genes.

**Page 12 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Upregulated in `μg` (`1,112` genes):** **`159` transcription factors** (`SPCH`, `WOX3`, `TCP22`, `GATA2`, `HFR1`, `CRF4`, `CRF5`, `ANT`) driving shoot meristem, leaf growth, and photomorphogenesis.

- *Panel 2:* **Auxin surge (`38+` genes):** `PIN3`, `PIN4`, `TIR1`, `AFB2`, `AFB3`, `6` `ARFs` (`ARF1/4/6/16/17/19`), and **`11` `SAURs`** (`9` induced `> 2-fold`, led by `SAUR76` at `log₂FC = 3.14` and `SAUR13` at `2.11`).

- *Panel 3:* **Vesicle & Golgi transport (`50` genes):** Coat protein (`COP`) complex and `Sec23/24` genes light up (`FDR = 3.0 × 10⁻¹⁰`), alongside `7` epigenetic regulators (`5` SET-domain histone methyltransferases, including `3` `SUVH` genes).

- *Panel 4:* Gravitropism genes **`SGR5` (`IDD15`)**, **`IDD14`**, and **`LAZY1`** are also upregulated in `μg`, even with no gravity vector to sediment statoliths.

**Page 13 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* `MEME` motif scan across `500 bp` upstream promoters of the `1,112` `μg`-upregulated genes (`Fig. 3` of PMC10800490).

- *Panel 2:* A razor-sharp consensus emerges: **`5'-GGCCCA-3'`** (**Site II / `Up1` element**, `E = 3.7 × 10⁻¹²`), appearing **`258` times in `174` genes** (`15.6%`) and peaking at `-70 bp` from the transcription start site.

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Land):** *"Site II is the binding signature of `TCP` transcription factors—and `TCP22` itself is upregulated in microgravity."*

**Page 14 (5-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Downregulated in `μg` (`1,429` genes):** A massive dampening of stress programs (`GO:0006950`, `FDR = 4.1 × 10⁻³¹`).

- *Panel 2:* **`120` stress TFs** drop, led by **`15` `ERFs`** (including `7` `DREBs`/`CBF2`), **`12` `WRKYs`** (`WRKY18/40/60`), `4` heat-stress TFs, and `CAMTA2/3/4`.

- *Panel 3:* **`~200` biotic defense genes** and **`~140` abiotic/ABA stress genes** (including `9` of `21` hierarchical ABA-network TFs) fall together.

- *Panel 4:* **`~40` `Ca²⁺` signaling genes** (`CBL1`, `CML24`, `CML37`, `CPK4/15/28/29/32`, `CNGC1/4`, `ACA1`, `KIC`, `KRP1`) are suppressed in `μg`.

- *Panel 5:* **`~100` cell wall genes** (`XTHs`, expansins, peroxidases, `6` dirigent proteins) and **`~300` plastid/chloroplast genes** (`PSI/PSII`, `LHCA`, `NADPH` subunits) are downregulated.

**Page 15 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **The `HY5` Master Switch:** `HY5` (Elongated Hypocotyl 5) targets are significantly enriched in **both** `μg`-upregulated (`p = 2.45 × 10⁻¹¹`, organ development/auxin) and `μg`-downregulated (`p = 1.70 × 10⁻¹²⁰`, stress) gene sets (PMC10800490).

- *Panel 2:* **Natural Antisense Transcripts (`NATs`):** `42` `NATs` up and `30` `NATs` down in `μg`; `29` of the `42` upregulated `NATs` inversely track repressed sense genes (`MYB44`, chloroplast & defense loci).

- *Panel 3:* **microRNAs (`PRR` sRNA-seq):** `miR319c`, `miR863-5p`, and `miR398b-3p` rise in `μg`, repressing targets—`38%` of which localize to the plastid.

- *Panel 4:* Meanwhile, `miR156f-3p` and `miR5654-3p` rise in `1g`, repressing `SPL9` and developmental phase-transition targets.

**Page 16 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* **The Paradox (`Fig. 5` & `Fig. 6`):** Why did `BRIC-19` and other ground-controlled missions report stress and defense genes *upregulated* in space, while `PS` and `PRR` show them *downregulated* in `μg`?

- *Panel 2:* Land adds the **`ERM-2` ground reference controls** to the heatmap alongside onboard `1g` and `μg`.

- *Panel 3:* **The Reveal:** Stress and salt/defense genes are **upregulated in onboard `1g` relative to Earth (`ERM-2`)**, yet **specifically suppressed in `μg` relative to onboard `1g`**!

**Page 17 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Dialogue (Perera):** *"Every plant on the ISS feels radiation, altered gas, and zero convection—that's why onboard `1g` turns on stress genes. But weightlessness itself masks that stress induction."*

- *Panel 2:* Cross-check against `EMCS` `Seedling Growth` (`SG2/SG3`): `54` genes are commonly downregulated in `μg` and `143` in low-`g` (`< 0.1g`) vs. `1g` (PMC10800490).

- *Panel 3:* Contrast with `BRIC-19`: `70` genes reported as upregulated in `BRIC-19` (vs. ground) are actually *downregulated* in `μg` (vs. onboard `1g`).

- *Panel 4:* **Caption:** *"Without an orbital centrifuge, spaceflight stress and microgravity wear the same mask."*

**Page 18 (3-Panel Sequence):**

### Right Spread (Page 32) — Root Fractional-`g` & Shoot `1g`-Control Findings

- **Roots Across Fractional Gravity (PMC8539686):**

  - `16` `μg` vs. `4` reps each at `0.53g`, `0.65g`, `0.88g` → **`101` fractional-`g` DEGs** (`padj < 0.001` curated from `382`) in `6` dose-response shapes.

  - **Key Groups:** `20` TFs (`WOX4`, `SPL6`, `TCP7`, `NFYA1` up with `g`; `HM2`, `ANAC009`, `GATA9` down with `g`), `9` HSPs (`HSP70`, `HSP90.1`, `HSP101`, `DNAJ` up with `g` and **`> 9-fold`** at `6.5× g`), `14` defense (`CNGC19`, `OSCA1.3` up with `g`), `17` cell wall (pectin lyases, `PAE7/12` highest in `μg`).

- **Shoots: `PS` (`OSD-223`) + `PRR` (`OSD-437`) (PMC10800490):**

  - `PS` WT shoots: **`1,819` DEGs**; combined `PS + PRR`: **`1,112` up** & **`1,429` down** in `μg` vs. onboard `1g`.

  - **Up in `μg`:** `159` TFs (`TCP22`, `SPCH`), `38+` auxin (`PIN3/4`, `TIR1`, `11` `SAURs`), `50` vesicle/Golgi, `SGR5`/`LAZY1`, **Site II (`5'-GGCCCA-3'`)** motif (`258` hits / `174` genes).

  - **Down in `μg`:** `120` stress TFs (`15` `ERFs`, `12` `WRKYs`), `~200` biotic defense, `~140` abiotic/ABA, `~40` `Ca²⁺` (`CBL1`, `CPKs`), `~300` plastid genes, `miR319c`/`miR863-5p`/`miR398b-3p` targets.

  - **Core Insight:** Stress genes are **up in onboard `1g` vs. Earth** (`ERM-2`), but **down in `μg` vs. onboard `1g`**—proving `μg` masks spaceflight stress.
