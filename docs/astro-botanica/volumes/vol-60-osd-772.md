## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Dr. Christina L. M. Khodadad & Dr. Anirudha R. Dixit (Co-Corresponding Authors, Noetic Strategies / AETOS):** Lead the microbial food-safety clearance and `QIIME2`/`DADA2` `16S` community dissection across `Q1–Q4` tissues, swabs, foam, wicks, and water.

- **Dr. Gioia D. Massa, Matthew W. Romeyn & Jacob J. Torres (KSC & NMSU `PH-04` Architects):** Conceptualize `PH-04` and down-select `'NuMex Española Improved'` (a Hatch Big Jim × Española cross) from over two dozen pepper cultivars—guided by Torres, an Española, New Mexico native.

- **Mary Hummerick, LaShelle Spencer, Oscar Monje, Lucie Poulet, Nicole DuFour, Jeffrey Richards, Cory Spern, Jennifer Gooden, Aaron Curry & Raymond Wheeler:** The KSC/Redwire/Sierra Space tele-operations, horticulture, and analytical team tuning `40%` blue light, `550 ppm` CO₂, and `APH` moisture for `137` days from Earth.

- **The Orbital Pollination & Harvest Crew (Megan McArthur, Mark Vande Hei, Shane Kimbrough, Kayla Barron, Thomas Pesquet):** Tend the `4` pepper plants in `Kibo`—thinning at Day `28`, installing support stands at Day `50`, hand-pollinating blossoms, and turning half the `26`-fruit harvest into orbital space tacos.

- **The `'NuMex Española Improved'` Plants (`Q1–Q4`):** Four independently watered root zones (`990 g` arcillite each) kept compact (`< 40 cm`) by `40%` blue light while enduring `137` days of closed-loop microgravity.

- **The `14`-Taxon Core & ISS Water Guild (`Sphingomonas`, `Novosphingobium`, `Pseudomonas`, `Puia`, `Cupriavidus`, `Burkholderia`):** Microbes traced across sanitized seeds, `Q1–Q4` roots, stems, fruits, and `SC` hardware—proving vertical/horizontal niche exchange while leaving the edible peppers pathogen-free.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Forty Percent Blue (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1**

- **Panel 1 (Full-width establishing shot):** Inside the `Kibo` module of the International Space Station, the **Advanced Plant Habitat (`APH`)** hums quietly—an enclosed, `180+`-sensor climate vault built for crops that take months, not weeks.

- **Panel 2 (Close-up, April 2021 at KSC):** Gloved hands sieve `1–2 mm` `Turface Proleague Elite` arcillite, autoclaved at `121°C` and oven-dried at `70°C` for `72` hours. Four `990 g` (`1.6 L`) quadrants of the **PH-04 Science Carrier (`SC`)** fill with ceramic grains and four timed-release `Nutricote` blends (`T70`, `T100`, `T180`).

- **Caption (Narrator):** *Leafy greens were sprints. Chile peppers—germination, flowering, pollination, fruit set, ripening—are a four-and-a-half-month endurance test.*

- **Panel 3 (Detail):** Chlorine-gas-sanitized seeds (`0.5 mL HCl + 30 mL bleach`, `1 h`) of *Capsicum annuum* cv. `'NuMex Española Improved'`—`12` per quadrant (`48` total)—sit anchored in guar gum.

**Page 2**

- **Panel 1 (Launch & Installation):** June 3, 2021: `SpaceX CRS-22` roars off Pad `39A`. July 12, 2021: the Flight Engineer slides the `PH-04` Science Carrier into `APH` and imbibes the four quadrants until saturated.

- **Panel 2 (Two-shot, KSC Tele-Op Console):** Gioia Massa and Matthew Romeyn watch `APH` sensor telemetry stream down to Kennedy Space Center, alongside Jacob Torres from New Mexico State University.

- **Jacob Torres:** "More than two dozen pepper varieties tested, and `'NuMex Española Improved'` won—`2,000` to `4,000` Scoville units, compact stature, packed with vitamins `C` and `K`."

**Page 3**

- **Panel 1 (Interior LED schematic):** Overhead `APH` LEDs lock onto `300 µmol/m²/s` (`16 h` light / `8 h` dark) across three pre-set canopy heights (`2.54`, `15.2`, and `35.6 cm`).

- **Panel 2 (Spectral callout):** **`27%` red, `33%` green, `40%` blue**.

- **Raymond Wheeler:** "`40%` blue light shortens the internodes—keeping the pepper plants under `40` centimeters so they don't smash into the `45`-centimeter `APH` ceiling."

- **Panel 3 (Atmosphere readout):** `23°C` air, `550 ppm` CO₂ to suppress leaf intumescence, and a `Purafil` potassium-permanganate scrubber stripping ethylene out of the closed loop.

**Page 4**

- **Panel 1 (Day 14 → Day 28):** Relative humidity steps down from `80%` to `50%`, and root-zone moisture drops from `50%` to `40–45%`. On Day `28`, an astronaut opens the `APH` door and thins each quadrant down to **one** dominant plant (`20 plants/m²`).

- **Panel 2 (Day 50):** Support stands slide into the `SC` to brace the thickening stems as `APH` fans pulse to `0.9 m/s` for `5` minutes a day between `45` and `70` days after initiation (`DAI`) to shake pollen loose from the first white blossoms.

**Page 5**

- **Panel 1 (Medium shot, `Kibo`):** Megan McArthur and Thomas Pesquet gently hand-pollinate individual *Capsicum* flowers in microgravity—replacing Earth's wind and bees with human fingertips.

- **Panel 2 (Calendar tension, Day 100 → Day 120):** Originally scheduled for harvests on Days `100` and `120`, fruiting lags in microgravity. On the KSC console, Oscar Monje and Lucie Poulet extend the mission clock past Day `120`.

- **Gioia Massa:** "Fruiting takes its own time in space. Extend `PH-04` to `137` days."

**Page 6**

- **Panel 1 (Day 109 & Day 137 Harvests):** Green and glossy crimson peppers hang heavy from all four quadrants. Crew members Mark Vande Hei, Shane Kimbrough, and Kayla Barron snip **`26` edible chile peppers** across the two harvests.

- **Panel 2 (Celebration beat):** Half of the peppers go straight into spaceflight tacos—crisp, aromatic heat breaking months of thermostabilized rations. The remaining half wrap in foil and vanish into `-80°C` `MELFI` freezers.

**Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread Anchor: *Dissecting the Four-Quadrant Habitat*)**

- **Left Page (Page 7) — *January 2022, KSC Return (`OSD-772` / `GLDS-675`)*:** The ambient-stowed `PH-04` Science Carrier opens on the aseptic KSC bench. Christina Khodadad, Mary Hummerick, and LaShelle Spencer lift the top cover and foam layer (`Fig. 2B`), gently teasing pepper roots out of `1–2 mm` arcillite and shaking loose grains free to isolate `109` samples across **12 niches**: `stems (4)`, `root-shoot junctions (4)`, `distal roots (12)`, `rhizospheres (4)`, `bulk substrates (16)`, `wicks (16)`, `foams (12)`, `surface swabs (8)`, `leaves (4)`, `fruits (10)`, `seeds (10)`, and `water (3)` (Q1 water was dry on return).

- **Right Page (Page 8) — *Dual-Track Pipeline (`TSA`/`IMA` + `16S` `MiSeq`)*:** Half of each sample hits `OMNI` bead-ruptors (`4 mm` glass beads, `5 m/s`) and serial dilutions on `TSA`, `IMA`, `3M Petrifilm`, and `RV`/Tetrathionate *Salmonella* broths. The other `100 mg` hits liquid-nitrogen `CryoMill` (`30 Hz`, `2 min`), `DNeasy UltraClean Microbial` extraction, `V4` `16S` PCR (`Kozich` primers), and `Illumina MiSeq` (`2 × 250 bp`, `QIIME2`/`DADA2`/`SILVA 138`).

- **Spread Caption:** *Four isolated root zones. One shared cabin air loop. Every pepper, wick, and foam pad tells us how microbes travel through a 137-day fruiting habitat.*

**Page 9**

- **Panel 1 (Food-safety readout):** On `TSA` and `IMA`, the `PH-04` pepper fruits register from below detection limits up to just **`111 CFU/g` fresh weight** on a single Q3 pepper.

- **Panel 2 (Pathogen check):** *Escherichia coli*, *Staphylococcus aureus*, and *Salmonella* screens come back **zero across every fruit**.

- **Mary Hummerick:** "Even after `137` days in a closed chamber, the peppers are cleaner than grocery-store produce."

**Page 10**

- **Panel 1 (Science Carrier plate counts):** Beneath the canopy, the hardware tells a busier story: `SC` surface swabs average `3.2 × 10⁵` to `4.0 × 10⁴ CFU/swab` (bacteria) and `1.2 × 10⁵` to `4.9 × 10⁴ CFU/swab` (fungi), while `Q1` substrate and wicks carry significantly higher bacterial counts than `Q2–Q4` (`p ≤ 0.015`).

- **Panel 2 (Isolate IDs):** `Biolog` and `MicroSeq` identify culturable `Sphingomonas sanguinis`, `S. leidyi`, `Roseomonas cervicalis`, `Phyllobacterium`, `Cupriavidus gilardii`, `C. campinensis`, `Burkholderia contaminans`, and fungi `Phialemoniopsis curvata`, `Aspergillus`, `Exophiala`, `Purpureocillium`, and `Penicillium`.

### Act II: Fourteen in Common (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11**

- **Panel 1 (Alpha diversity plots):** Anirudha Dixit displays the Shannon diversity (`H`) table from `QIIME2`. Above-surface tissues are sparse: seed, leaf, and fruit `H` all sit **`< 1.0`** (pepper fruit `0.44–0.51`, `46–194` species).

- **Panel 2 (Below-surface contrast):** Below ground, rhizosphere Shannon diversity climbs to **`1.6–2.1`** (`386–436` species—nearly double the leaves and fruits).

- **Anirudha Dixit:** "The plant filters its own canopy. Roots host the crowd; fruits stay quiet."

**Page 12**

- **Panel 1 (`Bray-Curtis` PCoA plots):** Three `PCoA` ordinations (`Adonis` `p = 0.001`) show that **tissue type** drives variation above surface, while **sample location** (wick vs. foam vs. root vs. water) drives variation below surface and across `SC` components.

- **Panel 2 (Heatmaps, `ampvis2`):** Above surface (`Fig. 6`), **`Sphingomonas`**, **`Novosphingobium`**, and **`Pseudomonas`** dominate (`> 10%` each, reaching nearly `50%` in `Q3` stems)—yet `Pseudomonas` vanishes from `Q3`/`Q4` roots and is completely absent from `SC` water.

**Page 13**

- **Panel 1 (Below-surface heatmap, `Fig. 7`):** In the roots, rhizosphere, and arcillite, **`Puia`** (a chitin- and cellulose-degrading member of the `Chitinophaga` group) emerges as a top taxon (`0–10%`) alongside `Sphingomonas`, `Pseudomonas`, `Burkholderia`, `Ralstonia`, `Cellulomonas`, and `Cupriavidus`.

- **Panel 2 (Hardware heatmap, `Fig. 8`):** On `SC` swabs, `Pseudomonas` hits up to `50%`, while `Leifsonia` and `Methylobacterium` peak on the wicks.

**Page 14**

- **Panel 1 (Four-way Venn diagram, `Fig. 9`):** Four overlapping ellipses—*Above Surface*, *Below Surface*, *SC Components*, and *T0 Seeds*—converge on a tiny center: **`13` named genera + `1` uncultured bacterium** (`14` total, just **`4.5%`** of the community), while `> 95%` of taxa stay niche-specific.

- **Christina Khodadad:** "Sanitized seeds still carry endophytes like `Sphingomonas`—and they meet ISS potable-water regulars like `Burkholderia`, `Ralstonia`, and `Cupriavidus`."

**Page 15**

- **Panel 1 (Niche-bridge schematic):** Arrows trace two-way microbial traffic: ISS cabin air deposits `Sphingomonas` on leaves; watering carries `Novosphingobium` and `Cupriavidus` down into wicks and roots; xylem and vertical transmission carry seed endophytes all the way into progeny pepper seeds.

- **Panel 2 (Caveat note):** Christina Khodadad adds a transparent note: the `SC` sat at ambient ISS temperature from November 2021 to January 2022 before Earth return, allowing post-harvest shifts in the tray components—unlike the `-80°C` `MELFI`-frozen peppers.

**Page 16**

- **Panel 1 (Wide KSC review):** The full `PH-04` team gathers around the `GLDS-675` (`OSD-772`) release dashboard (`7.55 GB`, `DOI: 10.26030/mjvk-v435`, published in *Scientific Reports* `16:12863`).

- **Panel 2 (Milestone banner):** `137` days. `26` chile peppers. Zero foodborne pathogens. `APH` officially validated for long-duration fruiting crops in microgravity.

**Page 17**

- **Panel 1 (Quiet beat, KSC high bay):** Matthew Romeyn and LaShelle Spencer walk past an `APH` ground unit. Inside, the `1–2 mm` arcillite bed looks solid and heavy.

- **Matthew Romeyn:** "`PH-04` proved we can grow fruiting peppers in arcillite for `137` days. But arcillite is single-use ceramic—heavy to launch, messy to dispose of, and it holds `40–45%` water by weight."

**Page 18**

- **Panel 1 (Flash forward):** Beside the `APH` rack sits a transparent root chamber—no clay, no rockwool, no soil. Roots hang bare in mid-air while mist nozzles and ebb-and-flow troughs pulse nutrient solution across them.

- **Caption (Narrator):** *To scale orbital farming beyond `26` peppers, NASA has to cut the substrate loose altogether—and test whether naked roots can survive microgravity.*

### Act III: Cutting the Clay Loose (Pages 19–30)

**Page 19**

- **Panel 1 (Hardware reveal):** The **Exposed Root On Orbit Test System (`XROOTS`)**, integrated directly into the ISS **Veggie** hardware—an **aeroponic and hydroponic** technology demonstration using an ebb/flow nutrient solution recovery loop.

- **Panel 2 (Sample table preview):** `n = 156` samples—plants, hardware swabs, and recirculating water—processed through the exact same `Khodadad et al., 2020` `16S` `V4` and `Zymo Quick-ITS Plus` fungal `ITS` `MiSeq` pipelines (`70 µm` cell strainers, `13,000 × g` pellets).

**Page 20**

- **Panel 1 (Contrast panel):**

  - Left: `PH-04` (`OSD-772`) — `990 g` arcillite per quadrant, `137` days, *Capsicum annuum* `'NuMex Española Improved'`, `26` peppers.

  - Right: `XROOTS` (`OSD-773`) — zero solid substrate, aeroponic/hydroponic nutrient recovery inside Veggie, `16S` + `ITS` food-safety & water-loop microbiome.

**Page 21**

- **Panel 1 (KSC archive lock):** `OSD-772` Version 2 locks its `109`-sample `16S` `MiSeq` dataset into OSDR (`29-Jan-2026`), closing the chapter on the first space-grown chile peppers.

**Page 22**

- **Panel 1 (Detail):** A single dried `'NuMex Española Improved'` seed from a space-grown Q1 pepper sits in a sterile vial—carrying vertically transmitted `Sphingomonas` from its `137`-day orbital parent.

**Page 23**

- **Panel 1 (Visual transition):** The crimson curve of a Hatch chile pepper dissolves into a web of bare white roots suspended inside a clear `XROOTS` chamber, droplets of nutrient mist clinging to root hairs in `0 g`.

**Page 24**

- **Panel 1 (Microgravity fluid physics):** In Earth gravity, mist falls and water drains. In `0 g`, surface tension wraps bare roots in thick liquid films—risking hypoxia unless ebb-and-flow recovery pulls the solution back.

**Page 25**

- **Panel 1 (Microbiology link):** On Earth (`OSD-723`), we saw how unbuffered hydroponic water amplifies `Cupriavidus` and `Sphingomonas` biofilms. In `XROOTS` (`OSD-773`), that same water loop runs in orbit.

**Page 26**

- **Panel 1 (Lab bench):** `70 µm` cell strainers filter plant debris out of `XROOTS` water and swab extracts, spinning `13,000 × g` pellets for dual `16S` (`V2 500-cycle`) and `ITS` (`V3 500-cycle`) `MiSeq` sequencing.

**Page 27**

- **Panel 1 (Crew perspective):** An astronaut inspects bare roots glowing pink under the Veggie LEDs—no dark pillows hiding what's growing beneath the canopy.

**Page 28**

- **Panel 1 (Data bridge):** `OSD-772` (`PH-04`) → `OSD-773` (`XROOTS`): from `137` days in solid arcillite to the first aeroponic/hydroponic root system in Veggie.

**Pages 29–30 (Cliffhanger Double-Page Spread: *Next Station — `OSD-773`*)**

- **Left Page (Page 29):** Inside the ISS Veggie rack, bare white plant roots hang suspended in clear `XROOTS` chambers under pink LEDs—mist droplets floating like tiny planets around the root hairs.

- **Right Page (Page 30):** Analytical telemetry overlay for **`OSD-773`**: aeroponic + hydroponic ebb/flow recovery, `n = 156` plant/swab/water samples, `16S` (`V2 500-cycle`) + `ITS` (`V3 500-cycle`) `Illumina MiSeq` food-safety & microbiome profiling.

- **Spread Caption:** *We harvested `26` chile peppers from four trays of clay. Next, we pull the clay away—and watch bare roots drink from mid-air in orbit.*

- **Cliffhanger Tag:** **NEXT VOLUME: `OSD-773` — *Exposed Root On Orbit Test System (XROOTS), an aeroponic and hydroponic plant growth system technical demonstration on the ISS*.**
