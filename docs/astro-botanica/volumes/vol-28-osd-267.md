## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles* — **Volume 28**

- **Title:** *The Outredgeous Harvest*

- **OSDR / GeneLab Accessions:** `OSD-267` / `GLDS-267` (`VEG-01A`, DOI `10.26030/0cpc-g985`, NASA OSDR), `OSD-268` / `GLDS-268` (`VEG-01B`, NASA OSDR), and `OSD-269` / `GLDS-269` (`VEG-03A`, NASA OSDR) *(chemistry companion: `OSD-745`)*

- **Chronology Note:** `OSD-252` through `OSD-266` are skipped (non-vascular/non-plant studies, including ISS *Fusarium oxysporum* isolates `OSD-264` and a ground *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii* Veggie selection study `OSD-265`); Volume 28 advances from `OSD-251` (Vol. 27, `EMCS` *Seedling Growth*) to the ISS **`VEG-01A` / `VEG-01B` / `VEG-03A`** *Outredgeous* red romaine lettuce trilogy.

- **Primary Publication:** Khodadad et al. (2020), *Frontiers in Plant Science* 11:199, DOI `10.3389/fpls.2020.00199` (PMC7067979 | PubMed 32210992)

- **Mission & Timeline:** Veggie launched on **SpaceX CRS-3** (`April 2014`); **`VEG-01A`** (`May 8–June 10, 2014`, `33 d`), **`VEG-01B`** (`July 8–August 10, 2015`, `33 d`), **`VEG-03A`** (`October 25–December 28, 2016`, `64 d` sequential "cut-and-come-again" harvests); ground controls at Kennedy Space Center (`KSC`, `24–72 h` delayed)

- **Hardware:** **Vegetable Production System (`Veggie`)** (ORBITEC / Sierra Nevada Corp., Madison, WI) with extensible transparent bellows, `12R:3B:1G` LED array (`~200 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹`, `16 h/8 h`), and `6` Kevlar/Nomex **plant pillows** (calcined clay/arcillite + `Nutricote 18-6-8`)

- **Organism:** *Lactuca sativa* cv. `'Outredgeous'` (red romaine lettuce) + phyllosphere and rhizosphere microbiota

- **Assays:** Culture-based food safety (`TSA`, `IMA`, `Petrifilm`, `Biolog`/`MicroSEQ`), `16S V4` & `ITS` amplicon sequencing on **Illumina MiSeq (`V2 500-cycle`)**, `ICP-OES` elemental analysis, total anthocyanins, total phenolics, and `ORAC-FL` antioxidant capacity

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Dr. Gioia D. Massa & Dr. Raymond M. Wheeler (NASA KSC Leads):** NASA Kennedy Space Center plant physiologists (with Matthew W. Romeyn & Trent M. Smith) who validated Veggie on orbit and led the food-safety and nutritional assessment of ISS salad crops (PMC7067979).

- **Christina L. M. Khodadad & Mary E. Hummerick (Lead Microbiologists):** KSC LASSO/AECOM researchers who ran heterotrophic plate counts, pathogen screens, `Biolog`/`MicroSEQ` isolate IDs, and `16S`/`ITS` MiSeq community profiling.

- **LaShelle E. Spencer, Anirudha R. Dixit & Jeffrey T. Richards (Chemistry & Data Team):** KSC researchers who quantified leaf elements (`ICP-OES`), anthocyanins, total phenolics, and `ORAC-FL` antioxidant capacity.

- **ISS Astronaut Farmers (Steve Swanson, Scott Kelly, Kjell Lindgren, Shane Kimbrough):** Crew members who hydrated plant pillows, manually syringed water during dry spells, and harvested—and tasted—the first NASA space-grown salad (`VEG-01B`, **August 10, 2015**).

- **`Outredgeous` Red Romaine & Its Microbiome:** Leafy crop hosting `Proteobacteria` (`Burkholderia`, `Ralstonia`, `Janthinobacterium`), `Chitinophaga`, and `Ascomycota` (`Microidium`, `Alternaria`, `Phaeoacremonium`) across leaves and roots.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Six Pillows and a Red-Blue Glow (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 (Splash Page):** Full-bleed interior of the International Space Station (`ISS`), where **Veggie** glows vivid magenta (`12` red `630 nm` : `3` blue `455 nm` : `1` green `530 nm` LEDs) inside its transparent extensible bellows, framing six lush heads of `'Outredgeous'` red romaine lettuce (PMC7067979). **Caption:** *"For twenty-seven volumes, plants in orbit were microscopic seedlings fixed in RNAlater or frozen for RNA. Now, in Veggie, NASA grows a crop meant for a fork."*

**Page 2 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Kennedy Space Center clean bench: Gioia Massa and team pack `6` Teflon-coated black **Kevlar** plant pillows with **Nomex** bottoms, porous calcined clay (**arcillite**), and `Nutricote 18-6-8` controlled-release fertilizer.

- *Panel 2:* Two surface-sanitized `'Outredgeous'` lettuce seeds are glued with guar between paired germination wicks and heat-sealed dry in gas-impermeable `Tedlar` bags.

- *Panel 3:* **April 2014 (`SpaceX CRS-3`):** Veggie—built by ORBITEC in Madison, Wisconsin—launches to the ISS.

- *Panel 4:* **Dialogue (Massa):** *"Veggie has no air filtration beyond particle screens. Whatever microbes float in ISS cabin air or water will meet these leaves."*

**Page 3 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* **May 8, 2014 (`VEG-01A`):** Astronaut Steve Swanson unzips the pillows, injects water onto the root mat, and sets the `200 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹` LEDs (`16 h` light / `8 h` dark).

- *Panel 2:* `Day 7`: seedlings are thinned to `1` plant per pillow. Early low-water periods force crew and ground teams to troubleshoot manual syringe watering in microgravity.

- *Panel 3:* **June 10, 2014 (`Day 33`):** Sanitized scissors snip the first crop; foil-wrapped leaves and `2` rooting pillows go straight into `-80 °C` **`MELFI`** for Earth return.

**Page 4 (5-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **July 8, 2015 (`VEG-01B`):** Second planting begins; manual pillow watering by crew keeps the crop thriving.

- *Panel 2:* **August 10, 2015 (`Day 33`):** Historic moment—astronauts **Scott Kelly** and **Kjell Lindgren** harvest `'Outredgeous'` leaves, sanitize them, and take the first bite of NASA space-grown salad (`~`half eaten, half frozen for `OSD-268`).

- *Panel 3:* **October 25, 2016 (`VEG-03A`):** Third planting tests **"cut-and-come-again"** sequential harvesting (`4` harvests at Days `38`, `45`, `59`, and `64`, ending **December 28, 2016**).

- *Panel 4:* Astronaut **Shane Kimbrough** eats harvests `1`, `3`, and half of `4`, freezing harvest `2` (`Dec 9`) and the rest of harvest `4` (`Dec 28`, `OSD-269`).

- *Panel 5:* Parallel ground controls at `KSC` Environmental Growth Chambers (`24 h` delayed for `VEG-01`, `72 h` delayed for `VEG-03A`) mirror ISS temperature, humidity, and `CO₂`.

**Page 5 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Hardware Mystery (`VEG-03A`):** HOBO data loggers show elevated temperature and humidity inside Veggie during the first `22 days`, with excess moisture pooling on germinating seedlings.

- *Panel 2:* Ground engineers trace the culprit: whenever Veggie's power is cycled, the fan restarts displaying **"low"** while actually remaining **off**—the same anomaly that plagued an earlier Zinnia test (`VEG-01C`)!

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Wheeler):** *"In weightlessness, without a working fan, transpired water clings to the leaf surface—a paradise for fungi."*

- *Panel 4:* Samples arrive at KSC `-80 °C` freezers, ready for the dual test: **Is it safe to eat?** and **Is it nutritious?**

**Page 6 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* Christina Khodadad and Mary Hummerick weigh frozen leaf and thawed root-pillow samples into sterile `50 mL` tubes with `PBS` and glass beads, shaking vigorously for `2 min`.

- *Panel 2:* Serial dilutions hit **`TSA`** (`48 h`, bacteria) and **`IMA`** (`72–120 h`, fungi) plates, plus `3M Petrifilm` (*E. coli*/coliform, *S. aureus*) and `BPW`/`RV` *Salmonella* enrichment broths.

- *Panel 3:* **Result (`Fig. 2`):** Leaf bacterial counts average **`3.53` (`VEG-01A`)**, **`4.86` (`VEG-01B`)**, **`2.89` (`VEG-03A` 2nd)**, and **`2.14` (`VEG-03A` 4th) `log₁₀ CFU/g`**—equal to or *lower* than supermarket lettuce (`4–8 log₁₀ CFU/g`).

**Page 7 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Pathogen screen readout: **`0` *E. coli***, **`0` *Salmonella* sp.**, **`0` *Staphylococcus aureus*** across every flight and ground leaf sample (PMC7067979).

- *Panel 2:* Fungal counts (`2.3–4.3 log₁₀ CFU/g`) stay within normal terrestrial ranges—though in `VEG-03A` flight leaves, fungal counts jump significantly from harvest `2` to harvest `4` (`P = 0.0002`).

- *Panel 3:* Root bacterial counts (`10⁶–10⁸ CFU/g`) are orders of magnitude richer than leaves, with `VEG-03A` roots lower than `VEG-01A/B`.

- *Panel 4:* **Dialogue (Hummerick):** *"All three crops pass food safety. Even after 64 days of cut-and-come-again harvesting, no human pathogens colonized the salad."*

**Page 8 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* Cultured colonies go to `Biolog Micro ID` and `ABI 3130` `MicroSEQ` sequencing: **`14` bacterial** and **`9` fungal** genera isolated from leaves; **`19` bacterial** and **`6` fungal** genera from roots.

- *Panel 2:* Cross-referencing against ISS environmental surveys (`Venkateswaran et al., 2017`): **`> 50%`** of bacterial genera and **`100%`** of fungal genera (`Aspergillus`, `Penicillium`, `Rhodotorula`) match known ISS surface and water flora!

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Khodadad):** *"Culture plates only catch ~10% of the community. To see the other 90%, we sequence the `16S V4` and `ITS` barcodes."*

**Page 9 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* `Qiagen Microbial Cell DNA` extraction → dual-indexed `16S V4` & `ITS` amplicons → **Illumina MiSeq (`V2 500-cycle`, `10% Phi-X`)**.

- *Panel 2:* `RDP GreenGenes` & `UNITE` taxonomic pipelines resolve the hidden microbiome (`OSD-267`, `OSD-268`, `OSD-269`).

- *Panel 3:* Shannon alpha diversity (`Table 6`): **roots (`1.96–2.66` flight)** are far more diverse than **leaves (`0.351–0.8` flight, `P < 0.05`)**.

- *Panel 4:* Only `VEG-03A` leaves show a significant flight vs. ground alpha-diversity difference (`P = 0.01`), and `Bray-Curtis` beta diversity shows **no significant global split** (`P > 0.05`).

**Page 10 (2-Panel Act Break):**

- *Panel 1:* Split-frame: microscopic `Burkholderia` and `Alternaria` filaments clinging to a red romaine leaf vein.

- *Panel 2:* Title card overlay: **ACT II: THE PHYLLOSPHERE, THE PILLOW, AND THE IONOME**.

### Act II: The Phyllosphere, the Pillow, and the Ionome (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Bacterial Community (`Fig. 4`):** `Proteobacteria` dominate every sample (`80–96%` in `VEG-01A/B`; `59%` in `VEG-03A` flight roots and `76%` in `VEG-03A` flight leaves), followed by `Bacteroidetes`, `Actinobacteria`, and `Chloroflexi`.

- *Panel 2:* Top `Proteobacteria` genera: **`Burkholderia`**, **`Ralstonia`**, and **`Janthinobacterium`**—known residents of ISS potable water systems.

- *Panel 3:* Nitrogen-fixing `Azospirillum`, `Herbaspirillum`, `Bradyrhizobium`, and `Mesorhizobium` surge in `VEG-03A` roots, likely responding to nutrient depletion over `64 days`.

- *Panel 4:* Gram-positive **`Thermogemmatispora`** (`Chloroflexi`) dominates leaf tissues across all three tests, while **`Chitinophaga`** (`Bacteroidetes`) thrives in roots.

**Page 12 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Fungal Community (`Fig. 5`):** `Ascomycota` makes up **`92–99%`** of fungal reads (`Basidiomycota ≤ 7%`).

- *Panel 2:* Top three fungal genera: **`Microidium`** (an obligate biotroph on leaves), **`Alternaria`** (saprophyte), and **`Phaeoacremonium`** (plant endophyte), plus `Rhodosporidiobolus`.

- *Panel 3:* `Fusarium` appears only in `VEG-01B` (higher in ground controls), and `Staphylococcus`—a human-associated transient—is cultured from both flight and ground `VEG-03A` leaves.

- *Panel 4:* **Dialogue (Massa):** *"The plants carry their own seed-borne `Thermogemmatispora`, drink ISS water's `Ralstonia`, and share air with the crew. Space agriculture is an ecosystem."*

**Page 13 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* LaShelle Spencer and Jeffrey Richards move to the chemistry bench: freeze-dried lettuce leaves are ground in an `IKA Tekmar A10` mill and acid-digested (`EPA 3050B`, `70% HNO₃` + `30% H₂O₂` at `95 °C`).

- *Panel 2:* **`iCAP 7000` `ICP-OES`** measures mineral nutrition (`Table 7`): **within** each experiment, flight and ground leaves show **no significant elemental difference**.

- *Panel 3:* **Across** the three plantings, however, **`VEG-03A`** has significantly lower **`Fe`** (`P ≤ 0.001`) and **`K`** (`P ≤ 0.0001`), while **`VEG-01B`** has higher **`Na`**, **`P`**, **`S`**, and **`Zn`** (`Ca`, `Mg`, `Mn` unchanged).

**Page 14 (5-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* `Dionex ASE 350` automated solvent extraction (`MWA` at `100 °C` for anthocyanins; `AWA` at `80 °C` for phenolics & `ORAC`).

- *Panel 2:* Spectrophotometer (`530/650 nm`) vs. cyanidin-3-glucoside standards: **total anthocyanins** show **no significant difference** across all three tests (`Table 8`).

- *Panel 3:* `BioTek Synergy` microplate reader runs the 2-hour **`ORAC-FL`** peroxyl-radical decay assay: **antioxidant capacity (`ORAC`) shows no significant variance** across flight or ground.

- *Panel 4:* Modified Folin-Ciocalteu assay (`gallic acid` equivalents): **`VEG-03A`** total phenolics (`15.0` ground, `19.6` flight `mg/g` DW) are significantly lower than `VEG-01A/B` (`P ≤ 0.001`).

- *Panel 5:* **Dialogue (Spencer):** *"Sixty-four days of sequential harvesting pushed `VEG-03A` toward reproductive senescence—which naturally draws down leaf potassium, iron, and phenolics."*

**Page 15 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Connecting water stress to phenolics: early under-watering (`VEG-01A/B`) vs. early fan-off over-watering (`VEG-03A`) both nudge secondary metabolism.

- *Panel 2:* Nutritional reality check: space-grown `'Outredgeous'` lettuce delivers fresh potassium, iron, and antioxidants without losing anthocyanins or `ORAC` activity.

- *Panel 3:* Food-safety benchmark comparison: NASA's non-thermostabilized food limit (`< 2 × 10⁴ CFU/g`) was written for freeze-dried Earth rations, not living salad crops—making `VEG-01`/`03` the new empirical baseline.

- *Panel 4:* **Caption:** *"Every bite Kjell Lindgren and Scott Kelly took on August 10, 2015, was backed by five years of microbiology and ionomics."*

**Page 16 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* Data deposition into NASA GeneLab / OSDR: **`GLDS-267` (`VEG-01A`)**, **`GLDS-268` (`VEG-01B`)**, and **`GLDS-269` (`VEG-03A`)** released **February 21, 2020** (PMC7067979).

- *Panel 2:* OSDR standardizes `16S` and `ITS` MiSeq reads via `NF_AmpIllumina` (`cutadapt`, `FastQC`, `MultiQC`, `ANCOM-BC`, `DESeq2`), and later archives the chemistry tables in **`OSD-745`**.

- *Panel 3:* **Caption:** *"From the first Veggie pillows to open-access microbiome baselines—fresh food in orbit had passed its first clinical trial."*

**Page 17 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* Looking back across the series: `Vol. 26` (`OSD-223`) and `Vol. 27` (`OSD-251`) used `EMCS` to dissect *Arabidopsis* gravity thresholds.

- *Panel 2:* `Vol. 28` (`OSD-267–269`) shifts the lens to **crop production** and **host–microbe ecology** in `Veggie`.

- *Panel 3:* **Dialogue (Romeyn):** *"In `VEG-01`, we proved Veggie can grow safe lettuce. Next, we have to grow multiple crops side by side in two Veggie units—and sample every wick, pillow, and surface."*

- *Panel 4:* Preview of the next `Veggie` wave (`VEG-03D/E/F`: red romaine, mizuna mustard, and Waldmann's green leaf lettuce) and `APEX` / `APH` studies ahead in OSDR.

**Page 18 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* Diagram of the **`Veggie` fan-restart bug**: power cycle → UI reads `"low"` → fan stays `0 RPM` → boundary layer moisture rises → `Microidium`/`Alternaria` risk climbs.

- *Panel 2:* Operational fix: crew checklists updated to verify actual airflow after every power cycle.

- *Panel 3:* **Caption:** *"In microgravity, a silent fan is a biological experiment."*

**Pages 19–22 (4-Page Epilogue Montage — 1 Panel per Page):**

- *Page 19:* Scott Kelly and Kjell Lindgren clinking fresh red romaine leaves in the ISS cupola/galley on **August 10, 2015**.

- *Page 20:* A `Shannon` diversity bar chart contrasting lush root microbiomes (`2.46`) against lean leaf communities (`0.52`).

- *Page 21:* Cut-and-come-again `VEG-03A` lettuce crowns regrowing for a fourth harvest at `Day 64`.

- *Page 22:* Two `Veggie` bellows glowing side-by-side in orbit, awaiting the next OSDR plant volume.

### Act III: Synthesis & Mission Debrief (Pages 23–32)

**Page 23 (4-Panel Grid):**

- *Panel 1:* **Food Safety Verdict:** Zero *E. coli*, *Salmonella* sp., or *S. aureus* across `VEG-01A`, `VEG-01B`, and `VEG-03A`; leaf aerobic counts (`2.14–4.86 log₁₀ CFU/g`) are at or below terrestrial market greens.

- *Panel 2:* **Microbiome Geography:** Roots (`Shannon 1.96–2.66`) are far more diverse than leaves (`0.351–0.8`), with `> 50%` of bacterial and `100%` of fungal isolates matching ISS surface/water flora.

- *Panel 3:* **Nutritional Stability:** Anthocyanins and `ORAC` antioxidant capacity hold steady between space and Earth; `VEG-03A` multi-harvest senescence lowers `Fe`, `K`, and total phenolics.

- *Panel 4:* **Hardware Lesson:** Manual microgravity watering and the `Veggie` fan-restart `"low"` bug directly shape leaf moisture and fungal load.

**Page 24 (3-Panel Sequence):**

- *Panel 1:* **Cut-and-Come-Again Trade-off:** Sequential harvesting (`VEG-03A`, `64 d`) multiplies fresh crew servings per pillow, but drives fungal buildup by harvest `4` (`P = 0.0002`) and shifts leaf diversity (`P = 0.01`).

- *Panel 2:* **Nitrogen-Fixer Signal:** `Bradyrhizobium`, `Mesorhizobium`, `Azospirillum`, and `Herbaspirillum` rise in `64 d` roots as `Nutricote` nutrients deplete.

- *Panel 3:* **Seed-to-Table Baseline:** `OSD-267–269` establishes the microbiological benchmark for all subsequent ISS `Veggie` and `APH` leafy-green crops.

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

### Left Spread (Page 31) — Mission, Hardware & Three-Harvest Timeline

- **Top Banner:** `OSD-267 (VEG-01A)` | `OSD-268 (VEG-01B)` | `OSD-269 (VEG-03A)` | **Paper:** Khodadad et al., 2020 (PMC7067979)

- **Three ISS Plantings (`Lactuca sativa` cv. `'Outredgeous'`):**

  - **`VEG-01A` (`OSD-267`):** `May 8–Jun 10, 2014` (`33 d`, single harvest).

  - **`VEG-01B` (`OSD-268`):** `Jul 8–Aug 10, 2015` (`33 d`, single harvest; **first NASA space salad eaten on orbit**).

  - **`VEG-03A` (`OSD-269`):** `Oct 25–Dec 28, 2016` (`64 d`, `4` sequential harvests; `2nd` [`Dec 9`] & `4th` [`Dec 28`] returned).

- **Hardware & Ground Controls:** `Veggie` (`SpaceX CRS-3`, `12R:3B:1G` LEDs `~200 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹`, `16 h/8 h`), `6` Kevlar/Nomex pillows (arcillite + `Nutricote 18-6-8`); `KSC` ground chambers (`24–72 h` delayed); `-80 °C` `MELFI` storage.

### Right Spread (Page 32) — Microbiology, Sequencing & Nutrition

- **Food Safety & Plate Counts:**

  - **Pathogens:** **Negative** for *E. coli*, *Salmonella* sp., and *S. aureus* across all crops.

  - **Leaf Bacteria:** `3.53` (`VEG-01A`), `4.86` (`VEG-01B`), `2.89` & `2.14` (`VEG-03A` 2nd/4th) `log₁₀ CFU/g`; **Fungi:** `2.3–4.3 log₁₀ CFU/g` (`4th > 2nd` in `VEG-03A`, `P = 0.0002`); **Roots:** `10⁶–10⁸ CFU/g`.

  - **Isolates:** `14` bacterial & `9` fungal leaf genera; `19` bacterial & `6` fungal root genera (`> 50%` bacteria & `100%` fungi match ISS surfaces).

- **16S (`V4`) & ITS MiSeq Profiling:**

  - **Alpha Diversity (`Shannon`):** Roots (`1.96–2.66` flight) > Leaves (`0.351–0.8` flight, `P < 0.05`); only `VEG-03A` leaves differ flight vs. ground (`P = 0.01`); **no `Bray-Curtis` beta-diversity difference**.

  - **Top Taxa:** `Proteobacteria` (`59–96%`: `Burkholderia`, `Ralstonia`, `Janthinobacterium`, `Bradyrhizobium`), `Thermogemmatispora`, `Chitinophaga`; `Ascomycota` (`92–99%`: `Microidium`, `Alternaria`, `Phaeoacremonium`).

- **Ionome & Antioxidants (`ICP-OES`, `ASE 350`, `ORAC-FL`):**

  - No flight vs. ground difference *within* any run; across runs, `VEG-03A` lower in `Fe`, `K`, and total phenolics (`15.0`/`19.6 mg/g` DW); `Ca`, `Mg`, `Mn`, anthocyanins, and `ORAC` unchanged.
