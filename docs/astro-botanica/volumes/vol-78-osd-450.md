### Act II: The Phospho-Switch Mechanism (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- Molecular cutaway of the nuclear pore complex.
- Why does 14-3-3κ translocate?
- In hypergravity, stretch-activated calcium channels activate calcium-dependent protein kinases (*CPKs*). These kinases phosphorylate transcription factors and membrane proteins, creating specific phosphoserine binding motifs (`RXXpS/pTXP`).
- 14-3-3κ binds these phosphorylated motifs like a molecular clamp, escorting them across the nuclear membrane or holding them in an active signaling state.

**Page 16–20**
- Fast-freeze RNA-Seq taken during the exact seconds of translocation.
- Upregulation of ion transporter regulators (*CIPK1*, *CBL1*) and rapid alteration in *AHA2* proton pump activity.
- The 14-3-3 translocation is the master switch that couples mechanical gravitational force to cellular electrochemical physiology!
