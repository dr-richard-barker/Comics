Grown in `4` replicate `48-well` culture plates (`12.5 mm` × `15 mm` wells) with drilled drainage holes, `10 × 15 mm` **Rockwool** plugs (`~7 mm` compressed) + `13 mm` `0.45 μm` nylon filters, subsurface-irrigated daily with `0.125× MS` (`pH 5.7`), inside vented terrarium chambers (`22°C`, `16:8` light:dark LEDs). Each well held **`900 mg`** (`<1 mm` sieved) of **Apollo 11** (`10084`), **Apollo 12** (`12070`), **Apollo 17** (`70051`), or **`JSC-1A`** lunar simulant. Thinned to `1` seedling on Day `6–8`; aerial tissue harvested at Day `20` (`JSC-1A`) / `21` (lunar samples) into liquid nitrogen (`-80°C`).

- **Organism & Tissue:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* `Col-0` wild-type (`TAIR CS70000`) aboveground biomass (leaves and hypocotyl).

- **Assay & Platform:** `n = 20` RNA-Seq samples (`JSC-1A` `n = 8` [`4` primary plate-matched controls + `4` additional replicates], Apollo 11 `n = 4` [`3` viable for DEG analysis], Apollo 12 `n = 4`, Apollo 17 `n = 4`) + daily **ImageJ** morphometric photography (`Canon EOS 5D Mark IV`). `RNeasy Plant Mini Kit`, `50 ng` RNA, `NEBNext Poly(A)` + `NEBNext Ultra II Directional` (`12` PCR cycles), `FAB` blocking, `Illumina NovaSeq 6000` (`S4` lane, `2 × 151` PE; `57.67M–65.47M` reads, `0.41%–7.25%` rRNA). GeneLab `GL-DPPD-7101-G` (`NF_RCP v2.1.0`, `STAR v2.7.11b`, `RSEM v1.3.3`, `DESeq2 v1.46.0`); paper pipeline: `Trimmomatic`, `STAR`, `RSEM`, `edgeR` (`FDR < 0.05`, `>2`-fold, `FPKM > 0`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Apollo 11 (`10084`, The Mature Mare Dust):** High-titanium (`5–7 wt.% TiO₂`), heavily space-weathered regolith rich in micrometeorite agglutinates (`30–52 vol.%` across lunar samples) and nanophase metallic iron (`Fe⁰`). Hydrophobic at first touch, it triggered the harshest plant response (`465` DEGs, one non-surviving replicate).

- **Apollo 12 (`12070`, The Submature Dust):** Also hydrophobic until actively stirred with `0.125× MS` nutrient solution; yielded an intermediate `265` DEGs.

- **Apollo 17 (`70051`, The Immature Dust):** Shorter lunar surface exposure, high-`Ti` (`5–7 wt.% TiO₂`), yet wetted cleanly by capillary action like `JSC-1A`—producing the mildest site-level transcriptome (`113` DEGs).

- **`JSC-1A` (The Terrestrial Mirror):** Volcanic-glass lunar simulant (`1.85 wt.% TiO₂`, containing `Fe³⁺` magnetite/chromite rather than lunar `Fe⁰` nanophase iron). Sixteen uniform, fast-growing rosettes set the baseline.

- **Anna-Lisa Paul, Stephen M. Elardo, & Robert Ferl (UF):** Plant molecular biologists teamed with a planetary geologist to design `900 mg` Rockwool-wicked micro-pots, winning `12 × 1 g` Apollo allocations from NASA's curator after fifty years of lunar quarantine.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Nine Hundred Milligrams of the Moon (Pages 1–10)

- **Page 1:** `1969–1972` archival flashback: Apollo astronauts in quarantine, Lunar Receiving Laboratory gloveboxes, and Walkinshaw's early tests sprinkling lunar fines onto leaves—never once letting a seed root *inside* real Moon dust.

- **Page 2:** `2021`, Gainesville. Four `1 g` vials each from **Apollo 11** (`10084`), **Apollo 12** (`12070`), and **Apollo 17** (`70051`) sit on the bench. Narration: *To earn Moon dust, you first prove you can grow a plant in less than a gram.*

- **Page 3:** The engineering of the `48-well` micro-pot (`PMC9098553` Figure 1): each `12.5 mm` well is drilled for drainage, plugged with a `10 × 15 mm` **Rockwool** cylinder (`~7 mm` compressed, spun basalt fiber that could be made on the Moon), and capped with a `0.45 μm` nylon filter to keep precious regolith from washing away.

- **Page 4:** Calibration flashback: `0.125× MS` (`pH 5.7`) beat plain water, which stalled `JSC-1A` seedlings at their first true leaves. Now `900 mg` of `<1 mm` sieved regolith fills each designated well (`1` well per Apollo site + `4` `JSC-1A` wells per plate, across `4` plates).

- **Page 5:** First surprise at hydration: `JSC-1A` and **Apollo 17** (`70051`) drink up `0.125× MS` by smooth capillary action—but **Apollo 11** (`10084`) and **Apollo 12** (`12070`) bead up **hydrophobically**, forcing the team to stir the nutrient solution directly into the dust.

- **Page 6:** `Col-0` seeds (`2–4` per well) are pipetted onto the wet surface, and the `4` plates enter clear, vented terrarium boxes (`22°C`, `16:8` LEDs) simulating a human-occupied lunar habitat rather than a sealed sterile chamber.

- **Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread Anchor):** **Day 2 to Day 20 — Germination Easy, Thriving Hard.** Top-down daily `Canon EOS 5D Mark IV` time-lapse (`PMC9098553` Figures 2–3). At `48–60 h`, **nearly `100%` of seeds germinate** on all three Apollo regoliths with normal cotyledons. On Days `6–8`, extra seedlings are pulled with forceps—revealing **stunted lunar roots**. By Day `20`, all `16` `JSC-1A` rosettes spread `8–10` lush green leaves, while lunar plants lag behind, some turning tiny, distorted, and deep reddish-black.

- **Page 9:** Harvest at Day `20`/`21`: aerial rosettes are snipped at soil level and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen (`-80°C`). One Apollo 11 plant fails to survive, leaving `n = 3` for Apollo 11 and `n = 4` each for Apollo 12, Apollo 17, and primary `JSC-1A` controls (`+4` extra `JSC-1A` replicates in OSDR `n = 20`).

- **Page 10:** `50 ng` RNA libraries (`NEBNext Ultra II Directional` + `FAB` blocking) run on one `Illumina NovaSeq 6000` `S4` lane (`2 × 151` PE, `57.67M–65.47M` reads/sample).

### Act II: The Ionic & Oxidative Footprint (Pages 11–22)

- **Page 11:** Site-by-site `edgeR` results (`FDR < 0.05`, `>2`-fold, `PMC9098553` Figure 3B): **Apollo 11** triggers **`465` DEGs**, **Apollo 12** **`265` DEGs**, and **Apollo 17** **`113` DEGs**—mirroring their maturity gradient from mature (`10084`) to immature (`70051`).

- **Page 12:** Heatmap breakdown (`PMC9098553` Figure 3C): **`71%`** of DEGs across all three Apollo sites belong to **salt, metal, and reactive oxygen species (ROS)** stress pathways.

- **Page 13:** The remaining **`29%`** of coordinately expressed lunar DEGs point to **nutrient metabolism**—led by the most repressed common gene, **`Protochlorophyllide Oxidoreductase-A` (`AT5G54190`)**, and the most induced, **`AT5G26270`**, both classic markers of **phosphate starvation**.

- **Page 14:** Other shared lunar alarms light up (`PMC9098553` Figure 3D): **`Nitrilase-2` (`AT3G44300`)** (cadmium/metal response), **`Jasmonate-regulated gene-21` (`AT3G55970`)**, **`Defensin-like` (`AT1G13609`)**, and **`AT3G28290` / `AT3G28300`** (aluminum toxicity and jasmonate signaling).

- **Page 15:** Why did mature regolith hurt more? Geological cutaway (`PMC9098553` Introduction & Discussion): billions of years of solar-wind and micrometeorite bombardment shatter particles, pack `30–52 vol.%` **agglutinates**, and coat grains in reactive **nanophase metallic `Fe⁰`**—which oxidizes in air/water into `Fe`-oxides and leaches ions from micropores.

- **Page 16:** Yet inside each Apollo site, individual plants fared differently! The team re-sorts `9` lunar plants by **morphology** (`3` per group, `PMC9098553` Figure 4): **Large** (green, near-`JSC-1A` shape), **Small** (green, proportioned), and **Severe** (tiny, distorted, reddish-black).

- **Page 17:** Morphology `edgeR` shock (`PMC9098553` Figure 4B): **Small** plants show just **`130` DEGs** and **Large** plants **`150` DEGs** vs. `JSC-1A`—whereas **Severe** plants erupt with **`>1,000` DEGs**!

- **Page 18:** Inside the **Severe** transcriptome: ROS-associated genes dominate, flanked by hormone chaos, cell-wall remodeling, and calcium-signaling collapse.

- **Page 19:** Inside the **Small** transcriptome: `33` unique DEGs, still driven by ROS signaling, yet **metal-stress genes are almost absent**—suggesting those roots dodged local metal hotspots.

- **Page 20:** Inside the **Large** transcriptome (`63` unique DEGs): **`>60%`** are **salt and drought** genes, with **`LEA` (Late Embryogenesis Abundant)** proteins surging **`50–100`-fold**, while the most downregulated unique gene is **`ATPC2` (`AT1G15700`)**, a regulator of **ATP synthase**.

- **Page 21:** Venn overlap check: **`18` of `25`** coordinately expressed morphology DEGs match the site-wide core—proving that *even the healthiest-looking lunar plants* still pay a hidden metabolic tax.

- **Page 22:** Side-by-side comparison of **root establishment**: stochastic physical contact with sharp, glassy, ion-leaching regolith grains decides whether a seedling becomes *Large* or *Severe*.

### Act III: Before We Plant on the Moon (Pages 23–30)

- **Page 23:** Blueprint for Artemis lunar greenhouses: lunar regolith *works* as a physical matrix—no pathogen, no toxin blocks germination—but **it is not a benign substrate**.

- **Page 24:** Site selection matters: **immature highland/mare regolith** (`Apollo 17`-like) is far gentler (`113` DEGs, capillary wetting) than **mature, weathered regolith** (`Apollo 11`-like, `465` DEGs, hydrophobic).

- **Page 25:** Mitigation strategies sketched out: pre-washing or chelating reactive nanophase `Fe⁰` ions, blending in **basalt-derived Rockwool** or organic compost/microbes, or breeding crops with tuned `LEA`/phosphate/metal-tolerance pathways.

- **Page 26:** Connecting back to `OSD-427` and `OSD-437`: in orbit, plants battled microgravity and cosmic radiation; on the Moon, they will face `1/6g` *plus* reactive regolith chemistry.

- **Page 27:** UF's `canon EOS` archive (`Morphometric Photography`) and `NovaSeq` reads (`781.76 GB` total in `OSD-476` / `GLDS-476` / `LSDS-12`) become the first open-science reference for lunar *in situ* agriculture.

- **Page 28:** Peer-review shoutout on the archival page: `Communications Biology` (`May 12, 2022`), reviewed by Richard Barker and colleagues, bridges fifty years between Apollo 11 and Artemis.

- **Page 29:** GeneLab `GL-DPPD-7101-G` (`NF_RCP v2.1.0`) standardizes all `20` `NovaSeq` libraries against `TAIR10.59` for cross-mission mining.

- **Page 30:** Closing frame: a single green *Arabidopsis* rosette rooted in `900 mg` of dark gray Apollo 17 dust under LED light. Narration: *Earth's life can take root in the Moon—provided we learn to tame the dust.*
