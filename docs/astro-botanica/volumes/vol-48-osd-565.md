## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **KFT-Vessel (`Kennedy Space Center Fixation Tube`):** For over a decade, `KFTs` were passive orbital fixative tubes—astronauts moved plants *into* them only at harvest. Here, `KFT` transforms into a triple-contained suborbital habitat-plus-plunger: holding two wire-mesh coupons (`12` seedlings total) for `4+` hours before flooding them with `~25 mL` `RNAlater` on a human-tended cue.

- **Mesh-Coupon (`22 × 62 mm`):** A `0.425 mm` stainless-steel wire-mesh strip bent at one end and embedded in `1%` Phytagel / `0.5×` MS medium. Cut cleanly from a `100 mm` square Petri dish, it slides into the `KFT` with an exact press-fit so roots stay anchored during launch vibration and high-g re-entry.

- **Q-Roots (`Q1`–`Q4` Quiescent Timecourse):** Sealed inside the gas-tight `KFT` for `3 h 50 min` (`Q1`) through `T+18 min` (`Q4`), roots register the lack of air exchange first—triggering an **ethylene** response vs. open Petri plates and a rising **hypoxia** wave (`123 → 183 → 240` DEGs).

- **Q-Leaves:** Calmer inside the tube (`114 → 36 → 38` DEGs over time), leaves adjust rapidly to the slightly opaque `KFT` walls via **fructose**, **light**, and **photosynthesis** gene clusters.

- **Clinostat-4 (`C`):** A `KFT` rotated at `1 rpm` for `4 minutes` (`Q2 → Q3`) on a `2D` clinostat (`150 mm` radius) to mimic the ~3-minute suborbital microgravity coast of `Blue Origin New Shepard` or `Virgin Galactic SpaceShipTwo`—provoking **defense**, **cell wall**, and **detoxification** genes in leaves while exposing how critical time-matched controls are in roots.

- **Panel 2:** Six *Arabidopsis* `Col-0` seeds planted `5 mm` below the bend, growing vertically for `3 days` under `100 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹` LEDs, `8 h` in dark travel-simulation storage, and `4 days` in a portable `Danby DFG17A1B` unit (`~80 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹`).

**Page 3**

- **Panel 1:** Scalpel slices cleanly around two gel-clad coupons. They are placed back-to-back (`12` seedlings total) and slid into the `KFT` sample cylinder—an exact press-fit.

**Page 4**

- **Panel 1:** Timeline diagram of the mock suborbital run: `T-4 h` loading and sealing; `3 h 50 min` vertical quiescent hold (`Q1` at `T-10 min`); mock launch at `T+0`; `Q2` (`T+4 min`, boost end / coast start); `Q3` (`T+8 min`, coast end); and `Q4` (`T+18 min`, landing).

- **Caption:** *Before a human-tended flyer ever touches the plunger at apogee, the plants must wait nearly four hours in a sealed tube.*

**Page 5**

- **Panel 1:** At `T-10 min` (`Q1`), four replicate `KFTs` (`Q1A`, `Q1B`, `Q1C`, `Q1D`) are actuated alongside an unsealed Petri-plate control (`Q1P`).

- **Panel 2:** `RNAlater` surges upward into the sample chamber, bathing the seedlings for `24 hours` at room temperature before dissection into **roots** and **leaves**.

**Page 6**

- **Panel 1:** `RNA-Seq` recovery (`NovaSeq 6000`, `2 × 150 bp` PE, `~60M` reads/sample, `DESeq2` `|Log2FC| ≥ 1`, `p ≤ 0.05`): comparing `Q1A–D` roots against `Q1P` yields `163`, `201`, `139`, and `178` DEGs.

- **Panel 2:** Overlap Venn (`45.7%` shared by `≥2` KFTs) and `Table 1` pairwise check across `Q1A–D`: an average of just **`23` DEGs** (`6` up, `17` down) between replicate tubes.

- **Caption:** *Reproducible tube to tube. Inside triple containment, every KFT speaks the same dialect.*

**Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread Anchor — "The Sealed-Tube Signature")**

- **Visual:** A vertical split-screen inside the `KFT` cylinder. On the **left**, `Q1A–D` vs. `Q1P` roots glow with `244` upregulated DEGs enriched for **ethylene response** (gas trapped by triple containment) and `115` downregulated DEGs repressing **nutrient starvation**. On the **right**, the `Q1 → Q2 → Q3 → Q4` timecourse unfolds like rising tide lines: roots climb from `123` DEGs (`Q2R`) to `183` (`Q3R`) to `240` (`Q4R`) as **hypoxia** deepens (`465` total root DEGs), while leaves drop from `114` DEGs (`Q2L`) down to `36` (`Q3L`) and `38` (`Q4L`), settling into **fructose**, **light**, and **photosynthesis** adjustments (`179` total leaf DEGs).

- **Caption:** *Roots feel the airless seal first; leaves quickly recalibrate to the dim, translucent wall.*

**Page 9**

- **Panel 1:** Close-up on leaf cluster heatmaps (`Figure 5c`): `49` upregulated genes enrich for **response to fructose**, `60` variable genes track **light and photosynthesis**, and `70` downregulated genes track **response to stimuli** under the `KFT` wall's slight opacity.

**Page 10**

- **Panel 1:** `Q2` arrives (`T+4 min`). One `KFT` (`C`) is mounted onto a `2D` clinostat (`150 mm` radius) parallel to the plane of rotation.

- **SFX:** *HUMMM — `1 RPM` FOR `4 MINUTES`.*

### Act II: Four Minutes on the Wheel (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11**

- **Panel 1:** The clinostat spins gently, averaging the gravity vector during the `Q2 → Q3` (`T+4` to `T+8 min`) coast window before the `KFT` plunger is slammed home at `Q3`.

- **Caption:** *Four minutes of randomized gravity—long enough to test whether a sealed `KFT` can still hear a rapid gravitational signal.*

**Page 12**

- **Panel 1:** Experimental trap (`Figure 6`): researchers compare Clinostat roots (`CR`) against **four** different controls—time-matched `Q3R` (`T+8 min`), pre-launch `Q1R` (`T-10 min`), pooled `Q1R + Q3R`, and pooled `Q1R + Q2R + Q3R`.

**Page 13**

- **Panel 1:** Heatmap shockwave in roots (`369` total DEGs): `CR vs. Q1R` lights up with **`267` unique DEGs (`72.4%`)**, including `238` induced genes enriched for **temperature stimulus** and **abiotic stress**.

- **Panel 2:** Yet when `CR` is compared to time-matched `Q3R` or pooled controls (`Q1R+Q3R`, `Q1R+Q2R+Q3R`), **`228` of those `Q1R`-unique genes vanish below threshold**!

**Page 14**

- **Panel 1:** Side-by-side explanation: `CR` sat sealed in the `KFT` `18 minutes` longer than `Q1R`. Comparing against `Q1R` alone mistakes `18 minutes` of extra **hypoxia** and **tube acclimation** for a gravity response.

- **Dialogue (Narration):** "In roots, a mistimed control writes false gravity alarms."

**Page 15**

- **Panel 1:** Solution verified: `11` root genes (`3%`) are shared across all four control comparisons, and `79` (`21.4%`) appear in `≥2`—and **pooled controls** (`Q1R+Q3R` or `Q1R+Q2R+Q3R`) strip away the `228` confounding genes almost as cleanly as `Q3R`!

**Page 16**

- **Panel 1:** Now check the leaves (`Figure 7`, `238` total DEGs): `8` genes (`3.4%`) are shared across all four comparisons, `127` (`53.4%`) appear in `≥2`, and `CL vs. Q1L` has only `53` (`22.3%`) unique genes.

- **Caption:** *Leaves are far less sensitive to the extra `18 minutes` of sealing—meaning even a pre-launch control leaves their gravity signal legible.*

**Page 17**

- **Panel 1:** GO enrichment of the clinostat leaf clusters (`Figure 7a`): the first `65` genes enrich for **defense-related pathways**; the next `65` enrich for **cell wall** and **detoxification**—the classic *Arabidopsis* spaceflight signature, emerging in just `4 minutes` of clinorotation!

**Page 18**

- **Panel 1:** Close-up on the `1%` Phytagel / `0.5×` MS gel clinging to the stainless-steel `Mesh-Coupon`: even under suborbital hyper-g and vibration profiles, prior flights showed `7–12 day-old` seedlings stay firmly anchored without tearing.

**Page 19**

- **Panel 1:** Inside the `UF ICBR` sequencing core: `100 ng` total RNA, `SPT Labtech mosquito LV` liquid handler (`1/10` reaction volume), `15` PCR cycles, `NovaSeq 6000` S4 lane (`Q30 ≥ 92.5%`).

**Page 20**

- **Panel 1:** `STAR v2.7.9a` → `HTSeq` → `DESeq2` (`TAIR10.51`) pipeline diagram, showing `rRNA` contamination held low (`0.52%–2.97%` across `Q1–Q3` samples) and `~50–102M` reads per sample.

**Page 21**

- **Panel 1:** Operational rehearsal: a human researcher practicing one-handed `KFT` plunger actuation while strapped in—seconds matter when microgravity lasts `180` seconds.

**Page 22**

- **Panel 1:** Checklist of best practices established by `OSD-565`: (1) pre-seal no earlier than necessary, (2) pair with time-matched or pooled quiescent ground controls, (3) separate roots and leaves before `RNA-Seq`.
