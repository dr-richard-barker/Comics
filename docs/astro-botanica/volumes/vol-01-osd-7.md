## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Volume:** `Vol. 1 — THE SILENT CANOPY, THE SCREAMING ROOT`

- **Target Accession:** `OSD-7` (`GLDS-7` / `E-MTAB-1264` / DOI: `10.25966/7f77-hw17`)

- **Primary Publication:** Paul AL, Zupanska AK, Schultz ER, Ferl RJ. "Organ-specific remodeling of the Arabidopsis transcriptome in response to spaceflight." *BMC Plant Biology*. 2013;13:112. DOI: `10.1186/1471-2229-13-112` (PMID: `23919896`)

- **Organism & Genotypes:** *Arabidopsis thaliana* — Wild-Type **Wassilewskija (`WS`)** (whole-plant assays) and mixed-plate **60% `WS` / 40% `Col-0`** (organ-dissected leaves, hypocotyls, roots); plus GFP reporter lines **`35s::GFP`**, **`Adh::GFP`**, and **`DR5r::GFP`** (imaging Run 3A).

- **Flight Hardware & Conditions:** **Advanced Biological Research System (ABRS)** with the **GFP Imaging System (GIS)** (6 vertical `10 cm²` solid `0.5x` MS phytagel plates; VOC-scrubbed air, `22–24°C` [mean `23°C`], white light, `light cycle: Not Available` in OSDR metadata). Flown on Space Shuttle `STS-129` (Run 1B), `STS-130` (Run 3A), and `STS-131` *Discovery* (Runs 2A & 2B; returned on `STS-132` *Atlantis*). Ground control in identical ABRS inside the **Orbital Environmental Simulator (OES)** at KSC on a 24–48 hr telemetry delay. Harvested at 12 days (14.5 days for Run 3A) into **RNAlater**-filled **KSC Fixation Tubes (KFTs)** and frozen in **MELFI** (`<-40°C`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Squad `WS` (Wassilewskija) & Squad `Col-0` (Columbia-0):** *Arabidopsis* seedling battalions sharing the `10 cm²` phytagel plates in a `60% WS / 40% Col-0` coalition (while pure `WS` stands watch in the whole-plant control). `Col-0` struggles with reduced lateral root branching in microgravity; both squads exhibit shorter roots, shorter hypocotyls, and pronounced root skewing.

- **Sentinel `DR5r::GFP` (Auxin Reporter):** A glowing bioluminescent scout embedded in the hypocotyls. Goes eerily dim during early orbital growth (Days 3.5–7.5) compared to Ground Control, then flares past terrestrial levels after Day 10.

- **Commander `HY5` (ELONGATED HYPOCOTYL 5) & `RHL41` (Responsive to High Light 41, `3-fold` up in roots):** Photoreceptor-linked tactical officers operating *underground* in the root zone, hijacking light-sensing pathways to compensate for missing gravity.

- **Commander `DREB2A` (`At5g05410`), `DDF1` (`At1g12610`), & `WRKY48`:** Stress and touch/wound transcription factor commanders consistently upregulated across Runs 1B, 2A, and 2B (`p ≤ 0.01`) in roots.

- **Engineer `TCH4` (`At5g57560`, Xyloglucan Endotransglycosylase):** Master cell-wall remodeler; heavily upregulated in roots across all three flight runs, cutting and re-ligating xyloglucan tethers to steer skewing root tips.

- **Tactical Operative `JAZ7` (`At2g34600`):** Jasmonate-signaling repressor upregulated across Runs 1B, 2A, and 2B in roots, coordinating defense/wound cross-talk.

- **Gatekeeper `PGP18` (P-Glycoprotein 18):** Dual-allegiance light/cell-wall transporter—**highly upregulated in leaves**, yet **strongly repressed in roots**.

- **Scout `ELIP1` (`At3g22840`, Early Light-Induced Protein 1):** Chloroplast/light-stress sentinel consistently **downregulated** in spaceflight roots across Runs 1B, 2A, and 2B.

- **Metadata Anomaly ("The 60/40 Chimera & Missing Clock"):** The organ-dissected microarray plates combine `60% WS` and `40% Col-0` in single tubes, while the OSDR sample table records `light cycle: Not Available`—an archival blind spot in the orbital log.

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

### Page 31 — Experimental Architecture & The Whole-Plant Trap

- **Core Takeaway:** Whole-plant transcriptomics masked spaceflight's true impact. Dissecting 12-day-old *Arabidopsis thaliana* seedlings grown in the **ABRS / GIS** (`STS-129`, `STS-130`, `STS-131`) into **Leaves, Hypocotyls, and Roots** uncovered **480 DEGs** (`p < 0.01`, `≥ 1.9-fold`) and **58 high-magnitude DEGs** (`≥ 7-fold`), with only **26 genes** coordinately regulated across all three organs (17 up, 9 down).

- **Organ-by-Organ Doctrine:**

  - **Leaves:** Repressed hormone signaling (`ABC transporters`, `auxin response factors`) and cell wall proteins (`endochitinase`, `polygalacturonase`, `xylan glucuronosyltransferase`); upregulated herbivory/pathogen defense, Ca²⁺/phosphate signaling, and **`PGP18`**.

  - **Hypocotyls:** Shorter morphology; `1.5–4-fold` upregulation of `SAUR-like` (`AuxRE`) genes; `DR5r::GFP` auxin reporter repressed early (Days 3.5–7.5) then surged past Ground Control after Day 10.

  - **Roots:** Dominated the response (**20 of 31** genes induced `≥ 7-fold`), driving shorter roots, fewer `Col-0` lateral roots, negative phototropism, and skewing via **`TCH4`**, **`DDF1`**, **`DREB2A`**, **`JAZ7`**, **`WRKY48`**, **`CAF1`**, **`COW1`**, **`SP1L5`**, and **`HY5`/`RHL41`** light-signaling co-option, while **`ELIP1`** and **`PGP18`** were repressed.

### Page 32 — BLSS Implications & Study Citation

- **Bioregenerative Life Support (BLSS) Impact:**

  - **Organ-Resolved Crop Monitoring:** Whole-plant bulk assays miss tissue-specific stress (especially root-zone remodeling and leaf defense priming); future BLSS biosensors should target organ-specific promoters.

  - **Lighting as a Gravity Substitute:** Roots co-opt `HY5`-mediated light pathways (`RHL41`, `EXO`, `XT1`, `NUDT21`, `WRKY48`) when gravity is absent—meaning directional LED spectra can actively steer root architecture in orbital farms.

  - **Controlled Environment Baseline:** Using VOC-scrubbed ABRS chambers and RNAlater KFTs eliminated hypoxia (`Adh::GFP` remained silent), isolating genuine microgravity/spaceflight adaptation.
