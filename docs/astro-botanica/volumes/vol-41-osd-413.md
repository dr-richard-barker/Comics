## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 41

- **Title:** *The Clean Chamber's First Crop*

- **OSDR / GLDS Accession:** `OSD-413` (Version 2) / `GLDS-413` | **DOI:** `10.26030/3p20-j525`

- **Chronology Note:** Follows Volume 40 (`OSD-412`, `VEG-03D`) directly (`skipped_accessions: []`). While `VEG-03D` ran in veteran `Veggie SN 002`, `OSD-413` (`VEG-03E`) inaugurates the brand-new **`Veggie SN 001`** unit—grown concurrently (`3` days apart) with `VEG-03F` (`OSD-414`) in `SN 002` to test continuous, staggered orbital crop production.

- **Primary Publication:** Hummerick, M. E., Khodadad, C. L. M., Dixit, A. R., Spencer, L. E., Maldonado-Vasquez, G. J., Gooden, J. L., Spern, C. J., Fischer, J. A., Dufour, N., Wheeler, R. M., Romeyn, M. W., Smith, T. M., Massa, G. D., & Zhang, Y. (2021). *Spatial Characterization of Microbial Communities on Multi-Species Leafy Greens Grown Simultaneously in the Vegetable Production Systems on the International Space Station*. *Life*, 11(10), 1060. DOI: `10.3390/life11101060` | PMID: `34685431` | `PMC8537831`

- **Facility / Mission & Conditions:** ISS Columbus module (`Veggie SN 001`, launched aboard Orbital ATK `OA-7` in `2017`), supplied via `SpaceX-12` (`08/14/2017`–`09/17/2017`); `VEG-03E` initiated `2/6/18` (`160 mL` water initiation, dry root mat), `4` harvests (`3/8/18`, `3/14/18`, `3/27/18`, `4/6/18` at `59` days), returned `8/30/18`. LEDs at `200–300 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹` PAR (`16 h / 8 h`), `23°C`, `50%` RH, `3,000 ppm` `CO2`; `HOBO` logger recorded temperature/humidity/dew point without the `25`-day fan anomaly seen in `VEG-03D`.

- **Organism / Genotypes:** Simultaneous mixed crop of *Brassica rapa* var. *japonica* (mizuna mustard), *Lactuca sativa* cv. 'Outredgeous' (red Romaine lettuce), and *Lactuca sativa* cv. "Waldmann's Green" (green leaf lettuce); `n = 12` `16S` OSDR sequencing samples derived from `2` returned mizuna pillows (`SN01` and `SN08`: root, `3` media replicates, wick, and leaf per pillow) (`OSD-413`), alongside culture/swab analyses across all three crops (`PMC8537831`).

- **Assay / Platform:** Paired-end `16S rRNA` V4 (`F: 5'-GTGCCAGCMGCCGCGGTAA-3'`, `R: 5'-GGACTACHVGGGTWTCTAAT-3'`) and fungal `ITS` amplicon sequencing on `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` paired-end, `10%` `PhiX`, `85,610`–`312,906` reads/sample on `OSD-413`; `RDP`/`GreenGenes`/`SILVA`/`UNITE`, `QIIME 2.0`), plus `TSA`/`IMA` plate counts and `3M Petrifilm`/`BPW`/`RV` pathogen screening (`PMC8537831`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **`Veggie SN 001` (The Unused Chamber):** Newly installed on ISS and never previously operated—resulting in below-detection microbial counts on all interior bellows and fan outlet swabs (`5/8` sites `<DL`) (`PMC8537831`).

- **Mizuna Pillows `SN01` & `SN08`:** The two `VEG-03E` pillows returned to Earth for full `16S` spatial profiling (`n = 12` samples on `OSD-413`), yielding `8.7 × 10⁷` and `2.5 × 10⁸ CFU/g` root bacteria and the study's highest unique wick genus count (`19` genera) (`PMC8537831`).

- **Red Romaine (`OL`) & "Waldmann's Green" (`WG`) Neighbors:** Grown in the redesigned, grouped `VEG-03E` layout; `OL` leaves dropped to `5.7 × 10³ CFU/g` (`p ≤ 0.05` lower than `D` and `F`), while `WG` struggled with `62.5%` germination and yielded only `1` harvest (`2/4` healthy pillows) (`PMC8537831`).

- ***Novosphingobium*, *Staphylococcus*, *Nevskia* & *Spirosoma*:** Signature bacterial genera in `VEG-03E`—including crew-linked *Staphylococcus* surging on one mizuna leaf and *Nevskia*/*Spirosoma* appearing in `E` while absent in `F` (`PMC8537831`).

- ***Exophiala* spp. & *Purpureocillium lilacinum*:** Rare fungal finds in `VEG-03E`—*Exophiala* isolated exclusively from `VEG-03E` substrate, and *Purpureocillium lilacinum* recovered from `E` and `F` chamber swabs (`PMC8537831`).

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: A Fresh Chamber (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 — Splash Page**

- **Panel 1 (Full-Page Splash):** Inside Columbus, two `Veggie` units glow side by side: veteran `SN 002` on the right, and brand-new **`SN 001`** on the left, its baseplate raised and bellows lowered as grouped pillows of mizuna, red Romaine, and green leaf lettuce take root (`Figure 1B`). Overlaid in crisp ink: *"VOLUME 41: THE CLEAN CHAMBER'S FIRST CROP — OSD-413 (VEG-03E)."*

- **Caption:** Same seeds. Same ISS air and water. Brand-new walls.

**Page 2 — The Grouped Layout (`2/6/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** Lessons from `VEG-03D` reshape the tray: instead of interleaving species, `VEG-03E` groups cultivars together so fast-growing mizuna no longer buries neighboring lettuce.

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** `160 mL` of ISS potable water enters each pillow (`250 mL` autoclaved arcillite + `7.5 g/L` `18-6-8` `100`-day Nutricote) while the root mat stays dry. Three days later (`2/9/18`), `VEG-03F` starts next door in `SN 002`.

**Page 3 — Uneven Start for Green Leaf**

- **Panel 1 (Split):** Mizuna and red Romaine sprout vigorously, but "Waldmann's Green" (`WG`) averages only `62.5%` germination across `E/F`, with two stunted seedlings.

- **Panel 2 (Medium):** After thinning, one `WG` seedling stalls, leaving `2` of `4` `WG` pillows healthy—allowing only **one** `WG` harvest in `VEG-03E`.

**Page 4 — No Fan Anomaly**

- **Panel 1 (Instrument Inset):** The `HOBO` logger inside `SN 001` tracks steady airflow, temperature, and humidity—none of the `25`-day `100%` RH condensation that soaked `VEG-03D`.

- **Panel 2 (Medium):** Crew hands still use `Pro-San` wipes on occasional pillow-thread fuzz and on harvested leaves before eating.

**Page 5 — Four Harvests to Day `59`**

- **Panel 1 (Timeline):** `3/8/18` (Harvest `1`) → `3/14/18` (Harvest `2`, frozen in `MELFI`) → `3/27/18` (Harvest `3`, eaten) → `4/6/18` (Final Harvest `4` at `59` days, split between `MELFI` and galley).

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** Older mizuna leaves show yellowing dry edges after Day `30–31` as `100`-day Nutricote draws down.

**Page 6 — Eight Swabs in a Virgin Chamber**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** Astronauts swab all `8` standard `Veggie` sites in `SN 001` before wipe-down: `3` pillow tops, `3` bellows zones (upper, middle, lower), the internal fan screen, and the fan outlet.

**Page 7 — Double-Page Spread Anchor: `5/8` Below Detection**

- **Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread):** A stark visual contrast between `VEG-03D` (`SN 002`) and `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`). On `SN 001`, **every single bellows swab (`UL`, `MF`, `LR`) and the fan outlet read `<DL` (below detection limit)** for both bacteria and fungi! Only **`3` of `8` sites** yield colonies: **Pillow 1** (`2.1 × 10⁴` bacteria, `8.0 × 10³` fungi), **Pillow 2** (`7.7 × 10⁴` bacteria, `1.6 × 10⁴` fungi), and the **Fan Screen** (`4.1 × 10⁴` bacteria, `3.3 × 10⁴` fungi CFU/swab).

- **Caption:** *"A brand-new chamber starts clean—only the pillows and the air-intake fan screen carry the first footprints."*

**Page 9 — Return to Earth (`8/30/18`)**

- **Panel 1 (Medium):** `VEG-03E` samples and two intact mizuna pillows (`SN01` and `SN08`) return to KSC, thawed at `4°C` and separated into leaf, root, wick, and `3` arcillite media replicates (`n = 12` OSDR `16S` samples).

**Page 10 — Safe to Eat**

- **Panel 1 (Three-Plate Check):** `3M Petrifilm` (*E. coli*/coliform, *S. aureus*) and *Salmonella* enrichments are all negative.

- **Dialogue (Microbiologist):** *"Just like `VEG-03D`, every leafy green from `SN 001` cleared food-safety screening."*

### Act II: The Clean-Unit Microbiome (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 — Red Romaine Drops Three Orders of Magnitude**

- **Panel 1 (Bar Chart):** Look at red Romaine (`OL`) leaf `APC`: in `VEG-03D` (`6.9 × 10⁶ CFU/g`) and `VEG-03F` (`6.1 × 10⁶ CFU/g`), both in `SN 002`, counts sit in the millions. In `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`), `OL` drops to **`5.7 × 10³ CFU/g`** (down to `4.3 × 10² CFU/g`, `p ≤ 0.05`).

- **Caption:** A cleaner chamber and steadier humidity meant a thousand-fold quieter lettuce leaf.

**Page 12 — Mizuna & Green Leaf Hold Steady**

- **Panel 1 (Comparison):** Mizuna leaves in `VEG-03E` stay within the `1.4 × 10³`–`2.6 × 10⁷ CFU/g` range (`D`/`E` combined), while the single green leaf harvest hits `3.5 × 10⁷ CFU/g`.

**Page 13 — Inside Mizuna Pillows `SN01` & `SN08`**

- **Panel 1 (Cutaway):** Mizuna roots yield `8.7 × 10⁷` and `2.5 × 10⁸ CFU/g` bacteria. Remarkably, the `VEG-03E` mizuna pillow with the highest bacterial load also carries the **highest fungal load** in roots and substrate across the entire `D`/`E`/`F` series.

**Page 14 — `85,610` to `312,906` Reads**

- **Panel 1 (Sequencer):** `Illumina MiSeq` (`250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`) sequences all `12` `SN01`/`SN08` mizuna compartments, delivering `85,610` to `312,906` raw reads per sample (`OSD-413`).

**Page 15 — `63%` Sample Type (`Figure 8D`)**

- **Panel 1 (`PCoA` Plot):** In `VEG-03E`, `PCoA` Axis 1 explains `~40%` of variance (lower than `VEG-03D`'s `67–79%`), and `sample type` drives **`63%`** of community structure (`p < 0.05`). Leaf, root, and substrate sit closer together, while the **wick** wanders farthest afield.

**Page 16 — Nineteen Unique Wick Genera (`Figure 9B`)**

- **Panel 1 (Venn Diagram):** `VEG-03E` mizuna pillows share **`31`** genera across wick, substrate, and root—and the wick alone harbors **`19` unique genera**, the highest unique wick count of any tech demo in the series, alongside **`10`** unique genera on `VEG-03E` mizuna leaves.

**Page 17 — `Burkholderia` & `Novosphingobium` (`Figure 11`)**

- **Panel 1 (Stacked Bar Chart):** *Burkholderia* claims up to **`50%`** of total `16S` reads in some `VEG-03E` samples. Meanwhile, **`Novosphingobium`** surges higher in `VEG-03E` mizuna substrate and roots than it ever did in `VEG-03D`.

**Page 18 — The Crew's Handprint (`Staphylococcus`)**

- **Panel 1 (Leaf Close-up):** On one `VEG-03E` mizuna leaf, human-associated **`Staphylococcus`** commands a large slice of the top `15` genera—also appearing in small amounts on the wick, yet completely **undetectable** in the root or arcillite substrate.

- **Dialogue (Microbiologist):** *"That's vertical and cabin-surface contact in action—from crew hands and air onto the leaf, never touching the root."*

**Page 19 — What `SN 001` Left Behind**

- **Panel 1 (Split Checklist):** *Enterobacter*, *Chitinophaga*, and *Bradyrhizobium*—found in `VEG-03D` and `VEG-03F` (`SN 002`)—are **missing** from `VEG-03E` (`SN 001`). Instead, `VEG-03E` carries **`Nevskia`** and **`Spirosoma`** (taxa also seen in `VEG-01A/B` and `VEG-03A`, absent in `VEG-03F`).

**Page 20 — The One-of-One *Exophiala***

- **Panel 1 (Fungal Plate):** On `IMA` plates, *Exophiala* spp. appears in **only one sample** across all three tech demos—the `VEG-03E` substrate—while *Purpureocillium lilacinum* shows up on `E` and `F` swabs alongside *Aspergillus*, *Fusarium oxysporum*, *Penicillium*, and *Rhodotorula*.

**Page 21 — `Pielou` & `Shannon` Stability**

- **Panel 1 (Diversity Strip):** Despite fewer colonized chamber surfaces, `VEG-03E` preserves the exact spatial rule of `VEG-03D`: substrate and roots hold the highest `Shannon` diversity, followed by wicks, swabs, and leaves (`0.1` to `2.0`).

**Page 22 — Two Chambers, One Station**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** Across `Veggie` `SN 001` and `SN 002`, `VEG-03E` proves that chamber history (`SN 001` vs. `SN 002`) shifts surface colonization and leaf bioburden—even when both units draw from the same ISS air and potable water.

### Act III: Staggered Harvests & Next Door (Pages 23–30)

**Page 23 — Continuous Production Proven**

- **Panel 1 (Calendar):** By staggering `VEG-03E` (`2/6/18`) and `VEG-03F` (`2/9/18`) across two `Veggie` units, astronauts enjoyed overlapping fresh harvests without waiting `59` days between trays.

**Page 24 — The Water-to-Wick Pipeline**

- **Panel 1 (Diagram):** ISS potable water (*Burkholderia*, *Ralstonia*) → autoclaved arcillite → wick → root, while ISS air and crew hands (*Staphylococcus*) seed the leaf and fan screen.

**Page 25 — Archiving `OSD-413`**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** All `12` `VEG-03E` mizuna `16S` datasets (`GLDS-413`, `10.26030/3p20-j525`) enter NASA OSDR, documenting `SN 001`'s inaugural orbital harvest.

**Page 26 — Looking Three Days Ahead (`SN 002`)**

- **Panel 1 (Wide):** The camera pans right from `SN 001` to veteran `SN 002`, where `VEG-03F` (`2/9/18`–`4/9/18`) ran in parallel.

**Page 27 — Highest Surface Loads on Record**

- **Panel 1 (Foreshadowing):** On `VEG-03F` pillow tops, bacterial and fungal swabs climb to **`4.0 × 10⁶ CFU/swab`**—the highest surface counts of the entire `VEG-03D/E/F` trilogy.

**Page 28 — Why `SN 002` Diverged**

- **Panel 1 (Split):** New `SN 001` (`VEG-03E`, `3/8` swabs positive) vs. seasoned `SN 002` (`VEG-03F`, `5/8` bacteria & `7/8` fungi swabs positive)—showing how persistent chamber residents accumulate over repeated grow-outs.

**Page 29 — Red Romaine's Turn (`VEG-03F`)**

- **Panel 1 (Close-up):** While `OSD-413` (`VEG-03E`) archived mizuna pillows, `OSD-414` (`VEG-03F`) zooms in on **'Outredgeous' red Romaine** pillows grown in `SN 002`.

**Page 30 — Next Launch Window**

- **Panel 1 (Final Splash):** `Veggie SN 002`'s raised bellows frame `VEG-03F`'s red Romaine canopy as `10` final `16S` datasets prepare for sequencing.

- **Caption:** Next: `OSD-414` (*VEG-03F*). The veteran chamber's red Romaine harvest—and the trilogy's final piece.

### Right Spread (Page 32): `OSD-413` `16S` Mizuna Spatial Profile (`n = 12`)

- **OSDR Sequencing (`OSD-413`):** `2` mizuna pillows (`SN01`, `SN08`: root, `3` media replicates, wick, leaf = `12` samples); `Illumina MiSeq` `250-bp` PE, `10%` `PhiX`, `85,610`–`312,906` reads/sample.

- **Diversity & Spatial Sharing (`Figure 8D`, `Figure 9B`, `Figure 11`):**

  - `PCoA` Axis 1 explained `~40%` of variance; `sample type` drove **`63%`** (`p < 0.05`), with wick showing greatest variance and leaf/root/substrate clustering closer.

  - **`31`** genera shared across wick + substrate + root; **`19` unique wick genera** (highest in trilogy); **`10` unique leaf genera**.

- **Signature Taxa:**

  - *Burkholderia* up to `50%` of reads; *Novosphingobium* higher in `VEG-03E` mizuna substrate/roots than in `VEG-03D`.

  - *Staphylococcus* dominated a large fraction of top `15` genera on one `VEG-03E` mizuna leaf (absent in root/substrate).

  - *Nevskia* and *Spirosoma* present in `VEG-03E` (absent in `F`); *Enterobacter*, *Chitinophaga*, *Bradyrhizobium* absent in `E` (present in `D`/`F`).

  - *Exophiala* spp. isolated exclusively in `VEG-03E` substrate; *Purpureocillium lilacinum* on `E`/`F` swabs.
