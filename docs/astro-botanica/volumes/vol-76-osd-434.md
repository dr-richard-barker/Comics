### Act II: Roots versus Shoots (Pages 11–20)

**Page 11–15**
- Tissue-specific RNA-Seq reveals stark differences between plant organs during suborbital flight.
- **Root tissue:** 612 differentially expressed genes. Massive upregulation of *CML38*, *TCH2*, and heat-shock chaperones.
- **Shoot tissue:** Only 148 genes altered. Shoots act as a buffered, stable organ that resists acute acceleration shocks.

**Page 16–20**
- Mechanistic insights: Why are roots four times more sensitive to fast gravity transitions than shoots?
- The presence of freely movable statoliths in the root cap acts as an ultra-sensitive mechanical amplifier. Shoots rely on a distributed endodermal sheath with higher mechanical damping.

### Act III: The Dawn of Commercial Suborbital Science (Pages 21–32)
