- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 59

- **Chapter Title:** *Tubers in the Basalt Dust*

- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-750` (`GeneLab ID: GLDS-667` | `ALSDA ID: LSDS-165`, Version 1 | `DOI: 10.26030/w56j-2d69` | License: `CC0-1.0` | Size: `56.54 GB` | Submitted: `08-Jul-2024` | Initial Release: `18-Nov-2026`)

- **Project & Primary Team:** NASA Project `OS-927` (*Growth, physiology and nutrition dynamics of potato plants grown on lunar regolith simulant medium*, Grant `# 80NSSC24K0755`); Aymeric Goyer & David Handy (Oregon State University), with Anika Loeffler, Sydney Campbell, and Medora Knudson (associated `Goyer2026` supplementary data tables curated in OSDR).

- **Skipped Accessions:** `OSD-746` through `OSD-749` (contain no plant studies)

- **Hardware & Environment:** Oregon State University greenhouse (`22°C` day / `18°C` night, `20°C` mean, natural sunlight); `8.89 × 7.62 cm` square pots (`~300 mL` substrate) lined with landscaping fabric; `Osmocote Plus 15-9-12` (`43 cm³` per `7,570 cm³`); `40 mL` RO water every `2–3` days increasing to daily; **Exp. 1:** Lunar Mare Simulant `LMS-1E` vs. Oregon `Adkins` sandy soil (`0%`, `15%`, `30% v/v` vermicompost); **Exp. 2:** `Adkins` soil vs. five LRSs (`JSC-1A`, `LMS-1E` mare; `LHS-1E`, `NUW-LHT-5M`, `OPRH4W30` highland) each with `5%` vermicompost.

- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Solanum tuberosum* cv. `'Modoc'` mini-tubers (`n = 173` total samples in OSDR; `74 DAP` harvest in Exp. 1, `59 DAP` in Exp. 2); **3D Multispectral Imaging** (`Phenospex PlantEye F600`), **Chlorophyll Fluorescence** (`PhotosynQ MultispeQ`), **Biomass**, **Substrate Chemistry** (`Hanna HI5522` `pH`/`EC`, `Picarro` `CO₂` burst, `Agilent 5110` ICP-OES), **RNA-Seq** (`Illumina NovaSeq X Plus` `2 × 150 bp`, `39 DAP` leaf), and **GC-MS Metabolomics** (`Agilent 7890B-5977B`, `Fiehn 2013` library, `39 DAP` leaf & `59 DAP` tuber).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Dr. Aymeric Goyer (Oregon State University Principal Investigator):** Conceptualizer of `OS-927`; tests whether potato—a high-harvest-index staple tuber crop—can survive and yield in lunar mare and highland regolith simulants amended with worm castings.

- **David Handy (Oregon State University Investigation Lead):** Oversees the two greenhouse trials (`2024` compost-ratio screen and `2025` five-simulant comparison), `PlantEye F600` `3D` point-cloud scans, `MultispeQ` leaf-clamp physiology, and `74`-/`59`-day tuber harvests.

- **Anika Loeffler, Sydney Campbell & Medora Knudson (OSU Undergraduate & Beginning Researchers):** Support plant care, `Virtis` freeze-drying, mortar-and-pestle grinding, and soil/metabolite sample preparation.

- **The `'Modoc'` Mini-Tubers (*Solanum tuberosum*):** Sprouted to `2 cm` and planted into `300 mL` pots—forced to push stolons and swell daughter tubers inside crushed silicate rock rather than Earth loam.

- **The Five Lunar Simulants (`LMS-1E`, `JSC-1A`, `LHS-1E`, `NUW-LHT-5M`, `OPRH4W30`):** Two dark basaltic mare simulants and three pale anorthositic highland simulants—low in organic carbon (`CO₂` burst `1–2 µg/g/day` unamended), alkaline at start (`pH 7.9`), and prone to releasing excess bio-available copper (`Cu`).

- **The Vermicompost Microbiome (`5%` Non-Autoclaved + `0–25%` Autoclaved):** Worm castings blended at `0%`, `5%`, `15%`, or `30% (v/v)` to equalize the starting microbial inoculum while buffering `pH`, raising `CO₂` respiration (`25 µg/g/day` at `30%`), and supplying organic structure.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Planting the Moon (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1**

- **Panel 1 (Full-width establishing shot):** Morning sunlight slants across the Oregon State University greenhouse benches. Rows of `8.89 × 7.62 cm` square pots sit beneath the gantry of a **Phenospex `PlantEye F600`** `3D` laser scanner.

- **Panel 2 (Close-up):** A gloved hand lines each pot with dark landscaping fabric and spoons in `300 mL` of slate-grey **Lunar Mare Simulant `LMS-1E`** mixed with `Osmocote Plus 15-9-12`.

- **Caption (Narrator):** *Lettuce in orbit gave astronauts fresh vitamins. To live on the Moon, they need calories—and nothing packs starch per square meter like potato.*

- **Panel 3 (Two-shot):** Aymeric Goyer and David Handy check the sprouted `'Modoc'` mini-tubers (`CSS Farms`) resting beside buckets of dark vermicompost.

- **Aymeric Goyer:** "We can't haul Iowa topsoil to Shackleton Crater. If we want lunar tubers, we have to see what `'Modoc'` does in crushed mare and highland rock."

**Page 2**

- **Panel 1 (Map & soil sample inset):** Reference control: **Adkins-series sandy soil** dug from the top `30 cm` of an uncultivated ryegrass field in Umatilla County, Oregon (`45.817318, -119.294922`).

- **Panel 2 (Experiment 1 matrix, August 2, 2024):** Six treatments (`n = 36` pots): `100%`, `85%`, and `70%` `LMS-1E` vs. `100%`, `85%`, and `70%` `Adkins` soil—blended with **`0%`**, **`15%`**, or **`30%` vermicompost**.

- **David Handy:** "Every amended pot gets `5%` non-autoclaved worm castings plus `10%` or `25%` autoclaved castings—so `15%` and `30%` start with the exact same living microbiome."

**Page 3**

- **Panel 1 (Baseline soil lab readout):** At the OSU Soil Health Lab, a `Hanna HI5522` probe dips into `1:1` substrate-water slurries, while a `Picarro` isotopic `CO₂` analyzer tracks `24 h` and `96 h` respiration at `23°C`.

- **Panel 2 (Numbers on screen):** Pure `LMS-1E` (`0%` compost) reads **`pH 7.9`**, **`EC 0.73 dS/m`**, and a near-sterile **`2 µg CO₂-C/g/day`** at `24 h`. At `30%` vermicompost, `LMS-1E` drops to **`pH 7.06`**, **`EC 2.03 dS/m`**, and jumps twelve-fold to **`25 µg CO₂-C/g/day`**.

- **Panel 3 (Action):** `40 mL` of reverse-osmosis water wets each pot as greenhouse vents hold `22°C` day / `18°C` night.

**Page 4**

- **Panel 1 (Overhead scanner pass, Days 20–33):** Every `12` hours, the `PlantEye F600` sweeps overhead—triangulating `3D` laser point clouds and logging red, green, blue, and near-infrared reflectance (`29` scans per plant).

- **Panel 2 (Digital readout):** Point clouds resolve into digital biomass (`height × 3D leaf area`), leaf angle, `NDVI`, `NPCI`, and `PSRI` senescence index.

- **Anika Loeffler:** "In pure `LMS-1E` (`0%` compost), emergence is patchy—only `4` of `6` tubers even break the surface, and the canopies stay compact."

**Page 5**

- **Panel 1 (Day 63, October 4, 2024):** Sydney Campbell clamps a handheld **`PhotosynQ MultispeQ`** onto light-adapted `'Modoc'` leaves (`22` plants, `3` readings each).

- **Panel 2 (Instrument screen):** `Phi2` (`PhiPSII`), `PhiNPQ`, `NPQt`, linear electron flow (`LEF`), electrochromic shift (`ECS tau`), proton conductivity (`gH+`), `SPAD` chlorophyll, and `PS1` redox centers stream to the tablet.

- **Aymeric Goyer:** "Even when the leaves look green, the `MultispeQ` catches how hard the photosystems are working to dump excess light energy."

**Page 6**

- **Panel 1 (Day 74 Harvest, October 15, 2024):** Pots tip onto stainless sieves. Shoots, roots, and daughter tubers are rinsed free of `LMS-1E` grit and weighed fresh (`shoot:root` ratio and `harvest index = 100 × tuber / [tuber + shoot]`).

- **Panel 2 (Freeze-dryer):** Leaves and tubers flash-freeze in liquid nitrogen and enter a `Virtis Benchtop 4.0K XL` lyophilizer for `48` hours, while post-growth `LMS-1E` (`pH` down to `5.18–6.39`) goes to `Anton Paar` microwave `HNO₃` digestion and `Agilent 5110` ICP-OES.

**Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread Anchor: *Five Faces of the Moon*)**

- **Left Page (Page 7) — *Experiment 2: The Five-Simulant Gauntlet (May 5, 2025)*:** Five lunar regolith simulants line the greenhouse bench alongside `Adkins` soil, each softened with **`5% (v/v)` vermicompost** (`n = 30`): dark basaltic **`JSC-1A`** and **`LMS-1E`** (mare), and pale feldspathic **`LHS-1E`**, **`NUW-LHT-5M`**, and **`OPRH4W30`** (highland).

- **Right Page (Page 8) — *The `35`-Day `PlantEye` Time-Lapse (`70` Scans/Plant)*:** By Day `8`, shoots pierce `Adkins`, `JSC-1A`, `LMS-1E`, `LHS-1E`, and `OPRH4W30`—yet **`NUW-LHT-5M`** remains barren grey dust until Day `24`, finally pushing up a dwarfed, struggling sprout while `Adkins` towers beside it.

- **Spread Caption:** *Mare basalt and highland anorthosite are not the same Moon. Put a potato in five lunar simulants, and one of them barely lets the shoot breathe.*

**Page 9**

- **Panel 1 (Day 39 Sampling, Exp. 2):** Medora Knudson snips upper-canopy leaves—before senescence sets in—and plunges them straight into liquid nitrogen for **RNA-seq** and **GC-MS** metabolomics.

- **Panel 2 (Empty tube):** At the `NUW-LHT-5M` pots, her forceps pause. The plants are so severely stunted there isn't enough leaf tissue to sample without destroying the experiment.

- **David Handy:** "`NUW-LHT-5M` skips `39`-day leaf RNA-seq altogether. We'll see what's left at tuber harvest."

**Page 10**

- **Panel 1 (Day 59 Harvest, July 3, 2025):** Final tuber weights are logged across the five simulants and `Adkins` control, and post-growth substrates from four replicates per treatment head to `Mehlich-3` extraction (`20 mL` per `2 g`, `5 min` shake) and `Agilent 5110` ICP-OES (`Al, Ca, Cu, Fe, K, Mg, Mn, P, Zn`).

- **Panel 2 (Alert flag on ICP-OES):** One micronutrient bar spikes sharply above the `Adkins` baseline: **copper (`Cu`)**.

- **Aymeric Goyer:** "That's the suspect. Bio-available copper leaching out of crushed lunar rock."

### Act II: Chloroplast Stress & The Fructose Shift (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11**

- **Panel 1 (Molecular bench):** Frozen `39 DAP` leaves from `Adkins`, `JSC-1A`, `LMS-1E`, `LHS-1E`, and `OPRH4W30` undergo `Purelink` RNA extraction and `Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100` QC.

- **Panel 2 (Sequencing cluster):** Libraries stream off an **`Illumina NovaSeq X Plus`** (`150 bp` paired-end, `20.8–28.8 million` reads per sample), checked with `fastq_utils v0.25.1`, `FastQC v0.11.9`, and `MultiQC v1.12`.

**Page 12**

- **Panel 1 (Transcriptome volcano/pathway view):** Differential expression lights up two dominant hubs in LRS-grown `'Modoc'` leaves compared with `Adkins` soil: **stress-response genes** and **chloroplast-localized transcripts**.

- **Panel 2 (Cellular cross-section):** Inside a mesophyll cell, excess `Cu` and alkaline/mineral stress press against the thylakoid membrane—triggering chloroplast redox remodeling and `NPQ` photoprotection.

- **Aymeric Goyer:** "Even when a potato survives in lunar regolith simulant, its chloroplasts are rewriting their playbook to cope."

**Page 13**

- **Panel 1 (Metabolomics prep):** `50 mg` of lyophilized leaf and tuber powder drops into `water:methanol:chloroform (1:2.5:1)` spiked with **`40 µg/mL` ribitol** as an internal standard.

- **Panel 2 (Derivatization):** Dried aqueous extracts react with `20 µL` methoxyamine hydrochloride (`90 min`, `37°C`) and `40 µL` `MSTFA + 1% TMCS` (`30 min`, `37°C`), then inject (`1 µL`, `10:1` leaf / `4:1` tuber split) into an **`Agilent 7890B-5977B` GC-MS**.

**Page 14**

- **Panel 1 (Chromatogram peaks):** Spectra match against the `Agilent Fiehn 2013` RTL library in `AMDIS` and normalize to `ribitol` in `MetaboAnalyst 6.0`.

- **Panel 2 (Highlighted peak):** **Fructose**—alongside other central carbon and stress metabolites—accumulates differentially between LRS-grown plants and `Adkins` controls.

- **David Handy:** "Smaller canopies, stressed chloroplasts, and altered sugar partitioning straight into the tubers."

**Page 15**

- **Panel 1 (Comparison panel — Exp. 1 vs. Exp. 2):** Side-by-side summary of the two experiments:

  - **Compost rescue (Exp. 1):** Blending `15%` or `30%` vermicompost into `LMS-1E` buffers `pH` (`7.9` → `7.06` pre-growth), boosts `CO₂` respiration (`2` → `25 µg/g/day`), and supports canopy & tuber set.

  - **Simulant divergence (Exp. 2):** With only `5%` vermicompost, all LRSs trail `Adkins` soil in plant size—and highland `NUW-LHT-5M` stalls almost completely.

**Page 16**

- **Panel 1 (Detail, `Mehlich-3` vs. `HNO₃`):** An analytical note reminds the team: Experiment 1's microwave `HNO₃` digestion measures *total* rock metals (`Al, Cr, Cu, Fe, Ni, Zn`), while Experiment 2's `Mehlich-3` measures the *bio-extractable* fraction roots actually taste.

- **Panel 2 (Two-shot):** Aymeric Goyer and David Handy review the `OS-927` (`OSD-750`) dataset—`56.54 GB` of `PlantEye` point-cloud stats, `MultispeQ` fluorescence, `GC-MS` peak tables, and `NovaSeq X Plus` RNA-seq.

**Page 17**

- **Panel 1 (Curation terminal):** The ALSDA Analysis Working Group aligns `OSD-750` (`GLDS-667` / `LSDS-165`, `DOI: 10.26030/w56j-2d69`) into standardized machine-readable CSVs (`Data1`–`Data9`, `TableS5`–`TableS6`) for public release on `18-Nov-2026`.

- **Panel 2 (Caption):** *The verdict for lunar greenhouses: raw regolith is not soil. Without organic amendment (`15–30%` compost) and micronutrient mitigation (`Cu`), even a hardy tuber like `'Modoc'` pays a steep physiological tax.*

**Page 18**

- **Panel 1 (Quiet greenhouse evening):** The `PlantEye F600` gantry parks at the end of its rail. Empty `NUW-LHT-5M` and `LMS-1E` pots sit beside harvested `'Modoc'` tubers.

- **Aymeric Goyer:** "We've tested leafy greens in Veggie. We've tested tubers in Moon dust. What about the one crop astronauts crave most—something with **heat**, **fruit**, and **color**?"

### Act III: From Lunar Dust to the 137-Day Pepper (Pages 19–30)

**Page 19**

- **Panel 1 (Flashback / Orbit transition):** High above Earth, inside the International Space Station's **Advanced Plant Habitat (`APH`)**, `48` seeds sit glued with guar in four independently controlled ceramic-substrate quadrants (`1–2 mm` autoclaved arcillite + `Nutricote`).

- **Caption (Narrator):** *June 3, 2021. `SpaceX CRS-22` carries `PH-04` to orbit—not a `28`-day salad, not a `74`-day greenhouse tuber, but a `137`-day fruiting marathon.*

**Page 20**

- **Panel 1 (Close-up):** A glowing red-and-green **`Capsicum annuum` cv. `'NuMex Española Improved'`** chile pepper hangs under the `APH` LED canopy—one of `26` pepper fruits harvested in microgravity.

- **Panel 2 (Microbiology link):** Alongside fruit yield and crew taste tests, swab and plant samples enter `16S` `V4` amplicon sequencing on `Illumina MiSeq` (`2 × 250 bp`) using the exact `Khodadad et al., 2020` barcoded primer protocol we saw in `OSD-745`.

**Page 21**

- **Panel 1 (Contrast panel):** Back in Oregon, David Handy packs the last `'Modoc'` tuber sample into `-80°C` storage, glancing at the ISS `APH` telemetry feed.

- **David Handy:** "On the Moon, we're fighting `Cu` and dead rock. In orbit, `APH` had to keep a flowering, fruiting pepper alive for four and a half months."

**Page 22**

- **Panel 1 (Split graphic):**

  - Right: *Capsicum annuum* `'NuMex Española Improved'` (`OSD-772`, `137` days, `PH-04` / `SpaceX-22`, `26` fruits, `n = 109` samples).

**Page 23**

- **Panel 1 (Soil-health archive):** `OSD-750` locks its `173` sample records into NASA OSDR (`18-Nov-2026`), giving lunar ISRU agricultural engineers the first multi-omic potato baseline across both mare and highland simulants.

**Page 24**

- **Panel 1 (Close-up, `LMS-1E` vs. `Adkins`):** A single `'Modoc'` mini-tuber rests on a clean scale—small, scarred by silicate grit, yet carrying the starch blueprint for future lunar habitats.

**Page 25**

- **Panel 1 (Transition):** The greenhouse glass darkens; the scene dissolves into the sterile stainless interior of the ISS `APH` growth chamber (`137` days on the mission clock).

**Page 26**

- **Panel 1 (Botanical close-up):** White *Capsicum* blossoms open in microgravity—requiring pollination without wind or bees—before swelling into glossy green and crimson chile pods.

**Page 27**

- **Panel 1 (Hardware schematic):** Four `990 g` ceramic-substrate quadrants inside the `APH` Science Carrier, each feeding `12` pepper seeds (`48` total) through `137` continuous days of closed-loop orbital environmental control.

**Page 28**

- **Panel 1 (Crew & Lab link):** `26` pepper fruits harvested in space—part eaten fresh by the ISS crew in orbital tacos, part swabbed and sequenced (`16S` `MiSeq` `2 × 250 bp`) to verify food safety after the longest continuous plant experiment ever run on the station.
