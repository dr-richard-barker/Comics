## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **MM2D ("The Synchronized Squad"):** *Arabidopsis* Landsberg *erecta* suspension cells—stripped of roots, shoots, and statoliths, yet still feeling gravity's pull while locked inside `1%` low-melting agarose.

- **Aphidicolin ("The Starting Pistol"):** The `G1/S` DNA-replication blocker that halts every `MM2D` cell at the exact `T0` starting line before releasing them in lockstep.

- **The Staggered Clocks (`T7/T10` & `T14/T16`):** Twin timekeepers. Under `RPM` simulated microgravity, `MM2D` sprints into mitosis—forcing researchers to harvest `RPM` `G2/M` at `7 h` (`1 g` at `10 h`) and `RPM` `G1` at `14 h` (`1 g` at `16 h`) just to catch the cells in the same phase.

- **The Nucleolus ("The Ribosome Forge"):** The nuclear factory where ribosome biogenesis slows down under simulated microgravity—especially at `G2/M`—even as the cell cycle accelerates.

- **Chromatin Sentinel:** The epigenetic gatekeeper packing DNA tighter in `RPM`—raising DNA methylation, dropping histone acetylation, and driving overall transcriptional inhibition.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: Launch & Environmental Shift (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1 — Splash: Locked in Agarose**

- *Panel 1 (Full-page splash):* Inside a darkened `27°C` incubator, `250 mL` Pyrex flasks swirl at `120 rpm` with golden *Arabidopsis* `MM2D` cell clusters. Cut to a `1%` low-melting agarose slab mounted onto the twin-frame gimbal of an Airbus `Random Positioning Machine` (`RPM`).

- *Caption:* "Whole seedlings mix dividing meristems with mature leaves. To catch microgravity red-handed inside the cell cycle, you have to freeze the whole army at `T0`."

**Page 2 — The `G1/S` Starting Line (`T0`)**

- *Panel 1:* Seven-day-old `MM2D` cells (`1:20` subculture in `MSS` medium: `3%` sucrose, `0.5 mg/L` NAA, `0.05 mg/L` kinetin, `pH 5.8`) hit **aphidicolin**.

- *Panel 2:* Every nucleus jams at the `G1/S` gate. Washout! `T0` begins.

- *MM2D Cell:* "Everyone on the line! Next stop: `S` phase, `G2/M`, and mitosis!"

**Page 3 — Spaceflight-Ready Gel**

- *Panel 1:* Warm `1%` low-melting agarose pours over the synchronized `T0` cells—immobilizing them in a semisolid matrix designed for real orbital hardware.

- *Panel 2:* Half the plates stay flat at `1 g` on Earth; half mount onto the `RPM` inner frame, tumbling continuously in three dimensions.

**Page 4 — The Race Begins: Gravity vs. `RPM`**

- *Panel 1:* Split-panel timer at `Hour 5`. On the `1 g` bench, `MM2D` nuclei march steadily through `S` phase.

- *Panel 2:* On the spinning `RPM`, the cell cycle **accelerates**—rushing prematurely toward `M` phase while ribosome biogenesis lags behind!

- *The Nucleolus:* "Slow down! We haven't finished building ribosomes for daughter cells!"

- *MM2D Cell:* "Can't! The `G2/M` checkpoint just slipped!"

**Page 5 — Staggering the Harvest (`T7/T10`)**

- *Panel 1:* Flow cytometry histograms flash on a monitor. To catch `> 2/3` of cells in `G2/M`, `RPM` must be harvested at **`7 h`**, while `1 g` control waits until **`10 h`**.

- *Caption:* "If you harvest both at `10 h`, you aren't comparing `G2/M` to `G2/M`—you're comparing two different phases."

**Page 6 — Second Wave (`T14/T16` & Asynchronous `14 h`)**

- *Panel 1:* The clock ticks on. After mitosis, `RPM` hits `G1` enrichment at **`14 h`**, while `1 g` reaches `G1` at **`16 h`**.

- *Panel 2:* Beside them, an **asynchronous** `MM2D` batch runs `14 h` `RPM` vs. `14 h` `1 g` as an un-synchronized reference.

**Page 7 — Liquid Nitrogen Snap**

- *Panel 1:* At `7 h`, `10 h`, `14 h`, and `16 h`, `0.5 g` cell pellets drop into liquid nitrogen (`-196°C`), locking every `Cy3`/`Cy5` target in place.

**Page 8–10 — The `NimbleGen` Dye-Swap Matrix**

- *Page 8:* Four `µg` of `DNase`-treated RNA feeds `SuperScript II RT` with `Cy3-dCTP` and `Cy5-dCTP`, purified through `QIAquick`.

- *Page 9:* Twelve `NimbleGen` `A-GEOD-17404` arrays (`557245A01`–`A12`) run as **four dye-swapped hybridizations** per comparison (`Asyn`, `G1`, `G2/M`) to cancel dye bias.

- *Page 10:* `Mapix` lasers (`532 nm` & `635 nm`) scan the slides; `loess` normalization flattens the log₂ ratios. *Caption:* "Twenty-four samples. Three cell-cycle windows. Now let's see which phase takes the hardest hit."

### Act II: The Molecular Battle (Pages 11–22)

**Page 11 — Global Silence**

- *Panel 1:* Across `Asyn`, `G1`, and `G2/M`, the dominant color on the `NimbleGen` heatmap is **repression**.

- *Chromatin Sentinel:* "Simulated microgravity doesn't just shout—it mutes the nucleus."

**Page 12 — The `G2/M` Peak**

- *Panel 1:* Comparing the three windows side-by-side (`30641127`), differential expression **peaks sharply at `G2/M`** (`T7`/`T10`), eclipsing `G1` (`T14`/`T16`) and the blurred `Asynchronous` average.

- *Caption:* "In an asynchronous culture, `G2/M`'s alarm gets diluted by `G1` and `S`. Synchronization pulls off the mask."

**Page 13 — Checkpoints Under Fire (`G2/M` & `G1/S`)**

- *Panel 1:* `qPCR` and flow cytometry (`30105864`) zoom in on core cell-cycle regulators: both the `G2/M` and `G1/S` checkpoints are misregulated, letting `RPM` cells race through division without normal growth coordination.

**Page 14 — Chromatin Locks Down**

- *Panel 1:* Inside the `MM2D` nucleus, **DNA methylation climbs** while **histone acetylation drops**.

- *Panel 2:* Euchromatin coils into dense heterochromatin blocks—physically choking global nuclear transcription.

**Page 15 — The Nucleolar Slowdown**

- *Panel 1:* Immunofluorescence microscopy shows shrunken, less active nucleoli under `RPM`—especially in **`G2/M`**—as nucleolar proteins and ribosome-biogenesis transcripts fall.

- *The Nucleolus:* "Cell division is speeding up, but protein-factory assembly is slowing down. That's how proliferation and cell growth decouple!"

**Page 16–18 — Shifting Stress Armies (`G2/M` vs. `G1`)**

- *Page 16:* Three GO pillars light up across the arrays: **cell proliferation**, **energy/redox**, and **stress responses**—alongside a crowd of **unknown biological process** genes.

- *Page 17:* Watch the stress-response squad between `G2/M` (`7 h`) and `G1` (`14 h`): the roster flips dramatically! What protects a cell preparing for mitosis is completely rewired once daughter cells enter `G1`.

- *Page 18:* Meanwhile, the `Asynchronous` (`14 h`) culture shows a blunted, averaged signature—missing the phase-specific handoff.

**Page 19–22 — Undifferentiated Cells Still Feel `g`**

- *Page 19:* A recurring question in space biology: *how can an undifferentiated `MM2D` cell with no statoliths sense microgravity?*

- *Page 20:* Through plasma-membrane tension, cytoskeletal/nuclear mechanics, and chromatin-remodeling cascades that alter `G1/S` and `G2/M` timing directly.

- *Page 21–22:* Comparing `OSD-144` (`RPM`) with future spaceflight callus studies (`BRIC-16-DNA`, `BRIC-17`), the Madrid/ESA team concludes that counteracting nucleolar and chromatin stress is essential before space crops can grow reliably from meristems.

## 4. Mission Debrief (Pages 31–32 Infographic Layout)

**Page 31 (Left Spread): Synchronization & `RPM` Design (`n = 24` Samples)**

- **Cell Line & Matrix:** *Arabidopsis* (`Ler`) `MM2D` suspension cells (`MSS` medium, `27°C`, dark) → **aphidicolin** `G1/S` block/release (`T0`) → immobilized in `1%` low-melting agarose → `RPM` vs. `1 g` Earth control.

- **Phase-Matched Harvest Schedule (`> 2/3` phase enrichment):**

  - `G2/M` synchronized: **`7 h` `RPM`** vs. **`10 h` `1 g`** (`4` dye-swap arrays, `GSM1480720–723`)

  - `G1` synchronized: **`14 h` `RPM`** vs. **`16 h` `1 g`** (`4` dye-swap arrays, `GSM1480716–719`)

  - `Asynchronous` reference: **`14 h` `RPM`** vs. **`14 h` `1 g`** (`4` dye-swap arrays, `GSM1480712–715`)

- **Platform:** `NimbleGen` `A-GEOD-17404` (`Cy3`/`Cy5` dye-swap, `loess` normalization).

**Page 32 (Right Spread): Nuclear & Transcriptomic Impact (`Kamal et al., 2019`)**

- **Cell-Cycle Acceleration:** `RPM` induces **quick entry into M phase**, shortening the cell cycle and decoupling proliferation from growth (`G2/M` & `G1/S` checkpoint misregulation).

- **Phase Peak:** Overall transcriptional inhibition across all samples, with differential expression **peaking at `G2/M`** and stress-response profiles shifting sharply from `G2/M` to `G1`.

- **Epigenetics & Nucleolus (`PMID: 30105864`):** Higher DNA methylation, lower histone acetylation, increased chromatin condensation, depleted nuclear transcription, and **reduced ribosome biogenesis / nucleolar activity** (strongest at `G2/M`).
