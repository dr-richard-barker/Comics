## 1. Volume Metadata Header

- **Series:** *Roots in Orbit: The NASA OSDR Plant Chronicles*

- **Volume:** 49

- **Chapter Title:** *Iron in the Mojave Dust*

- **OSDR Accession:** `OSD-581` (`GLDS-574` | ProteomeXchange: `PXD035041` | DOI: `10.26030/j6nv-8z71`)

- **Primary Publication:** Berni, R., Leclercq, C. C., Roux, P., Hausman, J.-F., Renaut, J., & Guerriero, G. (2023). *A molecular study of Italian ryegrass grown on Martian regolith simulant*. *Science of The Total Environment*, `854:158774`. (PMID: `36108852` | DOI: `10.1016/j.scitotenv.2022.158774`)

- **Chronology Note:** Continues after Volume 48 (`OSD-565`). Non-plant accessions `OSD-566` through `OSD-580` (Inspiration4 biomedical missions, rodent and microbial payloads) are recorded in `skipped_accessions`.

- **Setting & Hardware:** Fitotron climatic chamber (`16 h` light at `25°C` / `8 h` dark at `20°C`, `60%` RH, `8,600 lux`). Growth rigs: `50 mL` foil-wrapped skirted Falcon tubes perforated at the bottom, lined with Miracloth, sitting in `60 × 15 mm` Petri dish irrigation wells. Substrates: Potting soil (`PGS`: peat, sand, perlite with NPK) vs. Coarse Martian Regolith Simulant `MMS-1` (`PGM`: crushed basaltic ash from the Mojave Desert).

- **Organism & Assay Scope:** *Lolium multiflorum* Lam. (Italian ryegrass, Poaceae). Assays: ICP-OES leachate geochemistry (`10` elements), optical histochemistry (FASGA & Perls Prussian blue), targeted RT-qPCR of `18` stress and metabolic genes at `7d`, `14d`, and `7d` post-cut regrowth, and label-free LC-MS/MS shotgun proteomics (`NanoLC-425` + `TripleTOF 6600+`).

## 2. Dramatis Personae (Grounded in Study Data)

- **Squad Lolium (`PGM` vs. `PGS`):** *Lolium multiflorum* (Italian ryegrass) battalions. The terrestrial soil cohort (`PGS`) flourishes with vibrant green blades and delicate fibrous root nets. The Martian pioneer squad (`PGM`) battles harsh basaltic grit, displaying leaf epinasty (downward curling), stunted elongation, chlorosis, and thickened cuticular shields.

- **The Iron Sentinel (`YSL13` & `Perls-Positive Lacunae`):** Heavy metal and nicotianamine gatekeeper. Down in the regolith roots, `YSL13` rallies alongside aerenchyma engineers to compartmentalize excessive insoluble iron deposits revealed by Prussian blue staining.

- **The Lacuna Architect (Cortical Aerenchyma):** Specialized cell wall remodeling program triggered in `PGM` roots. By hollowing out cortical cells into gas-filled lacunae, it prevents asphyxiation and creates metabolic shelters within compacted simulant.

- **Commander `SuSy1` & `ASNS1` (Carbon-Nitrogen Mobilizers):** Sucrose synthase and asparagine synthetase officers intensely upregulated in `PGM` leaves to reroute sugars and amino reserves as photosynthetic efficiency drops.

- **The Phenylpropanoid Shield (`PAL1-5` & `CAD1`):** Phenylalanine ammonia-lyase and cinnamyl alcohol dehydrogenase vanguard reinforcing cell walls with lignin and polyphenols against abrasive basalt grains and oxidative stress.

- **C1-Methylase Vanguard (Cluster 5 Epigenetic Guard):** S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) cycle and histone/DNA methylation enzymes enriched in `PGM` roots (`FC ≥ 2`), locking chromatin against geochemical toxicity.

## 3. 32-Page Chapter Script

### Act I: The Mojave Basalt Pit (Pages 1–10)

**Page 1**

- **Panel 1 (Wide angle):** Red dust winds swirling over the Mojave Desert cinder cones. A sterile robotic scoop excavates dense, crushed basaltic ash—`MMS-1 Martian Regolith Simulant`.

- **Caption:** *Mars is not soil. It is crushed volcanic fire, devoid of organics, saturated with iron, silicates, and sharp basalt crystals.*

- **Panel 2:** Laboratory bench inside the Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology. Two rows of `50 mL` skirted falcon tubes wrapped in reflective aluminum foil. One row filled with black rich terrestrial potting soil (`PGS`); the other packed with reddish-brown coarse `MMS-1` (`PGM`).

**Page 2**

- **Panel 1:** Close-up of the drainage modification. Three sterile punctures drilled in each Falcon tube base, lined with fibrous Miracloth wicks resting in `60 × 15 mm` Petri dish reservoirs.

- **Panel 2:** Deionized water (`25 mL`) pipetted into the dishes every three days. Water ascends through the Miracloth via capillary action, encountering the tight, hydrophobic compaction of `MMS-1`.

**Page 3**

- **Panel 1:** Day 3. Tiny emerald coleoptiles of *Lolium multiflorum* breach the surface. In `PGS`, roots fan out instantly into the soft organic loam.

- **Panel 2:** In `PGM`, primary radicles strike abrasive basalt grains. Microscopic view: delicate root cap cells grind against angular mineral facets.

- **SFX:** *GRAAAT — ABRASION.*

**Page 4**

- **Panel 1:** Day 7 harvest. `PGS` shoots stand tall, bright green, and linear. `PGM` shoots appear noticeably shorter and darker, with leaf tips beginning to curl downward.

- **Panel 2 (Data HUD Overlay):** ICP-OES analysis of leachate water. `P`, `K`, and `Mn` levels in `PGS` leachate register high, whereas `MMS-1` leachate is starved of soluble phosphorus and potassium.

- **Caption:** *The simulant locks away nutrients like a vault. What it yields instead is mineral stress.*

**Page 5**

- **Panel 1:** Day 14. Severe physiological divergence. `PGM` blades display striking chlorosis along the leaf margins and pronounced leaf epinasty (downward helical rolling).

- **Panel 2:** The scissors descend. The blades are cleanly cut at `2 cm` above the substrate to test cutting stress and regrowth resilience.

**Page 6**

- **Panel 1:** Day 21 (7 days post-cut). `PGS` shoots surge upward in full regrowth canopy. `PGM` regrowth is sluggish, stiff, and heavily pigmented at the leaf sheaths.

- **Panel 2:** Roots lifted from tubes. While `PGS` roots form a dense white beard, `PGM` roots are short, thickened, brownish-red, and stubbornly clumped.

**Pages 7–8 (Double-Page Spread — "The Mineral Wall")**

- **Spread Landscape:** Split cross-section comparing root systems. On the left, terrestrial roots weave gracefully through airy soil pores. On the right, `PGM` roots are enveloped in dense basalt silt.

- **Data Inset Left:** Normal parenchymal cortex, intact vascular cylinders, standard lignin distribution.

- **Data Inset Right (FASGA staining):** Reddish-purple fluorescence reveals thick lignified hypodermal barriers and narrowed central-vein metaxylem vessels.

- **Caption:** *To survive Martian dust, the grass must rebuild its anatomy from the cuticle to the core.*

**Page 9**

- **Panel 1:** Perls Prussian Blue histochemical staining under the optical microscope.

- **Panel 2:** Extreme macro shot of `PGM` root cortex. Deep, intense azure blue precipitates flare across cortical cell walls and intercellular pockets.

- **Dialogue (Doc B / Narrator):** "Iron! Massive insoluble ferric deposits accumulating right in the apoplast! The roots are barricading heavy metals before they poison the stele!"

**Page 10**

- **Panel 1:** Discovery of cortical aerenchyma. Cross-section reveals large, hollow lacunar cavities spanning the middle cortex of `PGM` roots.

- **Caption:** *Lysigenous aerenchyma—normally formed under flooding hypoxia—spontaneously opens inside Martian simulant to shed suffocated cells and store trapped oxygen.*

**Page 14**

- **Panel 1:** The Gatekeeper's struggle: `YSL13` (Yellow Stripe-Like 13 metal-nicotianamine transporter) works at peak capacity along plasma membranes.

- **Panel 2:** `YSL13` channels iron complexes into vacuolar storage vaults, keeping cytoplasmic free radicals in check.

**Page 15**

- **Panel 1:** Day 14 post-cut battle. The oxidative burst! Reactive oxygen species (`H₂O₂` and superoxide) flare within the leaf mesophyll.

- **Panel 2:** `APX2` (Ascorbate Peroxidase 2) and `CAT2-2` (Catalase 2) step into the breach, detoxifying peroxides as cellular shields glow under fluorescent screentone.

**Page 16**

**Page 17**

- **Panel 1:** `NanoLC-425` micro-chromatography column separating thousands of peptide fractions along a reverse-phase gradient.

- **Panel 2:** High-resolution mass spectrometry injection into the `TripleTOF 6600+`. Ion sprays illuminate the vacuum chamber with brilliant violet electric arcs.

**Page 18**

- **Panel 1:** Mass spectrometer flight path. Peptide fragmentation spectra matched in real-time against the UniProtKB *Pooideae* taxonomic database.

- **SFX:** *ZRRRT — SPECTRAL ACQUISITION.*

**Page 19**

- **Panel 1:** Volcano plots emerge on the analysis terminals. Proteomic shift in `PGM` leaves: dramatic upregulation of cysteine synthases and stress-responsive chaperones.

- **Panel 2:** Vesicular trafficking proteins mobilize in `PGM` leaf mesophyll, accelerating vacuolar turnover and damaged organelle autophagy.

**Page 20**

- **Panel 1:** The Root Proteome Epigenetic Discovery: Cluster 5 proteins in `PGM` roots show a `> 2-fold` accumulation over Earth controls.

- **Panel 2:** Commander `C1-Methylase` stands in the root cell nucleus, orchestrating S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) cycle enzymes and histone methyltransferases.

### Left Spread (Page 31): Experimental Architecture & Leachate Geochemistry

| Parameter | Study Specification (`OSD-581`) |
|---|---|
| **Organism** | *Lolium multiflorum* Lam. (Italian ryegrass, Poaceae) |
| **Substrates (`n = 18` tubes)** | `9` Potting soil (`PGS`: `1/3` sand, `2/3` peat, perlite, NPK `14-16-18 + 2MgO`) vs. `9` coarse `MMS-1` (`PGM`) |
| **Growth Rig & Chamber** | `50 mL` foil-wrapped skirted tubes with Miracloth in `60 × 15 mm` Petri plates; `Fitotron` (`16 h` `25°C` / `8 h` `20°C`, `60%` RH, `8,600 lux`) |
| **Irrigation & Sampling** | `25 mL` deionized water every `3 days`; leaves at `7d`, `14d`, and `7d` post-cut regrowth; `3` biological replicates (`3` plants each) |
| **Leachate (`ICP-OES`)** | `P`, `Mn`, `K` higher in `PGS` (`p < 0.05`); soluble `Fe` detected only in unplanted `MMS-1` controls; plant growth shifted `MMS-1` `pH` |

|---|---|

| **qPCR Clusters (`C1`–`C3`)** | `C1` high in `PGM` (`SuSy1/6`, `ASNS1`, `PAL1-5`, `YSL13`, `CAD1`, `APX2`, `CAT2-2`); `C2` low in `PGM` (`PHR1`, `Fd-GOGAT`, `YSL14`, `HCT1-3`); `C3` diverged at `14d` (`MgCHLI1`, `FeSOD1/2`) |
| **Proteomics Platform** | `n = 12` (`3` Mars/Soil Leaves & Roots); `50 μg` protein (`PreOmics iST` + `BeatBox`), `NanoLC-425` / `TripleTOF 6600+`, UniProtKB `Pooideae` (`95%` conf., `p < 0.05`) |
| **Proteome Reprogramming** | `PGM` roots enriched `4` Cluster 5 proteins (`FC ≥ 2`) in **C1 metabolism (methylation)**, **DNA binding**, and **histone methylation**; `PGM` leaves shifted to stress, cysteine synthesis, and vesicle transport |
