## Volume 01 — BREATHLESS IN ZERO-G: The Invisible Pillow

*When gravity vanishes, air stops rising—and space leaves suffocate in their own oxygen.*

- **Art Style (Marvel):** Modern Marvel Sci-Fi Action — Bold inked linework, dramatic chiaroscuro shadows, heroic foreshortening, and glowing cyan-vs-amber translucent CFD streamlines wrapping spaceflight leaves.
- **Worlds:** ISS, Earth · **POV:** Phytonaut, Mission Control · **NGSS:** `MS-PS2-4 · MS-LS1-6 · HS-LS1-5 · HS-LS1-7`
- **Take-Home Message:** “When you can't see the bottleneck, simulate the invisible! A gentle, well-aimed breeze is the difference between a suffocating leaf and a thriving orbital garden.”
- **Hands-On Mission Activity:** Launch LunarLeaf-CFD and Spaceflight Plant Hardware CFD, drag gravity from Earth (1g) to the ISS (0g), and calculate the minimum fan speed needed to sweep away the stagnant oxygen blanket.

### Key Research Breakthroughs Synthesized

- In 0g microgravity, buoyant natural convection collapses (Gr, Ra → 0), thickening the stagnant leaf boundary layer more than 2.5×.
- Trapped oxygen around space leaves forces Rubisco to grab O₂ instead of CO₂, flipping photosynthesis into energy-wasting photorespiration across 6 NASA flight contrasts.
- Chamber ventilation physics—not mysterious 'zero-G mutations'—explains decades of hypoxia and carbon-starvation gene signatures in NASA OSDR.
- Auditing 5 flight chambers (VEGGIE, APH, PGU, CARA, BRIC) via OpenFOAM CFD and the MI-CFD-HW standard reveals how angled cross-flow louvers rescue space crops.
